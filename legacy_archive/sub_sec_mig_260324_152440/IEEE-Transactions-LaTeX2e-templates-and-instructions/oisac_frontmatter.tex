\begin{abstract}
Optical integrated sensing and communication is emerging as a promising sixth-generation network direction because optical carriers can jointly support very high data rates and high-resolution sensing across fiber, free-space, visible-light, and photonics-assisted terahertz settings. This systematic review synthesizes 220 peer-reviewed studies and establishes a cross-modality comparison framework that keeps medium, detection regime, and measurement plane explicit. The review develops a unified taxonomy, separates physical resolution from estimator accuracy and bound-based metrics, maps enabling technologies and application transfer paths, and consolidates the field into five challenge domains. The resulting evidence-based review clarifies where cross-paper comparison is defensible, where deployment evidence is concentrated, and which bottlenecks most strongly shape credible optical sensing-communication systems for future networks.
\end{abstract}
