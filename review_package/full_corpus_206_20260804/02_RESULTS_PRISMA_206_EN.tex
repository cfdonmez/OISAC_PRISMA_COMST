\subsection{Study Selection and PRISMA Flow}
\label{subsec:prisma_flow_results}

The final searches identified 1,733 records: 1,273 from Scopus, 329 from IEEE
Xplore, 24 from ScienceDirect, 75 from SpringerLink, 29 from Wiley Online
Library, and 3 from Taylor \& Francis Online. No record was identified through
a register or an additional-methods branch. Before screening, 472 duplicate
records and 2 records with other metadata or platform dispositions were
removed; no record was removed autonomously by an automation tool. The
remaining 1,259 records underwent title-and-abstract screening.

Of the 1,259 screened records, 927 did not advance to retrieval. This group
comprised 864 title-and-abstract exclusions, 61 records retained only for the
contextual corpus, and 2 duplicate or related-version flags. The remaining 332
source records formed the historical retrieval queue.

The formal PRISMA retrieval and eligibility boxes use unique reports rather
than historical source records. Two post-screening bibliographic aliases were
consolidated, reducing the formal retrieval denominator from 332 source
records to 330 unique reports. Of these, 58 reports were not retrieved and 272
reports were assessed for full-text eligibility.

Full-text assessment retained 227 reports in the primary technical corpus,
excluded 39 reports, and retained 6 reports only for contextual use. The 39
full-text exclusions comprised 4 non-optical or RF-only ISAC reports, 2
communication-only reports, 12 sensing-only reports, 5 reports without genuine
sensing--communication integration, 4 abstract/poster/editorial/opinion or
other non-full-paper reports, and 12 reports with insufficient English full
technical content. The six contextual-only reports were not counted as
full-text exclusions.

Report-to-study mapping subsequently consolidated the 227 eligible reports
into 206 included studies, a reduction of 21 reports attributable to multiple
reports or versions of the same underlying study. The final contextual corpus
contained 67 items: 61 retained at title-and-abstract screening and 6 retained
after full-text assessment. Contextual items remained outside the primary
technical study count.

The reconciled identities were:

\begin{align}
1{,}733 - 472 - 0 - 2 &= 1{,}259, \\
1{,}259 - 927 &= 332, \\
332 - 2 &= 330, \\
330 - 58 &= 272, \\
272 - 39 - 6 &= 227, \\
227\ \text{reports} &\longrightarrow 206\ \text{studies}.
\end{align}
