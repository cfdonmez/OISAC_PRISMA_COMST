# Abstract & Conclusion Templates (The "Bookends")

## Part 1: The Abstract (The "Storefront")

### A. Methodology & Analysis match
Analyzed 20 COMST Abstracts (COMST_001 - COMST_020).
*   **Average Word Count:** ~200-250 words (Safe target: **250 words**).
*   **Structure:** Rigid 4-part flow found in 90% of papers.
*   **Key Marker:** The word "Finally" starts the last sentence in almost every sample.

### B. The "Golden Abstract Formula" (4 Blocks)

1.  **Block 1: The Hook (Context) [1-2 sentences]**
    *   *Goal:* Connect to a major trend (6G, IoT, AI).
    *   *Pattern:* "With the rapid development of [Trend]..." or "[Tech] is emerging as a key enabler..."
2.  **Block 2: The Gap (Problem) [1-2 sentences]**
    *   *Goal:* Identifying the missing link.
    *   *Pattern:* "**However**, existing solutions suffer from [Limitation]..." or "Despite these advances, [Problem] remains addressed..."
3.  **Block 3: The Solution (Contribution) [2-3 sentences]**
    *   *Goal:* What this paper does.
    *   *Pattern:* "In this paper, we present a comprehensive survey...", "We propose a novel taxonomy...", "Specifically, we..."
4.  **Block 4: The Roadmap (Future) [1 sentence]**
    *   *Goal:* The exit strategy.
    *   *Pattern:* "**Finally**, we outline open challenges and future directions..."

### C. Micro-Templates for O-ISAC Abstract

#### Option 1: The "Convergence" Narrative (Preferred)
> **[Context]** As 6G networks evolve towards ubiquitous intelligence, Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) has emerged as a critical enabler. While Radio Frequency (RF) based ISAC has been extensively studied, the optical spectrum offers superior bandwidth and immunity to interference.
> **[Gap]** **However**, the Optical ISAC (O-ISAC) landscape remains fragmented, with disjoint developments in fiber sensing, Visible Light Positioning (VLP), and Free-Space Optical (FSO) ranging.
> **[Solution]** To bridge this gap, this paper presents the **first comprehensive survey** on physical-layer O-ISAC technologies. We propose a unified taxonomy that categorizes system architectures into cabled (fiber) and wireless (FSO/VLC) domains. Furthermore, we conduct a systematic review following the **PRISMA** guidelines to analyze 221 recent studies.
> **[Result]** **Finally**, we identify fundamental performance trade-offs, discuss enabling technologies like Optical RIS, and outline future research directions for achieving seamless optical convergence in 6G.

#### Option 2: The "Problem-Solver" Narrative
> **[Context]** The exponential growth of data traffic and the need for high-precision sensing are driving the convergence of optical networks.
> **[Gap]** **However**, current optical sensing and communication systems are designed in isolation, leading to inefficient spectrum and hardware utilization. Existing surveys fail to provide a holistic view of how these domains can be integrated.
> **[Solution]** In this paper, we address this limitation by providing a tutorial-style overview of O-ISAC fundamentals. We analyze integration strategies across three key domains: Fiber, DLC to VLC, and FSO.
> **[Result]** **Finally**, we highlight critical standardization gaps and propose a roadmap for future O-ISAC standardization.

## Part 2: The Conclusion (The "Receipt")

### A. Methodology & Analysis
Analyzed 20 COMST Conclusions.
*   **Average Word Count:** ~150-250 words (excluding "Future Work" section).
*   **Structure:** A mirror image of the Introduction.
*   **Key Function:** To provide a "receipt" that proves the promises made in the Introduction were delivered.

### B. The "Receipt" Formula (3 Blocks)

1.  **Block 1: The Restatement (The Goal) [1 sentence]**
    *   *Goal:* Remind the reader why we started. (Past Tense).
    *   *Pattern:* "In this survey, we have presented a comprehensive review of..." or "This paper has explored..."
2.  **Block 2: The Summary (The Delivery) [2-3 sentences]**
    *   *Goal:* Summarize the main pillars (Taxonomy, Performance, Challenges).
    *   *Pattern:* "Specifically, we first introduced... Then, we analyzed... Finally, we compared..."
    *   *Note:* This should match the "Organization" paragraph in the Intro.
3.  **Block 3: The Vision (The Exit) [1 sentence]**
    *   *Goal:* Final inspiring thought.
    *   *Pattern:* "We hope this survey serves as a guideline..." or "It is expected that this work will pave the way..."

### C. Micro-Templates for O-ISAC Conclusion

> **[The Content]**
> "In this article, we have presented the **first unified survey** on O-ISAC, bridging the gap between fiber sensing and optical wireless communication systems.
>
> **[The Delivery]**
> Specifically, we first established a fundamental understanding of optical channel models and hardware enablers. Then, we proposed a comprehensive **taxonomy** that categorizes O-ISAC into cabled and wireless domains, analyzing contributions from over 200 studies. Furthermore, we provided a **comparative analysis** of performance metrics, highlighting the trade-offs between sensing resolution and data rate.
>
> **[The Exit]**
> Hopefully, this survey will serve as a foundational roadmap for researchers aiming to unlock the full potential of the optical spectrum in the **6G** era, fostering a truly integrated sensing and communication ecosystem."
