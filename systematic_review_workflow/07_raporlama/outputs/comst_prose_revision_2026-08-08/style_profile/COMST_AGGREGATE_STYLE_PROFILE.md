## Interpretation Rules for the O-ISAC Rewrite

1. Use the corpus to calibrate pace and rhetorical order, never to copy wording.
2. A numeric outlier is a review prompt, not an automatic defect.
3. Scientific claims, corpus counts, and citation identity are governed by the
   frozen O-ISAC evidence base, independently of prose style.
4. Prefer one principal idea per paragraph and introduce specialized metric
   distinctions only after the reader understands why they matter.
5. Keep PRISMA and extraction mechanics subordinate to the survey's technical
   story outside the Methods and reporting sections.
