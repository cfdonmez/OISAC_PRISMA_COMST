\section{CONCLUSION}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Across 206 studies, O-ISAC integration was realized through shared hardware,
carriers, waveforms, resources, links, design rules, and application contexts.
The portability of these results depended less on the sharing label than on
continuity across modality, task, measurement plane, operating condition, and
validation setting. The corpus thus establishes a broad technical base while
locating its principal maturity gap in transferable joint evidence.

Modality aware measurement contracts, paired function validation under
realistic disturbances, and versioned configuration, calibration, data, and
processing artifacts provide the basis for closing that gap. Together, these
practices can convert isolated demonstrations into repeatable evidence for
cumulative engineering and network scale evaluation.
