# Search Records

The publication-facing search record comprises two files:

- `FINAL_SEARCH_EXECUTION_AND_STRATEGIES_FOR_REPORTING_2026-08-14.csv`
  gives one row for each of 19 executed searches across Scopus, IEEE Xplore,
  ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, Wiley Online Library, and Taylor & Francis
  Online;
- `FINAL_SEARCH_METHODS_AND_EXECUTION_FOR_REPORTING_2026-08-14.md` explains
  strategy provenance, the 1,733-record reconciliation, and the boundary
  between the reporting view and the historical audit files.

The exact query-to-export mapping for two low-yield Taylor & Francis exports
was not recorded at execution and could not be reconstructed. Their fields are
reported as `NR_query_to_export_mapping_unavailable`; no query was invented.
Interface on-screen counts were likewise not recorded and are reported as
`NR_not_recorded_at_execution` rather than as pending work.

Historical source summaries and QA notes remain in the internal audit archive
but are excluded from the peer-review upload because their draft-era status
language no longer describes the completed review. Raw database exports are
also excluded because redistribution rights were not established.
