# Evidence-to-Survey Workflow

## Authorities remain separate

- **Evidence authority:** locked study, claim, appraisal, and synthesis records
  determine facts, counts, and inferential limits.
- **Style authority:** the filtered 76-paper COMST corpus supplies descriptive
  architecture and reader-load guardrails. Its sentences are not copied.
- **Editorial authority:** the authors decide scientific emphasis and approve
  the complete reading version.

Fluency cannot override evidence. PRISMA mechanics cannot replace the survey's
technical contribution.

## Drafting sequence

1. Freeze the evidence and language manifest.
2. Resolve study identity and citation keys.
3. Assign one reader job to each main section.
4. Draft mechanism and meaning before dense counts.
5. Move repeated inventories to tables or figures.
6. Preserve conditions before relating results across studies.
7. Run static integrity, evidence-assertion, citation, redundancy, and prose
   checks after every substantive edit.
8. Conduct one complete author read in article order, then reopen only the
   sections affected by a requested revision.

The workflow does not require the author to approve every sentence while it is
being drafted. Questions are escalated only when local evidence cannot resolve
a material scientific choice.

## Mandatory QA layers

1. **Identity:** every citation key resolves to the intended source.
2. **Evidence:** claims remain within source and ledger support.
3. **Denominator:** reports, studies, claims, metrics, and tradeoff records are
   not interchanged.
4. **Comparison:** metric meaning, plane, task, conditions, baseline, and
   validation remain visible.
5. **Conduct:** retrospective registration and reviewer boundaries are stated
   accurately.
6. **Architecture and prose:** the manuscript follows the nine-section COMST
   reader journey without catalog-like repetition.
7. **Visual contract:** each table or figure has a unique reader task and a
   named evidence authority.
8. **Author review:** final acceptance applies to the integrated manuscript,
   not to isolated drafting fragments.

## Deferred gates

Figure production and the final figure-inclusive layout remain controlled
manuscript tasks. The 38-source contextual-synthesis matrix is an internal
audit artifact rather than a reader-facing ST-RS1 annex. Repository release,
DOI, and licensing remain separate external tasks.
