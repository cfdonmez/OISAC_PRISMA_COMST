## Purpose and boundary

This audit supports the related-synthesis subsection of the Introduction in the new 206-study O-ISAC survey. It does not use the unpublished 220/221-study manuscript as evidence authority. Contextual syntheses do not enter the 206-study primary technical denominator.

A source qualifies as a related synthesis when:

1. its central scope is joint or integrated optical communication and sensing;
2. its main function is to synthesize more than one prior system, solution class, or research line;
3. it is a review, survey, tutorial, overview, invited synthesis, or a full-text report whose dominant function is synthesis rather than a new independently evaluable primary contribution; and
4. it is not merely an editorial, project roadmap, neighboring photonics review that only mentions ISAC, or a new model, simulation, or experiment.

Illustrative author material does not by itself make a synthesis primary evidence when it lacks a standalone reproducible method, estimand, and independently evaluable result. This rule is used for the retroreflective O-ISAC preprint described below. The audit is a bounded manuscript-positioning search, not a claim that no other synthesis exists worldwide.

Publication form is recorded but is not treated as a quality score. Short conference overviews are retained separately because their reader task and evidential depth differ from full-length syntheses.
