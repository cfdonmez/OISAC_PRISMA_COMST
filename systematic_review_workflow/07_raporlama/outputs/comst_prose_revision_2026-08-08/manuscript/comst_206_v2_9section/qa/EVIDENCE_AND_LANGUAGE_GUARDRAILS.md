# Evidence and Language Guardrails

These rules govern every prose revision in this candidate. They protect the
survey narrative from becoming either a PRISMA report or an unsupported
technology ranking.

## Registration and Review Conduct

- The review was retrospectively registered on 12 February 2026: OSF `7f6wb`,
  DOI `10.17605/OSF.IO/7F6WB`.
- The registration records a predecessor 221-study state after searching and
  screening had already been completed. It is not a prospective
  preregistration.
- The manuscript must explain the later search refresh and re-baselining to the
  frozen 206-study corpus, together with material protocol deviations.
- One investigator supervised the executed workflow and made the final review
  decisions. Routine independent duplicate human screening, extraction, appraisal, and
  adjudication were not performed and must not be claimed.
- PRISMA 2020 governs transparent reporting. It is not presented as the
  scientific contribution of the survey.

## Units of Analysis

- `227` is the number of eligible reports; `206` is the number of unique
  included studies.
- `8,306` denotes governed extraction/coding records, not independent effects.
- The primary synthesis contains `8,203` records: `3,020` evidence records,
  `4,779` metric records, and `404` trade-off records.
- The trade-off ledger contains `404` coded rows linked to `169` studies,
  including two explicit absence-status audit rows. Substantive trade-off
  evidence comprises `402` non-absent records from `168` studies. The records
  must not be called experiments, effects, or independent observations.
- The reduction from 227 reports to 206 studies is report-to-study
  reconciliation, not an additional exclusion stage.

## Quantitative Comparison

- Of 4,779 primary metric records, 118 support record-specific bounded
  relations across studies under aligned definitions and conditions. The
  remaining 4,661 support interpretation in their native evidence contexts.
- The comparison contract assigns every record one of these analytical roles.
- Corpus-level synthesis is expressed through recurring mechanisms, bounded
  cases, within-study interpretation, feasibility evidence, and qualitative
  synthesis.
- Every bounded cross-study relation retains the metric definition,
  measurement plane, task, scenario, operating conditions, validation type,
  and provenance.
- Range resolution, estimator error, precision, and a Cramér--Rao bound are
  distinct quantities. Electrical SNR, OSNR, received optical power, gross
  rate, and net rate are likewise non-interchangeable.

## Appraisal

- TQAF is a review-specific deterministic eight-dimension reporting and
  technical-adequacy rubric. It is not a validated risk-of-bias tool, GRADE,
  or an independently verified scale.
- Its overall labels are `strong` (125), `adequate` (75), and `low` (6) under
  this review's rules.
- The 115 evidence-body labels are review-defined groupings; they must not be
  called GRADE certainty categories.
- Workflow labels such as `survey_ready` are claim-use states, not scientific
  quality certification.

## Scope and Novelty

- Do not claim that this is the first, only, most comprehensive, or first
  unified O-ISAC survey.
- State the defensible distinction: a frozen, report-reconciled 206-study
  optical evidence base is organized across modalities with claim-level
  governance, measurement-plane normalization, explicit comparison controls,
  trade-off synthesis, and validation/reproducibility appraisal.
- Photonics-assisted THz may use optical generation or distribution while the
  radiated link remains a THz radio link. Do not label every such propagation
  path as optical.
- VLC and LiFi are grouped for this review; they are not universal synonyms.
- Corpus distributions describe the included literature, not worldwide field
  prevalence or market maturity.

## Required Limitations

The final manuscript must disclose the date-bounded six-source English search,
58 unretrieved reports, incomplete query-to-export mapping for two low-yield
Taylor & Francis exports, retrospective registration and re-baselining,
investigator-supervised conduct without routine duplicate human review,
review-specific TQAF, absence of GRADE, formal missing-results bias
assessment, sensitivity analysis, meta-analysis, and causal moderator analysis,
possible taxonomy/coding error, extraction-granularity effects, 92 legacy
metric rows, and the fact that artifact availability was assessed mainly from
source reporting rather than universal link testing.

## Prose Rules

- Findings are bounded with phrases such as `within the included corpus` when
  the denominator could otherwise be read as a universal field claim.
- Prefer `indicates`, `was coded as`, and `was reported` to `proves`,
  `demonstrates`, or `establishes` unless the stronger verb is directly
  warranted.
- Methods and PRISMA details remain compact outside the Methods section.
- Tables may inventory evidence; prose must explain the engineering meaning,
  conditions, and limits rather than repeat table cells.
