## Figure 2: Photonic ISAC Testbed (Pearl River Estuary)
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_0.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_020/O_ISAC_020/_page_2_Figure_0.jpeg)
- **(i) Hardware:** Shared rack at Sanjiao Island control office housing both discrete communication modules (Top) and $\phi$-OTDR sensing modules (Bottom).
- **(ii) Spectrum:** WDM spectral allocation showing 4 primary data channels ($1550$ nm region) and a dedicated monitoring channel (at the edge) used for the sensing probe.

- **Mechanism:** Demonstrates $\phi$-OTDR principle where a coherent probe pulse is injected and backscattered signals are used to recover acoustic/vibration phases.

## Figure 3: Ocean and Seismic Wave Characterization (f-k Spectrum)
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_5.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_020/O_ISAC_020/_page_3_Figure_5.jpeg)
- **Type:** 2D Heatmap (Frequency vs. Wavenumber).
- **Sensing Range:** 2 km to 11 km from the interrogator.
- **Ocean Waves:** Dominant energy below **0.3 Hz**, propagating westward (Q2, Q4) along the **10 m/s** phase velocity line. Matches surface gravity dispersion relation (white dashed line).
- **Seismic Waves:** Higher frequency components (**>0.8 Hz**) with smaller wavenumbers, localized between the **100 m/s and 1000 m/s** phase velocity lines.

## Figure 4: Real-time Earthquake Waveform Detection
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_020/O_ISAC_020/_page_4_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Event:** Mw 4.0 earthquake, 143 km from testbed (2022-03-13).
- **Sensing Elements:** 1226 virtual sensing channels along 10 km of fiber (8.16 m interval).
- **Observation:** Clear distinction of **P Wave** (arriving at **24.5 s**) and **S Wave** (arriving at **41.5 s**).
- **Velocities:** Slopes denote propagation velocities of **5.9 km/s** (P) and **3.47 km/s** (S).
- **Filtering:** 2–15 Hz bandpass applied to isolate seismic signals from low-frequency ocean noise.

## Figure 5: Regional Seismic Event Catalog (Sept-Oct 2021)
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_6.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_020/O_ISAC_020/_page_4_Figure_6.jpeg)
- **Type:** Geographic Map with Event Overlay.
- **Coverage:** Successfully detected earthquakes in Taiwan (Mw 6.3), Japan (Mw 5.6), Philippines (Mw 5.7), and Southwest China (Mw 4.3, 6.0).
- **Sensitivity:** Local microseisms in Guangdong detected down to **Mw 1.1** within a 200 km radius, demonstrating submarine fiber's superior sensitivity over sparse land-based seismic stations.

## Figure 6: Marine Vessel (Ferry) Tracking
**File:** [_page_5_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_020/O_ISAC_020/_page_5_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **Type:** Spatio-temporal Trace (Time vs. Distance).
- **Description:** Tracks a daily ferry crossing the buried fiber line.
- **Quantification:** Dotted trajectory line yields a mapping speed of **26 km/h** (approx. 14 knots).
- **Signature:** Pronounced bi-polar strain-rate fluctuations (Red/Blue peaks) across a 4 km spatial window, capturing the hydrodynamic pressure and engine vibration of the passing vessel.
