Type: System Diagram / Schematic (380-km Field Trial)
---
Components:
- NKT Lasers: Low-phase-noise seed lasers for signal/LO.
- FS (Frequency Shifter): Offsets seed frequency for heterodyne detection.
- IQM 1 & 2: Modulators for 32-Gbaud 16-QAM data.
- Pol Mux: Polarization multiplexing units.
- Coherent Rx (CW/CCW): Receivers for bi-directional sensing/data.
- DSO (Digital Sampling Oscilloscope): 80-Gsps 33-GHz 4-channel capture.
- Bidirectional Repeaters: Spaced across the 380km field loop.
- Localization (c, d): Correlation peaks for vibration events at -1.67 ms.

Flow:
1. Seed lasers generate optical carriers which are frequency-shifted (16.5 GHz) and modulated with 16-QAM data.
2. Signals travel in Clockwise (CW) and Counter-Clockwise (CCW) directions through the field loop (Long Beach Island, Forked River, Seaside Heights, etc.).
3. Vibrations at specific fiber poles induce phase shifts on both signals.
4. The Coherent Rx at the hub performs heterodyne single-sided detection.
5. Cross-correlation of CW and CCW signals yields a time-delay peak used to localize events with ~7km uncertainty ($34 \mu s$ s.d.).

Type: System Diagram / Schematic & Performance Charts (1007-km DAS)
---
Components:
- DWDM Transmitter: Source for multi-wavelength 200-Gb/s traffic.
- Sensing Tx: Comprises AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator) and MZM for pulse shaping.
- WSS (Wavelength Selective Switch): Combines data and sensing wavelengths.
- SSMF Link: 1007km total distance across 13 spans with All-Raman amplification.
- Raman Pumps: Bi-directional pumps for gain without isolators.
- Sensing Rx: Coherent detection block (Optical Hybrid + BPDs + ADC).

Flow (Setup):
1. 10 Tb/s data traffic is combined with a chirped sensing pulse sequence.
2. The sensing sequence is amplitude-shaped ($T_{rt}=60 \mu s$ transitions) to minimize nonlinear crosstalk.
3. The reflective backscatter is captured by the coherent Sensing Rx for vibration monitoring over the full 1007km.

Chart (b) - BER Analysis:
- Axes Extraction: X-axis: P_{sens} (dBm) [-5 to 10]. Y-axis: Post-FEC BER [10^-10 to 10^-2].
- Legend: T_{rt} = 0, 20, 40, 60 \mu s (Shaping rise/fall times).
- Data Points: For T_{rt}=60 \mu s, the sensing pulse power can be increased to **+3.2 dBm** while maintaining BER below the 1.5e-2 FEC limit. T_{rt}=0 (no pulses) limits power to ~-2 dBm.

Chart (c) - Sensing Sensitivity:
- Axes Extraction: X-axis: Position (km) [0 to 1070]. Y-axis: Sensitivity (p\epsilon/\sqrt{Hz}) [10^1 to 10^3].
- Data Points: Sensitivity is approx **$16 p\epsilon/\sqrt{Hz}$** at the start of the fiber and degrades to **$100 p\epsilon/\sqrt{Hz}$** after 1007 km of SSMF.
