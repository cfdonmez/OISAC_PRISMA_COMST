Type: System Diagram / Schematic (FSO-ISAC Concept Overview)
---
Components:
- Tx Terminal: Optical transmitter with laser source and modulator.
- ISAC Waveform Generator: Creates joint communication-sensing signal.
- Beam Steering Unit: Directs optical beam toward target/receiver.
- Atmospheric Channel: Free-space propagation medium.
- Target: Reflective object for sensing (range, velocity estimation).
- Rx Terminal: Coherent receiver with photodetector.
- DSP Unit: Joint demodulation and sensing parameter extraction.

Flow:
1. ISAC waveform is generated combining data symbols and sensing pilots.
2. Tx Terminal modulates the optical carrier with ISAC waveform.
3. Beam Steering directs the beam toward Rx/Target.
4. Signal propagates through atmospheric channel.
5. Target reflects portion of signal (for sensing).
6. Rx Terminal receives direct + reflected signals.
7. DSP performs joint communication decoding and sensing estimation.

Image: _page_7_Figure_13.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Photonics-based ISAC Transmitter)
---
Components:
- CW Laser: 1550 nm continuous-wave source.
- IQ Modulator: Dual-parallel MZM for complex modulation.
- AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator): Generates I/Q baseband signals.
- RF Amplifier: Boosts AWG output to drive modulator.
- EDFA: Amplifies modulated optical signal.
- BPF (Bandpass Filter): Removes ASE noise.
- Beam Expander: Collimates optical beam for FSO transmission.
- Steering Mirror: Directs beam toward target/receiver.

Flow:
1. CW Laser outputs at 1550 nm.
2. AWG generates I/Q waveforms (OFDM ISAC signal).
3. RF Amplifier boosts signals to Vπ level.
4. IQ Modulator imprints complex waveform onto optical carrier.
5. EDFA amplifies to +20 dBm output power.
6. BPF removes out-of-band ASE.
7. Beam Expander shapes beam (5 cm aperture).
8. Steering Mirror directs toward FSO link.

Image: _page_8_Figure_20.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Coherent FSO-ISAC Receiver)
---
Components:
- Rx Telescope: 10 cm aperture collection optics.
- Mode Matching Optics: Couples free-space beam to SMF.
- LO (Local Oscillator): Narrow-linewidth reference laser.
- 90° Optical Hybrid: Mixes signal with LO for I/Q detection.
- BPD (Balanced Photodetectors): 4 BPDs for differential detection.
- TIA (Transimpedance Amplifier): Converts photocurrent to voltage.
- ADC: 80 GSa/s digitization.
- DSP: Performs carrier recovery, equalization, sensing estimation.

Flow:
1. Rx Telescope collects incoming optical beam.
2. Mode Matching couples light into single-mode fiber.
3. 90° Hybrid mixes received signal with LO.
4. 4 BPDs detect I+, I-, Q+, Q- components.
5. TIAs amplify photocurrents.
6. ADC samples at 80 GSa/s.
7. DSP recovers data and extracts sensing parameters.
