---
**Components:**
- **Space Layer:** Satellites (Low Earth Orbit?) with inter-satellite links and beams to ground/air.
- **Air Layer:** UAVs/Drones serving as relay nodes or aerial base stations.
- **Ground Layer:** Users (Phones), Vehicles (Cars), Ground Stations (Dishes), Infrastructure (Houses), Core Network (Fiber links in "lake"? or just ground).
- **Links:** Red/Blue/Purple beams indicating multi-layer connectivity (Satellite-to-Ground, UAV-to-Ground, etc.).

**Flow:**
Integrated Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) and Terrestrial Network (TN).
1.  **Satellites** provide wide area coverage and backhaul.
2.  **UAVs** provide intermediate coverage or relaying.
3.  **Ground Stations** gateway the space segment to the terrestrial infrastructure.
4.  **End Users** (Cars, Phones) connect to the best available node (Satellite, UAV, or Tower).

**Components:**
- **Orchestration:** Crossdomain Orchestration (Red bar), Service Management and Orchestration (SMO).
- **RIC:** Non-real time RIC (RAN Intelligent Controller), Near real time RIC.
- **Domains:** NTN domain 1 ... NTN domain n.
- **TN Infrastructure:** Data collection -> Reasoner -> Control. Management, Service Based Architecture (SBA).
- **Interfaces:** KPI Data (In), Control (Out).

**Flow:**
O-RAN based architecture for NTN-TN integration.
1.  **Management:** The SMO and Non-real time RIC handle high-level policy and orchestration across domains.
2.  **Control Loop:** The Near real time RIC operates closer to the edge for faster control.
3.  **Integration:** The "Crossdomain Orchestration" coordinates between separate NTN domains and the TN infrastructure.
4.  **Data Driven:** The system relies on "Data collection" and a "Reasoner" (AI/ML) to optimize network performance via the "Control" interface.
