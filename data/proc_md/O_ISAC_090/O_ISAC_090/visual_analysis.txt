Type: Functional System Diagram
---
**Components:**
* **West-End Laser & East-End Laser**
* **PBS (Polarization Beam Splitter)**
* **IQM (I/Q Modulator)**
* **DSP + DAC** (Input streams $I_X, Q_X, I_Y, Q_Y$)
* **$\Delta\tau$ (Delay block)**
* **AMP (EDFA)**
* **90° Hybrid**
* **ADC + DSP**
**Signal Flow:**
West-End Laser source is split by a PBS into X and Y polarizations. One path goes through dual IQMs (modulated by DSP/DAC) and the other through a delay $\Delta\tau$. They are recombined and transmitted through a fiber link with multiple inline amplifiers (AMPs). A vibration event ($\varphi_{vib}(t)$) occurs at distance $Z_W$ from West and $Z_E$ from East. At the East receiver, the signal is mixed with the East-End LO in a 90° Hybrid, followed by ADC and DSP for phase recovery ($\varphi_{LO}(t)$).

Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: DSP Frequency-Domain Processing Flowchart
---
**Flow:**
1. **Independent phase estimation:** Extracts $\varphi_{f0,w}[n]$ and $\varphi_{f1,w}[n]$ from subcarriers $f_0$ and $f_1$.
2. **Summation ($\Sigma$):** Calculates the differential phase $\Delta\varphi[n]$.
3. **FFT:** Converts to frequency domain $\Delta\varphi[\omega]$.
4. **Equalization:** Multiplies by $1/H[\omega]$ (compensation filter).
5. **IFFT:** Converts back to time domain to yield the recovered laser phase $\hat{\varphi}_{L,w}[n]$.
**Inset (H[ω]):** Shows a periodic transfer function with zeros at $2\pi/\Delta\tau$ multiples.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Experimental Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Transponders (West/East):** EDFA, Splitters (75/25, 20/80), VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator), PC (Polarization Controller), IQM, AWG 8199A, West/East 90° Hybrids, LPFs, RTO (Real-Time Oscilloscope).
* **Link:** 80 km fiber spans, inline Rx EDFAs.
* **Sensing:** PZT (Piezoelectric Transducer) driven by an AWG to simulate vibrations.
**Connection Path:** West Laser -> EDFA -> 75/25 split. 25% goes to Local Hybrid. 75% goes to 20/80 split. Path A (20%) goes through delay $\Delta\tau$, VOA, PC. Path B (80%) goes through IQM (driven by AWG). Paths recombine (10/90) and enter the 80km link via Tx EDFA.
