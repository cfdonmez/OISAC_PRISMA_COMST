## Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
### Type: Conceptual Diagram & System Principle

### Title: Operating Principle of ISAC-OF

### Panel (a) Waveform:
- Transmission Light E_X(t)
- LFM Optical Carrier (Chirped freq vs Time)
- Loaded with PAM4 data (Amplitude modulation)

### Panel (b) Fiber Effects:
- Interaction of CD (Chromatic Dispersion), SBS (Stimulated Brillouin Scattering), and SPM (Self-Phase Modulation).
- Problem: SBS limits power; CD fades power.
- Solution: LFM suppresses SBS -> Allows High Power -> High Power induces SPM -> SPM compensates CD.

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Fundamental mechanism explanation for why this ISAC waveform improves *communication* performance (Synergy).

## Image: _page_4_Figure_2.jpeg
### Type: Experimental Setup Diagram

### Title: ISAC-OF Experimental Setup

### Components:
- **Source:** CW Laser (1549.5 nm)
- **Modulation Chain:**
  1. MZM1 (Driven by AWG LFM) -> OBPF -> LFM Carrier (SSB)
  2. MZM2 (Driven by PAM4) -> Data Modulation
- **Channel:** 24.5 km SMF (Standard Single Mode Fiber)
- **Receiver (Comms):** Direct Detection (PD -> DSO)
- **Receiver (Sensing):** Coherent Detection (Ref Light + RBS -> Balanced PD -> DSO)

### Key Parameters:
- Repetition Period: 248 us (Range Ambiguity > 24.5 km)
- Sample Rate: 65 GSa/s (Tx), 80 GSa/s (Rx Comms)

### Title: BER Performance Comparison (ISAC vs Conventional)

### Panel (a) BER vs Power:
- Conventional PAM4 (Orange): BER degrades > 13 dBm launch power due to SBS.
- ISAC (Blue/Purple): BER improves up to 16 dBm.
- Result: ISAC tolerates 7 dB higher power.

### Panel (c) Stokes Power (SBS indicator):
- Conventional: Stokes power rises sharply to 12 dBm.
- ISAC: Stokes power suppressed at -28 dBm (due to LFM).

### Panel (d) Sensitivity:
- ISAC achieves ~1.3 dB sensitivity gain at FEC threshold compared to optimized Conventional.

Key Technical Findings:
1. **Synergy:** LFM carrier suppresses SBS by >40 dB (Fig 3c), allowing launch power to increase from 9 to 16 dBm.
2. **Comms Improvement:** Higher launch power enhances SPM, which compensates CD, improving BER by 1.3 dB.
3. **Sensing Performance:**
   - Spatial Resolution: 4 m
   - Strain Resolution: 4.32 ne/sqrt(Hz)
   - Frequency Response: >21 kHz (via stitching)
   - Bandwidth used: 1-2 GHz
4. **Data Rate:** 56 Gbit/s PAM4 (Net Rate > 100 Gbps potential with WDM).

Key Innovations:
1. **ISAC-OF Scheme:** Using LFM as both the Comms Carrier (to kill SBS) and Sensing Probe (Phi-OTDR).
2. **SBS-SPM-CD Interplay:** Exploiting nonlinearities rather than just mitigating them.
