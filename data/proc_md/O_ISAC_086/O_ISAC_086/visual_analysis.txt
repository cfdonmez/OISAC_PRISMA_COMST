Type: System Architecture Diagram
---
**Components:**
* **Transmitter (MMW Multiband ISAC signal TX):** OFDM/LFM generators, Phase Modulator, I/Q-MZM, AWG, Optical Frequency Comb (OFC), WSS, EDFA.
* **Communication Receiver:** Horn antennas (RX Antennas), BPF, LNAs, Mixers, Local Oscillators (LO), ADC.
* **Radar Receiver (Coherent multiband radar RX):** MZM-1 to N, OBPF-1 to N, Variable Optical Delay Line (VODL), Low-speed PDs, ADC & DSP.
* **Scenario:** Radar detection of targets (e.g., vehicles) using multiple frequency bands simultaneously for communication and sensing.
---

Image: _page_3_Figure_11.jpeg
Type: Multiband Data Fusion Flowchart
---
**Flow:**
1. **Spectral Input:** Discrete sub-bands ($B_1, B_2, B_N$) interspersed with vacant bands ($B_{v1}, B_{vM}$).
2. **Processing:** All-pole model fitting applied to each sub-band.
3. **Fusion:** Multiple sub-bands data fusion occurs to bridge the vacant gaps.
4. **Output:** Ultra-high resolution radar ranging and imaging.
