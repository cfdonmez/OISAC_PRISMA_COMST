Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Passive Optical Network ISAC)
---
Components:
- OLT (Optical Line Terminal): Central office.
- Feeder Fiber: 20 km SMF-28.
- Splitter: 1:32 passive.
- Drop Fiber: 5 km per ONU.
- ONU Array: 32× Optical Network Units.
- Sensing Target: Vehicle in drop zone.
- Uplink Burst Tx.
- Downlink Continuous Rx.

Sub-components:
- DFB Laser: 1490 nm downstream.
- APD: 1310 nm upstream receiver.
- RSOA: Seeded upstream at ONU.
- TDM Frame: GPON standard.

Sensing Integration:
- Reflectometry on drop fiber.
- Target detection using upstream gaps.
- Range: 0-5 km per branch.

Flow:
1. OLT: DFB → EDFA → Feeder → Splitter.
2. Splitter → 32× Drop Fibers → ONUs.
3. Sensing: OTDR pulses during guard bands.
4. Upstream: RSOA bursts → Splitter → Feeder → OLT.

Type: Chart (Multi-Panel: PON Sensing Performance)
---
Panel (a) - OTDR Trace:
Axes:
- X-axis: Distance (km). Range: 0 to 25 km.
- Y-axis: Backscatter Power (dB). Range: -40 to -10 dB.

Data Points:
- Splitter @ 20 km: 15 dB loss step.
- ONU reflections @ 22-25 km.
- Spatial resolution: 10 m.

Panel (b) - Target Detection:
Axes:
- X-axis: Range in drop (m). Range: 0 to 200 m.
- Y-axis: Reflected Power (dB). Range: -50 to -20 dB.

Data Points:
- Vehicle @ 85 m: Reflection = -28 dB, SNR = 18 dB.
- Pedestrian @ 120 m: Reflection = -42 dB, SNR = 8 dB.

Panel (c) - Communication Throughput:
- Downstream: 2.5 Gbps aggregate.
- Upstream: 1.25 Gbps aggregate.
- Sensing overhead: <5%.
