**Components:**
- **Multi-carrier integrated waveform:**
    - **OFDM:** Sinc-shaped subcarriers in frequency domain, processed via IDFT.
    - **OCDM:** Linearly chirped subcarriers in time-frequency plane, processed via IDFnT (Inverse Discrete Fractional Fourier Transform).
- **Single-carrier integrated waveform:**
    - **LFMCW:** Linearly Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (Radar).
    - **SC:** Single Carrier modulation (Communication).
    - **LFM-PSK:** PSK data embedded onto an LFM chirp.

**Flow:**
The diagram compares waveform integration strategies. In multi-carrier systems, OFDM transitions to OCDM via fractional transform to improve chirp-like sensing properties. In single-carrier systems, radar (LFMCW) and communication (SC) signals are combined or embedded (LFM-PSK) to share the same time-frequency resources.

Image: _page_4_Figure_14.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **Photonic THz-ISAC Transmitter:** LD (Laser Diode), PM (Phase Modulator), WSS (Wavelength Selective Switch), EDFA (1 & 2), PC (1, 2, 3), AWG, IQM, UTC-PD (Uni-Traveling-Carrier Photodiode), Tx Antenna.
- **Com. Receiver:** Rx Antenna, Mixer1, LO1, LNA1, DSO (Digital Storage Oscilloscope).
- **Rad. Receiver:** Rx Antenna, Mixer2, LO2, LNA2, DSO.
- **DSP Blocks:** Tx DSP (S/P, Mod, IFFT/IDFnT, Up/Dn-chirp), Rx Com. DSP (Sync, FFT/DFnT, Equ, BER Calculation).

**Flow:**
Optical carriers from an LD are modulated and split by WSS into dual paths (PC1/PC2). Data is modulated via IQM (driven by AWG) and recombined for UTC-PD, which generates THz signals. The transmitted THz-ISAC signal is received by separate Communication and Radar receivers. The DSP blocks manage waveform synthesis (Up/Down chirps) and high-speed data recovery.
