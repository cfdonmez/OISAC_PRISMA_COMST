Type: System Diagram / Schematic (FMCW LiDAR Waveform Concept)
---
Components:
- Frequency Axis: Showing chirp modulation pattern.
- Time Axis: Duration of chirp cycles.
- Tx Chirp: Transmitted frequency-modulated waveform.
- Rx Chirp: Received waveform with time delay τ.
- Beat Frequency (fb): Difference frequency for range measurement.

Description:
- Chirp bandwidth (B): Illustrated span on frequency axis.
- Chirp period (T): Duration of one complete up/down chirp.
- Time delay (τ): Proportional to target range (R = c·τ/2).
- Beat frequency: fb = 2·B·R / (c·T).

Key Parameters (from diagram labels):
- Chirp slope: S = B/T.
- Range resolution: ΔR = c/(2B).
- Maximum unambiguous range: Rmax = c·T/2.

Data Points:
- At SNR = 10 dB: 500 MHz = 25 cm; 1 GHz = 12 cm; 2 GHz = 6 cm; 4 GHz = 3 cm.
- At SNR = 20 dB: 500 MHz = 8 cm; 1 GHz = 4 cm; 2 GHz = 2 cm; 4 GHz = 1 cm.
- At SNR = 30 dB: 500 MHz = 2.5 cm; 1 GHz = 1.3 cm; 2 GHz = 0.6 cm; 4 GHz = 0.3 cm.

Trend:
- Range accuracy scales as RMSE ∝ 1/(B·√SNR).
- 4 GHz bandwidth achieves sub-cm accuracy at SNR > 25 dB.
- Doubling bandwidth halves RMSE (consistent with Cramér-Rao bound).

Image: _page_1_Picture_12.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Automotive LiDAR ISAC Concept)
---
Components:
- Vehicle: Equipped with LiDAR transceiver.
- LiDAR Unit: FMCW-based coherent sensor.
- Target Vehicles: Other cars in traffic.
- Pedestrians: Moving objects to detect.
- Infrastructure: Road signs, buildings.
- V2X Link: Vehicle-to-everything communication overlay.
- Data Link: Communication channel using sensing waveform.

Flow:
1. Vehicle LiDAR emits FMCW optical beam.
2. Beam scans surrounding environment (targets, pedestrians, infrastructure).
3. Reflected signals processed for range/velocity estimation.
4. Communication data encoded on same waveform to nearby vehicles (V2V).
5. Nearby vehicles decode data while performing own sensing.
6. V2X infrastructure nodes also receive communication overlay.

Axes:
- X-axis: Data Rate (Mbps). Range: 0 to 500 Mbps.

Legend:
- Range Accuracy (Blue solid): Left axis.
- BER (Red dashed): Right axis.

Data Points:
- At 50 Mbps: Range = 1.5 cm; BER = 10^-6.
- At 100 Mbps: Range = 2.0 cm; BER = 10^-5.
- At 200 Mbps: Range = 3.5 cm; BER = 10^-4.
- At 300 Mbps: Range = 6.0 cm; BER = 5×10^-4.
- At 500 Mbps: Range = 12 cm; BER = 10^-3.

Trend:
- Range accuracy degrades approximately linearly with data rate.
- BER increases exponentially with data rate (limited by OSNR).
- Optimal operating point: ~100-200 Mbps for balanced performance.
- At 500 Mbps: 8× worse ranging but still sub-BER threshold.
