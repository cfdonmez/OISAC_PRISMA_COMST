**Components:**
- Tx DSP blocks: PRBS, QAM Mapping, S/P (Serial to Parallel), ISFFT (IDFT, DFT), HS, CP+&TS+ (Cyclic Prefix & Training Sequence insertion), P/S.
- Antennas (Tx and Rx)
- Rad. Rx DSP blocks: S/P, TS-&CP-, DFT, SFFT (IDFT, DFT), ConMul. (Conjugate Multiplication), ZP-2D-DFT (Zero-Padded 2D DFT), H_Rad (Radar Channel Matrix).
- Com. Rx DSP blocks: Synch., S/P, TS-&CP-, DFT, SFFT (IDFT, DFT), Channel Est. & Equ., QAM Demap., Recovered bits.

**Flow:**
1.  **Tx DSP:** Data bits from PRBS are mapped to QAM symbols, converted to parallel, processed by ISFFT (Inverse Symplectic Fast Fourier Transform), and formatted with CP/TS before transmission via antenna.
2.  **Rad. Rx DSP:** The received radar signal is processed to remove overhead (TS/CP), transformed via DFT and SFFT, and then processed using Conjugate Multiplication and ZP-2D-DFT to extract radar information (likely Range/Doppler).
3.  **Com. Rx DSP:** The communication signal is synchronized, overhead removed, transformed via DFT/SFFT, equalized using channel estimation, and demapped to recover the original bits.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **ISAC Transmitter:** Tx DSP, AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator), Bias-Tee, ECL1 (External Cavity Laser at 192.723 THz), ECL2 (at 192.824 THz), PC (Polarization Controller), MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator), SSMF (50km Standard Single Mode Fiber), OC (Optical Coupler).
- **Wireless Link:** EDFA, VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator), PD (Photodiode), EA (Electrical Amplifier), Tx HA (Transmitter Horn Antenna), Target (Corner Reflector) at 1.6m distance.
- **Receiver:** Rx HA (Receiver Horn Antenna), ED (Envelope Detector), KPA (K-band Power Amplifier), DSO (Digital Storage Oscilloscope), Com./Rad. Rx DSP.

**Flow:**
**ECL1** provides the optical carrier, which passes through a **PC** and is modulated by the **MZM**. The MZM is driven by the **Tx DSP** signal via an **AWG** and **Bias-Tee**. This modulated signal travels through **50km SSMF**. It is combined with **ECL2** (local oscillator/reference) via an **OC**. The signal is then amplified by an **EDFA**, attenuated by a **VOA**, and converted to an electrical signal by a **PD**. This electrical signal is amplified by an **EA** and radiated by a **Tx HA**. The signal reflects off a **Target** (1.6m away) and/or travels to the **Rx HA**. At the receiver, the signal is detected by an **ED**, amplified by a **KPA** and **EA**, captured by a **DSO**, and processed by the **Com./Rad. Rx DSP**.
