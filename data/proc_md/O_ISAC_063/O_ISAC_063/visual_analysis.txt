**Components:**
- **Full-field frequency response section:** 
  - Charts for I and Q branches: |A(f)| (amplitude response) and φ(f) (phase response).
  - IQ skew formula: τ_I - τ_Q.
- **TOSA (Transmitter Optical Sub-Assembly):**
  - Digital Input.
  - DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter).
  - EA (Electrical Amplifier).
  - Laser.
  - IQM (I/Q Modulator).
  - Optical Output.

**Flow:**
Digital inputs A_I(f)e^{jφ_I(f)} and A_Q(f)e^{jφ_Q(f)} are processed through DACs and EAs. These electrical signals then drive the IQM. A Laser provides the optical carrier to the IQM, which modulates the carrier and produces the Optical Output. The left panel illustrates how the frequency response and temporal skew (τ_I, τ_Q) affect the complex signal.

Type: System Diagram / Schematic

**Components:**
- **Transmitter Side:**
  - Signal generators for C_{1I} to C_{4I} (In-phase) and C_{1Q} to C_{4Q} (Quadrature).
  - Labels for frequency spacing (Δf_ILAFR, Δf_ILPFR) and subcarrier durations.
  - TOSA (Transmitter Optical Sub-Assembly).
- **Receiver Side:**
  - Optical Spectrum display.
  - PD (Photodiode).
  - ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) + FFT (Fast Fourier Transform).
- **Analysis Side:**
  - AFR @ I, AFR @ Q, PFR @ I, PFR @ Q charts showing |H_{iI}|, |H_{iQ}|, Ψ_{S/b,iI}, and Ψ_{S/b,iQ}.

**Flow:**
Multi-tone/comb-like signals are generated in the I and Q domains with specific frequency offsets for Amplitude Frequency Response (AFR) and Phase Frequency Response (PFR) measurement. These signals pass through the TOSA. At the receiver, the Optical Spectrum is captured. After detection by the PD, the signal is digitized (ADC) and analyzed via FFT to extract the AFR and PFR for both In-phase and Quadrature branches (AFR@I/Q, PFR@I/Q).

Type: System Diagram / Schematic

**Components:**
- **Tx DSP (Transmitter):**
  - PRBS (Pseudo-Random Binary Sequence).
  - Constellation Mapping.
  - Up sampling.
  - RRC (Root-Raised Cosine).
  - Resample.
  - **Frequency Response Pre-compensate** (Highlighted yellow).
- **Physical Link:**
  - AWG.
  - Coherent TOSA.
  - Laser.
  - ICR (Integrated Coherent Receiver).
  - DSO.
- **Rx DSP (Receiver):**
  - Resample.
  - GSOP (Gram-Schmidt Orthogonalization Procedure).
  - RRC.
  - Synchronization.
  - Decode.
  - BER (Bit Error Rate).

**Flow:**
Signal is generated and pre-compensated in Tx DSP to counteract the previously measured frequency response. It passes through the Coherent TOSA, undergoes coherent detection at the ICR, is digitized by the DSO, and finally processed by the Rx DSP to calculate the BER.

**Structure:**
- **Format:** 16QAM (16 distinct clusters in a 4x4 grid).
- **Rows:** 32GBaud and 64GBaud.
- **Columns:** (a/d) w/o calibration, (b/e) w/ amp calibration, (c/f) w/ full calibration.

**Quality & Data Points:**
- **32GBaud:**
  - (a) Blurred clusters, BER @ 4.6e-4.
  - (c) Sharp, well-separated clusters, BER @ 1.9e-4.
- **64GBaud:**
  - (d) High noise, overlapping clusters, BER @ 6.1e-3.
  - (f) Recovered grid structure, clusters distinct but larger than 32G, BER @ 2.1e-3.

**Trend:**
Calibration significantly improves bit error rate and cluster separation, especially at higher baud rates (64GBaud) where frequency response distortion is more severe. Full calibration (amplitude + phase + skew) provides the best performance.
