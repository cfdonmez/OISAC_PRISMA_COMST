Type: Integrated Signal Processing Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Electrical Processing:** Baseband signal $\mathbf{s}(t)$, Digital precoding block ($\mathbf{x}(t) = \mathbf{W}\mathbf{s}(t)$).
* **Optical Processing:** Laser source, multiple SOAs (Semiconductor Optical Amplifiers), Splitter network, EOPMs (Electro-Optic Phase Modulators).
* **Radiator:** Array of Edge emitters with spacing $d$.
* **Scenario:** Combined Optical Beamforming for Communication UEs (1 to K) and a Sensing target (vehicle).
**Flow:** Baseband signal is digitally precoded to produce signals $x_1(t)$ to $x_M(t)$. Laser source is amplified by SOA and split. Each optical branch is amplified by another SOA and phase-modulated by an EOPM (controlled by the precoded electrical signals $A_i x_i(t)$) before emission.

Type: Integrated Sensing and Loss Chart
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Azimuth angle $\theta_a$ ($\pi$) (-0.03 to 0.03).
* **Y-axis:** SNR gain $\varpi(\theta_a)$ [dB] (-15 to 15).
**Legend/Trends:**
* **Variables:** SDR (solid with markers) vs LP (dashed with markers) for $\Gamma = 15, 20, 25$ dB.
* **Performance:** SDR consistently maintains higher SNR gains (approx 7-10 dB across the range) regardless of target angle, whereas LP shows significant performance dips (down to -12 dB) at specific angles like 0.02.

Image: _page_5_Figure_1.jpeg
Type: Communication SINR vs ISLR Performance Chart
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Average ISLR (dB) (-2 to 10).
* **Y-axis:** Communication SINR (dB) (0 to 40).
**Data Points:**
* **SDR (Blue/Orange):** Threshold $\Gamma$ starts at 32 dB (at 1.7 dB ISLR) and gradually drops to 5 dB (at 9 dB ISLR).
* **LP (Yellow/Purple):** Starts at 25 dB (at -2.1 dB ISLR) and drops sharply to 0 dB at approx 8 dB ISLR.
**Trend:** Semi-Definite Relaxation (SDR) methods provide significantly better SINR (approx 10-15 dB advantage) compared to Linear Programming (LP) as the Integrated Sidelobe Ratio (ISLR) increases.
