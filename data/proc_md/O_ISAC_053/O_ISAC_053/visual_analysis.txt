**Flow:**
Control system block diagram.
1.  **Sensing:** The Coordinator's Receiver measures the angle (likely via the retroreflective foil reflection from the Worker).
2.  **Processing:** This "Measured Angle" is fed into a summation point, compared with an "Angle Set" point to generate an "Error" signal.
3.  **Control:** The "PID Controller" processes the error and generates a "Controller Output".
4.  **Actuation:** The output drives the "LED Module" (likely modulating the intensity or frequency 1/f) to illuminate the Worker or adjust the system state to minimize the error in the "Control Loop".

**Components:**

- **Phases:**
    -   **Sensing:** Signal is OFF (or constant low).
    -   **Illumination:** Signal is toggling ON/OFF (Square wave/PWM).
- **Sequence:** Sensing -> Illumination -> Sensing -> Illumination -> Sensing.

**Flow:**
The diagram illustrates a Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) or hybrid frame structure.
1.  **Sensing Interval:** The transmitter is in a specific state (likely OFF or listening) to perform sensing (detecting reflections without self-interference).
2.  **Illumination Interval:** The transmitter pulses (PWM) to provide illumination and potentially transmit data, while sensing is paused.
3.  **Repetition:** This cycle repeats, allowing both functions (Sensing and Illumination) to coexist in time.
