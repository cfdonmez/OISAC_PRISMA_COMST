Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Fiber-Wireless ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
- CW Laser: 1550 nm continuous-wave source.
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): E/O conversion.
- AWG: Arbitrary waveform generator for OFDM.
- EDFA: Erbium-doped fiber amplifier.
- SMF (Single-Mode Fiber): 25 km fiber span.
- OBPF: Optical bandpass filter.
- PD: High-speed photodetector.
- EA (Electrical Amplifier): RF gain stage.
- Circulator: Tx/Rx isolation.
- Horn Antenna: W-band radiator.
- Target: Radar reflector.
- LNA: Low-noise amplifier.
- Mixer: RF downconversion.
- ADC: Analog-to-digital converter.
- DSP: Joint radar-comm processing.

Flow:
1. CW Laser → MZM (OFDM from AWG) → EDFA → SMF (25 km).
2. At remote antenna unit: OBPF → PD → EA → Circulator → Horn Antenna.
3. RF signal radiates toward Target → reflected signal returns.
4. Circulator routes received signal to LNA → Mixer → ADC → DSP.
---
