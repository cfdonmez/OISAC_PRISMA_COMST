Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (FSO-ISAC Transceiver Architecture)
---
Components:
- Laser Diode (LD): 1550 nm CW source.
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): Electro-optic modulation.
- AWG: Arbitrary waveform generator for ISAC signal.
- EDFA: Erbium-doped fiber amplifier.
- Beam Expander: Collimation optics.
- FSO Channel: Free-space optical link.
- Target: Retro-reflector or diffuse surface.
- Telescope: Receive aperture (Cassegrain type).
- APD (Avalanche Photodiode): High-sensitivity detection.
- TIA: Trans-impedance amplifier.
- ADC: High-speed digitizer.
- DSP: Joint ranging and demodulation.

Flow:
1. LD → MZM (OFDM + sensing pilots) → EDFA.
2. Beam Expander transmits collimated beam to FSO Channel.
3. Signal reaches Target; partial reflection returns.
4. Telescope collects return signal.
5. APD detects optical power → TIA → ADC → DSP.
