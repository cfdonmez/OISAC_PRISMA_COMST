Image: _page_1_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: RO-ISAC System Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Downlink Device:** OFDM mod. with clipping, D/A, DC adder, LED, PD, A/D, Cross corr. (outputs Estimated distance), Uplink data extraction, Sync, OFDM demod. (outputs Uplink output bits).
* **Uplink Device:** PD, A/D, Sync, OFDM demod. (outputs Downlink output bits), CCR (Corner Cube Retroreflector), LED, DC adder, D/A, OFDM mod. with clipping.
**Signal Flow:** 
1. **Downlink:** Bits -> OFDM mod -> LED -> Free-space link.
2. **Reflected:** Downlink signal hits CCR -> reflected back to Downlink Device PD -> Cross corr. for distance estimation.
3. **Uplink:** Bits -> OFDM mod -> LED (on Uplink device) -> PD (on Downlink device) -> demod.
**Timing Inset (b):** $T_{\text{downlink}}$ followed by $T_{\text{AGI}}$ (Adaptive Guard Interval) which includes $\Delta T$ and $T_{\text{uplink}}$. $T_{\text{FGI}}$ is Fixed Guard Interval.

**Axes:** X: Length of time-domain window (30 to 70), Y: RMSE (cm) (0 to 3).

* **Axes:** X: Distance (cm) (48 to 96), Y: BER ($10^{-5}$ to $10^{-3}$).

Type: Chart (Overall Data Rate vs OFDM Symbols)
---
**Panels:** (a) 0.06m-0.85m, (b) 0.06m-8.5m, (c) 0.06m-85m.
**Axes:** X: Number of total OFDM symbols (10 to 70), Y: Overall data rate (Mbit/s).
**Comparison:** Fixed GI (blue) vs Adaptive GI (red curves).
**Data (c):** At 10 symbols, Fixed GI is ~183.5 Mbit/s. Adaptive GI ($d=0.06$m) is significantly higher at ~195.3 Mbit/s. Adaptive GI maintains higher rates across all configurations by optimizing the guard interval based on distance.

Type: Chart (BER/RMSE vs Clipping Ratio)
---
**Left Panel (BER):**
* **Axes:** X: Clipping ratio (dB) (1 to 13), Y: BER ($10^{-5}$ to $10^{-1}$).
* **Trend:** BER drops sharply between 5 and 7 dB. At 11 dB, BER reaches a minimum (approx $5 \times 10^{-5}$ for 48 cm).
**Right Panel (RMSE):**
* **Axes:** X: Clipping ratio (dB) (1 to 13), Y: RMSE (cm) (1 to 2.5).
* **Trend:** RMSE increases with clipping ratio. At 13 dB, 96 cm case has RMSE ~2.3 cm vs ~1.5 cm at 1 dB.
