**Components:**
- **Transmitter (Top):** User Data, QPSK Mapping, Multiplier (x), m Sequence, Shaping Filter, DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter), Laser, IQ Modulator.
- **ISAC-OW #1 (Receiver/Radar):**
    - **Physical Front-end:** Antennas (Tx and Rx), LO Laser, 2x4 90° Optical Hybrid, Photodiodes, ADC.
    - **Radar Path (Rad):** Matched Filter, Radar Processing, Object Detection.
    - **Communication Path (Com):** Matched Filter, Dispreading, QPSK Demapping, User Data.
- **ISAC-OW #2 (Communication Link Target):** Car icon.
- **ISAC-OW #2 Receiver (Bottom):** Coherent Receiver, ADC, Matched Filter, Communication Signal Processing.

**Flow:**
1. **Transmission:** User Data is QPSK mapped and multiplied by an m Sequence for spreading. The signal passes through a Shaping Filter and DAC. It then drives an IQ Modulator powered by a Laser source. The resulting optical signal s(t) is transmitted wirelessly.
2. **Channel:** The signal propagates to a target (Car - ISAC-OW #2) and back.
3. **ISAC-OW #1 Reception (Radar/Sensing):** The received echo s_r(t) is mixed with an LO Laser in a 2x4 90° Optical Hybrid. The outputs are detected by photodiodes and digitized by an ADC.
4. **Processing (ISAC-OW #1):** The digitized signal is split into Radar (Rad) and Communication (Com) branches. In the Rad branch, after a Matched Filter, it undergoes Radar Processing for Object Detection. In the Com branch, it is dispread (m Sequence), demapiped (QPSK), and recovered as User Data.
5. **ISAC-OW #2 Reception (Communication):** The direct signal s_c(t) is received, processed by a Coherent Receiver, ADC, Matched Filter, and finally undergoes Communication Signal Processing to recover the data.

- **Case: QPSK-DSSS Strong (Green Square):** At SNR = 25 dB, BER is approx **3 x 10^-5** (well below threshold).
- **Case: QPSK Moderate (Orange Square):** Crosses ABER_th at approx **SNR = 25.5 dB**.
- **Case: QPSK-DSSS Moderate (Purple Diamond):** Crosses ABER_th at approx **SNR = 16.5 dB**.
- **Case: QPSK-DSSS Weak (Dark Yellow Circle):** Achieves BER = 10^-6 at approx **SNR = 23 dB**.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** BER vs SNR for QPSK and QPSK-DSSS modulation schemes under different atmospheric turbulence conditions (Weak, Moderate, Strong).
- **Trend:** QPSK-DSSS significantly outperforms standard QPSK across all turbulence levels. The coding gain provided by the DSSS spreading (m-sequence) allows the system to maintain a reliable BER below the threshold even in strong turbulence where standard QPSK fails (stays above threshold even at 30 dB SNR).
