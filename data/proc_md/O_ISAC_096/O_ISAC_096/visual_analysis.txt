Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Photonic Radar-Communication System)
---
Components:
- ECL (External Cavity Laser): Narrow linewidth CW source at 1550 nm.
- IQ Modulator: Complex modulation for OFDM.
- AWG: Waveform generation (2 GSa/s).
- EDFA: Optical pre-amplification.
- SOA (Semiconductor Optical Amplifier): Booster stage.
- Circulator: Tx/Rx path separation.
- Optical Antenna: Free-space coupling.
- Target: Radar reflector.
- Balanced PD: Coherent detection.
- LO Path: Local oscillator distribution.
- Real-time Oscilloscope: 80 GSa/s digitizer.
- DSP: Offline processing.

Flow:
1. ECL → IQ Modulator (OFDM waveform from AWG).
2. EDFA → SOA → Circulator → Optical Antenna (Tx).
3. Target reflects signal → Antenna → Circulator (Rx path).
4. Balanced PD with LO from ECL tap → Oscilloscope → DSP.
