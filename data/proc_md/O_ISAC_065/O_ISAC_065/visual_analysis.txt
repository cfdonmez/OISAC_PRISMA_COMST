**Components:**
- **OCDM generation:** S/P, θ₂*, IDFT (IDFnT), θ₁*, CP+, P/S, PN+.
- **Radar processing:** DFT, (2N-1)DFT, Conjugation (*), (·)*, W, DFT, IDFT.
- **Communication processing:** Sync, S/P, CP-, DFT, Γ (DFnT), IDFT, S/P.
- **Experimental system:** Transmitter, Receiver.

**Flow:**
The diagram depicts the end-to-end processing for an Orthogonal Chirp Division Multiplexing (OCDM) based ISAC system. In the **OCDM generation** block, digital data X is transformed via an IDFnT process (involving phase rotations θ₁*, θ₂*) to generate a chirp-based signal S. After CP and PN addition (T), it is sent to the **Transmitter**. At the **Receiver**, the signal is split into two paths: **Radar processing**, which uses the transmitted signal S as a reference to perform cross-correlation or matched filtering (via (2N-1)DFTs) to detect targets; and **Communication processing**, which performs synchronization, CP removal, and a DFnT-based equalization process to recover the original data.

Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **ECL:** External Cavity Laser.
- **PM:** Phase Modulator with RF input.
- **PC1, PC2, PC3:** Polarization Controllers.
- **WSS:** Wavelength Selective Switch.
- **EDFA1, EDFA2, EDFA3:** Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifiers.
- **IQM:** I/Q Modulator driven by AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator).
- **SMF:** Single Mode Fiber (10km).
- **OBF:** Optical Bandpass Filter.
- **OC1, OC2:** Optical Couplers.
- **VOA:** Variable Optical Attenuator.
- **UTC-PD:** Uni-Traveling-Carrier Photodiode.
- **THz Lenses:** (1.57m path).
- **Mixer:** Powered by LO (Local Oscillator).
- **DSO:** Digital Storage Oscilloscope.
- **Meter:** Power meter.
- **Insets (a, b):** PSD (dB) vs Frequency (GHz) and Power (dBm) vs Frequency (THz).

**Flow:**
An ECL output is modulated by an RF signal in a PM to create a comb, which is processed by a WSS. Two paths emerge: one amplified by EDFA1 and sent to a coupler, the other amplified by EDFA2, filtered, and modulated with an OCDM/OFDM signal via an IQM (from AWG). This communication signal travels through 10km SMF, is re-amplified (EDFA3), filtered (OBF), and combined with the first path in OC1. The combined signal is attenuated (VOA), split (OC2) to a power meter, and finally converted to a THz signal by a UTC-PD. The THz beam is focused by lenses over a 1.57m path to a Target. The reflected/sensing signal is received by a Mixer (with LO) and analyzed on a DSO.

Type: Constellation Diagram
---
**Structure:**
- **Format:** 16QAM.
- **Rows:** (a) Back-to-back (B2B), (b) 1.57m wireless THz link.
- **Columns:** Without Equalization, Linear Equalization, Non-linear Equalization.

**Quality:**
- **Row (a):** Without equalization is slightly blurred. Linear brings it to a clean 4x4 grid. Non-linear provides even tighter clusters.
- **Row (b):** Without equalization is severely distorted (circular smear). Linear recovers the 16 clusters but with significant noise spread. Non-linear equalization (Right) provides the best cluster separation and defined boundaries.

**Trend:**
Non-linear equalization is essential for mitigating THz channel impairments and achieving communication reliability over the 1.57m link.
