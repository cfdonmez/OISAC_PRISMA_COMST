Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **RO-ISAC downlink device:** PAM mod., OFDM mod., Adaptive combiner, D/A, DC bias, BLED (Blue LED).
- **Reflected signal path:** CCR (Corner Cube Reflector), PD BOF (Photodiode with Blue Optical Filter).
- **RO-ISAC uplink device:** PD, A/D, Sync, HSM demodulation (Adaptive splitter, PAM dem., OFDM dem.), GLED (Green LED).
- **Uplink path:** PD GOF (Photodiode with Green Optical Filter).

**Flow:**
Input bits are modulated via a Hybrid Subcarrier Modulation (HSM) scheme combining PAM and OFDM. The "Adaptive combiner" merges them into a downlink signal transmitted by a BLED. Part of this signal is reflected by a CCR back to the downlink device's "Cross corr" unit for "Estimated distance" (sensing). The remaining downlink signal is received and demodulated by the "uplink device". Simultaneously, the uplink device transmits its own data via a GLED to the downlink device's "OFDM dem" unit.

**Legend/Trends:**
- Illustrates the time-domain structure of the Hybrid Subcarrier Modulation (HSM) for different ratios of PAM and OFDM allocation within a single symbol period.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Chart / Grid Plot (Communication & Sensing)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
- **(a) Downlink BER:** Y: BER [$10^{-4}$ to $10^{-1}$]; X: HSM ratio, α [0% to 100%].
- **(b) Uplink BER:** Y: BER [$10^{-4}$ to $10^{-1}$]; X: Distance (cm) [60 to 96].
- **(c) Ranging RMSE:** Y: RMSE (cm) [0.5 to 4]; X: HSM ratio, α [0% to 100%].
- **(d) Cross-correlation:** Y: Profile [0 to 1]; X: Index [0 to 100].

**Data Points:**
- **(a):** At **α = 0%** (Full OFDM), BER is as low as **$10^{-4}$** for $d=60$ cm. As α increases to 100% (Full PAM), BER rises to **$10^{-2}$**.
- **(c):** Ranging accuracy improves as α increases. At $d=60$ cm, RMSE drops from **~2.2 cm (α=0%)** to **~0.8 cm (α=100%)**.
- **(d):** Shows that PAM provides a much sharper correlation peak (Profile = 1.0) compared to OFDM (Profile = 0.1), explaining the improved ranging accuracy with PAM.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** $d=60, 72, 84, 96$ cm.
- **Trend:** There is a trade-off between communication (better with OFDM) and sensing/ranging (better with PAM). HSM allows tuning the system between these two regimes.

Image: _page_3_Figure_4.jpeg
Type: Chart / Multi-pane Graph (Uplink & Ranging)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
- **(a) Uplink BER:** Y: BER [$10^{-5}$ to $10^{-1}$]; X: Uplink Vpp (mV) [150 to 300].
- **(b) Ranging RMSE:** Y: RMSE (cm) [1 to 15]; X: Uplink Vpp (mV) [150 to 300].

**Data Points:**
- **(a):** With a filter, OFDM Uplink BER reaches the **HD-FEC limit (3.8x10⁻³)** at **~180 mV**. Without a filter, it stays above $10^{-2}$.
- **(b):** Ranging RMSE for the "w/ filter" case is highly robust, staying below **4 cm** regardless of Vpp. In the "w/o filter" case, RMSE spikes from **~4 cm to ~15 cm** as Vpp increases beyond 200 mV, due to increased uplink-to-downlink interference.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** α ratios (0% to 100%); w/ filter vs w/o filter.
- **Trend:** Optical filters are essential for isolating the BLED sensing signal from the GLED uplink interference, maintaining cm-level ranging accuracy during full-duplex operation.
