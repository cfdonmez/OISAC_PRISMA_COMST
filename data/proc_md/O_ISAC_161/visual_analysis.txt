## Image: _page_8_Picture_7.jpeg
### Type: Circuit Diagram (Mixer Comparison)

### Structure:
Two architectures compared:
- (a) Double-balanced mixer (nonlinear interference)
- (b) Multiport interferometric receiver (linear interference)

### Key Equations:
- Mixer output: i(t) = 4αi_s V_RF I_1(αV_LO) cos((ω_LO - ω_RF)t) + ...
- Interferometric: p_i = |S_LOi|²|a_LO|² + |S_RFi|²|a_RF|² + ...

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Comparison of mixer-based vs. linear interferometric receivers
- Interferometric: Lower LO power (~20 dB less), broader bandwidth
---

## Image: _page_8_Figure_8.jpeg
### Type: Block Diagram (Receiver Architectures)

### Components:
- Heterodyne receiver: RF → Mixer → IF → ADC
- Interferometric receiver: RF → Hybrid couplers → Power detectors

### Quantitative Comparison:
| Parameter | Heterodyne | Interferometric |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| LO Power | High | Low (~20 dB less) |
| Bandwidth | Moderate | Broadband |
| Dynamic Range | High | Limited |
| Complexity | High | Low (passive) |

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Interferometric ideal for large-scale arrays
- Trade-off between dynamic range and power efficiency
