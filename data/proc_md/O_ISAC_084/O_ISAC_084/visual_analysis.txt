Type: Blockage Sensing Concept Schematic
---
**Flow:**
* **Left Diagram (Conventional):** Ambient light/Communication signals are blocked by human activity. Only the residual "Sensing signal" reaches the photodiode (PD).
* **Right Diagram (Proposed ISAC):** Ambient light and "Sensing & communication signal" are incident. Human activity blocks part of the beam, but the ISAC signal (facilitated by the LCLV array) allows for simultaneous sensing and data retrieval at the PD despite the blockage.

Image: _page_2_Figure_0.jpeg
Type: Signal Processing Flowchart
---
**Flow:**
1. **Data Acquisition:** LED source -> Pulse waveform acquisition.
2. **Signal Decoupling:** Segmented ALS (Ambient Light Sensing), LOWESS (Locally Weighted Scatterplot Smoothing), and Signal correction to isolate sensing and comms.
3. **Sensing Path:** Sensing module -> Data pre-processing -> Data division (Training/Test sets) -> Neural Model (Objective function/Prediction/Truth) -> **Gesture Prediction**.
4. **Communication Path:** Communication module -> High-pass filtering -> Parallel Demodulation (Trend and Threshold) -> Comprehensive decision -> **Multi-channel decoding**.

Type: BER vs Light Intensity Performance Chart
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Light Intensity (log10Lux, 2.0 to 4.5 i.e., 100 to 31.6k Lux).
* **Y-axis:** Bit Error Rate (BER, 0.0 to 0.6).
**Data Points:**
* **Outdoor Conditions (Red line):** BER starts at ~0.5, fluctuates, and settles at ~0.08 at 4.3 log10Lux.
* **Indoor Conditions (Green line):** Starts at ~0.33, drops sharply to 0.03 at 2.3 log10Lux, and stays near zero (<0.01) for higher intensities.

Type: BER vs Distance Performance Chart
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (cm, 0 to 70).
* **Y-axis:** Bit Error Rate (BER, 0.0 to 0.3).
**Data Points:**
* **Constant Intensity (400-1000 Lux):** 
    - At 5-10 cm: BER = 0.
    - At 20 cm: BER = 0.04.
    - At 35 cm: BER peaks at 0.07.
    - At 65 cm: BER = 0.04.
The system maintains BER below the 0.1 threshold across the entire tested distance range.

Image: _page_3_Picture_6.jpeg
Type: Machine Learning Model Architecture (Transformer)
---
**Components:**
* **Input Block:** 4 Channels (Voltage vs Time).
* **Feature Extraction:** Conv 1d + ReLU + BN layers, MaxPooling / AvgPooling.
* **Encoding:** FC Layer, Positional Encoding.
* **Transformer core:** Transformer Encoder comprising Embedding, Multi-Head Attention, Add & Norm, Feed Forward NN.
* **Output:** Classification into 10 distinct Gestures.
