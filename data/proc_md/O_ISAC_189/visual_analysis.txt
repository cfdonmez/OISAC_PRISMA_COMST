## Image: _page_3_Picture_11.jpeg
### Type: Conceptual Diagram (Pulse Compression DAS)

### Theory:
- LFM/FMCW probe (not pulse)
- Matched filter → compressed pulse
- Resolution: ~3 dB bandwidth of autocorrelation
- Bypasses nonlinearity limits (MI, SBS)

### Key Equation:
- r(t) = LFM * h_FUT (convolution)
- s(t) = r(t) ⊗ LFM (correlation)
- p(t) = sinc-like compressed pulse

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Pulse compression enables long-haul DAS
- Continuous LFM compatible with comm

### Type: Conceptual Diagram (Multi-LFM Pilot Sensing)

### Principle:
- 128 LFM pilots at different frequencies
- Matched filter per pilot → DAS trace per frequency
- RBS spectrum reconstructed at each point
- Strain → frequency shift of RBS spectrum

### Key Relationship:

- 1.8 GHz bandwidth → 6 µε max range
