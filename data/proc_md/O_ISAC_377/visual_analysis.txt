   - **Sensing Resolution:** ~10 cm (Dual target), ~2 cm accuracy (Single target).
4. **Tunability:** Verified from 26 to 40 GHz.

Key Innovations:
1. **DC-Offset Optimization:** Balancing the power between the Sensing Component (Pure Chirp) and Comms Component (QPSK noise).
2. **Baseband Generation:** avoiding expensive high-frequency DACs.
