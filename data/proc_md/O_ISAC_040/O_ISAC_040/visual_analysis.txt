### Components Identified:
1.  **UAV Network:** Four Unmanned Aerial Vehicles labeled **UAV 1**, **UAV 2**, **UAV 3**, **UAV 4**.
2.  **Target User:** A human figure labeled "Target" equipped with a Corner Cube Reflector (**CCR**) and a Photodetector (**PD**).
3.  **Transmitter Module (at UAVs):**
    *   **ADT (Active Diffusion Transmitter):** Represented by a small red square.
    *   **RO-ISAC Transceiver:** Block containing the transmission logic.
    *   **Internal Tx Chain:** Input bits -> **OFDM modulation** -> **D/A** (Digital-to-Analog) -> **+ DC** (Bias addition) -> **ADT**.
4.  **Receiver/Sensing Module (at UAVs):**
    *   **PD:** Photodetector (blue oval).
    *   **Internal Rx Chain:** PD -> **A/D** (Analog-to-Digital) -> **Cross correlation** -> **3D positioning**.
5.  **Target Receiver:**
    *   Chain: PD -> **A/D** -> **OFDM demodulation** -> Output bits.
6.  **CCR (Corner Cube Reflector):**
    *   Optical component that reflects signals back to the source.
    *   Dimensions labeled: Aperture diameter **Dc**, lengths **La** and **Lc**.

### Signal Flow Description:
1.  **Downlink Communication & Sensing Probe:** The UAVs (1-4) transmit optical signals (red dashed arrows, "Transmitted signal") via their **ADT**s.
2.  **Communication Path:** The signal reaches the **Target's PD**, passes through A/D conversion and OFDM demodulation to recover "Output bits".
3.  **Reflection Path:** The **CCR** at the Target reflects the signal (blue dashed arrows, "Reflected signal") back toward the UAVs.
4.  **Sensing/Positioning Path:** The reflected signal is captured by the **PD** at each UAV. It is digitized (A/D) and processed via **Cross correlation** to estimate distances (labeled **Distance 1**, **Distance 2**, **Distance 3**, **Distance 4**).
5.  **Position Calculation:** The estimated distances from all four UAVs are fed into a central **3D positioning** block to determine the Target's coordinates.

### Components Identified:
1.  **Center Transmitter (C0):** Orange circular area with radius **r**.
2.  **n-th Side Transmitter (Cn):** Blue oval area with semi-major axis **r'** (implied from width 2r').
3.  **Geometric Parameters:**
    *   **R:** Radius / Distance from center C0 to center Cn.
    *   **Theta_n:** Azimuthal angle of the n-th side transmitter relative to the horizontal axis of C0.
    *   **Epsilon (ε):** Clearance/Gap distance between the outer edge of C0 and the inner edge of Cn.
    *   **Phi (φ):** Tilt/elevation angle of the side transmitter Cn relative to the horizontal plane.

### Structure Description:
*   **View (a) - Top Down:** Shows the spatial arrangement where side transmitters are distributed on a circle of radius **R** around the central C0. The gap **ε** separates the components.
*   **View (b) - Side/Cross-Section:** Illustrates the mounting orientation. The Center transmitter is horizontal. The Side transmitter is tilted at angle **φ**. The radial distance **R** is dimensionally broken down into the Center radius **r**, the gap **ε**, and the Side radius/projection **r'** (labeled as part of width **2r'**).

### Panel (a) Top-Left: Communication BER vs. Tx SNR
*   **X-Axis:** Tx SNR (dB) [Range: 101 - 110]
*   **Y-Axis:** BER (Bit Error Rate) [Log Scale: 10^0 to 10^-4]
*   **Variables/Legend:**
    *   **w/o ADT (Red Square):** Baseline performance.
    *   **w/ ADT, 4 LEDs (Blue Diamond)**
    *   **w/ ADT, 7 LEDs (Black Cross)**
    *   **w/ ADT, 10 LEDs (Magenta Star)**
*   **Extracted Data Points:**
    *   **At 102 dB:** w/o ADT ~2.5x10^-1 | 10 LEDs ~7x10^-2.
    *   **At 106 dB:** w/o ADT ~1.5x10^-1 | 10 LEDs ~8x10^-3.
    *   **At 110 dB:** w/o ADT ~8x10^-2 | 10 LEDs ~1.2x10^-4.
*   **Insight:** "w/ ADT, 10 LEDs" provides the lowest BER. At 110 dB, it offers nearly 3 orders of magnitude improvement over "w/o ADT".

### Panel (b) Top-Right: Positioning RMSE vs. Tx SNR
*   **X-Axis:** Tx SNR (dB) [Range: 111 - 120]
*   **Y-Axis:** RMSE (cm) [Linear Scale: 0 - 20]
*   **Extracted Data Points:**
    *   **At 111 dB:** w/o ADT ~17 cm | 10 LEDs ~6.5 cm.
    *   **At 115 dB:** w/o ADT ~12 cm | 10 LEDs ~2.5 cm.
    *   **At 120 dB:** w/o ADT ~4 cm | 10 LEDs ~1.2 cm.
*   **Insight:** Positioning accuracy improves (RMSE decreases) with SNR. The 10 LED ADT configuration achieves sub-5cm accuracy much earlier (at ~112 dB) compared to w/o ADT.

### Panel (a) Bottom-Left: BER vs. Height of UAV (m)
*   **X-Axis:** Height of UAV 2 and UAV 3 (m) [Range: 1.8 - 2.5]
*   **Y-Axis:** BER [Log Scale: 10^0 to 10^-6]
*   **Extracted Data Points:**
    *   **At 1.8 m:** w/o ADT ~2x10^-2 | 10 LEDs ~1x10^-6.
    *   **At 2.2 m:** w/o ADT ~5x10^-2 | 10 LEDs ~1x10^-5.
    *   **At 2.5 m:** w/o ADT ~8x10^-2 | 10 LEDs ~1.2x10^-4.
*   **Insight:** Performance degrades (BER increases) as UAV height increases. The "10 LEDs" system is significantly more robust, starting at 10^-6 BER at 1.8m.

### Panel (b) Bottom-Right: RMSE vs. Height of UAV (m)
*   **X-Axis:** Height of UAV 2 and UAV 3 (m) [Range: 1.8 - 2.5]
*   **Y-Axis:** RMSE (cm) [Linear Scale: 0 - 10]
*   **Extracted Data Points:**
    *   **At 1.8 m:** w/o ADT ~3.8 cm | 10 LEDs ~0.9 cm.
    *   **At 2.2 m:** w/o ADT ~5.8 cm | 10 LEDs ~3.2 cm.
    *   **At 2.5 m:** w/o ADT ~8.2 cm | 10 LEDs ~6.4 cm.
*   **Insight:** Positioning error increases with height. The performance gap between methods narrows at higher altitudes (2.5m).
