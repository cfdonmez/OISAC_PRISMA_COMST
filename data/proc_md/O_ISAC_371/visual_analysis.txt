Key Technical Findings:
1. **System Type:** Visible Light Communication (VLC) using CDMA.
2. **Code:** Optical Complementary Codes (OCC) used to maintain non-negativity and orthogonality.
3. **Receiver:** Rake Receiver used to harvest Multipath energy.
4. **Performance:** OCC provides superior BER compared to traditional OOC (Optical Orthogonal Codes) due to zero sidelobes.
5. **Combing:** MRC > SC > EGC in low-interference regimes; SC is robust for high user counts (K > 6).

Key Innovations:
1. Application of Non-negative OCC to VLC-CDMA.
2. Derivation of Channel Impulse Response (CIR) for the multi-source indoor scenario.
