## 2. 6G Architecture Vision
- **Fig. 2 (_page_5_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Possible 6G Communication Architecture Scenario.
    - **Infrastructure Layers**:
        - **Ultra-Massive MIMO (UM-MIMO) BS**: Core terrestrial base stations facilitating dense connectivity.
        - **FSO Backhaul/Communication**: High-capacity optical links between sky (planes), sea (ships), and ground hubs.
        - **Satellite & UAV Integration**: Seamless global coverage using Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites and drone-based BS.
    - **Application Scenarios**:
        - **Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) & AI**: Direct brain-to-thing communication integrated with Software Defined Networking (SDN).
        - **Five Senses Transfer**: Remote exchange of sensory data (touch, smell, taste, sight, hearing).
        - **eHealth**: Real-time remote patient monitoring and medical telepresence.
        - **Smart Home & City**: VR/MR immersion, VLC (Visible Light Communication) for high-speed indoor and vehicle-to-everything (V2X) links.
        - **Underwater/Maritime**: Communication between submarines, ships, and aircraft via hybrid FSO/RF.
