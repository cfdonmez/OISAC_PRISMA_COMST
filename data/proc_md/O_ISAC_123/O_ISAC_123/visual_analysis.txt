Image: _page_1_Picture_2.jpeg
Type: System Architectural Block Diagram
---
**Components & Signal Flow:**
1. **Robot 1 ROS (Transmitter):**
   * **Application Nodes:** Source of control/coordination information.
   * **SCC Transmitter Module:**
     * *Bits Encoding* -> *Modulation* -> *Clipping & Padding* -> *Detectors & Patterns* -> *Screen*.
2. **Optical Channel:** Free-space wireless link between the LCD and camera.
3. **Robot 2 ROS (Receiver):**
   * **SCC Receiver Module:**
     * *Camera* -> *Region Detection*.
     * **Sensing Branch:** *Region Detection* -> *Pose Estimation* (Feedback loop to Communications).
     * **Communications Branch:** *Region Detection* -> *Perspective Transformation* -> *Channel Equalization* -> *Demodulation & Decoding* -> *Error Correcting*.
   * **Information (ROS topics):** Final decoded data output.

* **At 0.1 m/s:** Proposed (0.0148m) vs EKF (0.0593m).

* **At 0.6 m/s:** Proposed (0.0773m) vs EKF (0.3110m).
**Conclusion:** The proposed SCC-based sensing reduces braking distance by approx **75%** compared to standard Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) methods across all tested velocities.

**Technical Breakdown:**

* **Sub-figures (b-d):** Convergence of x, y, and theta errors. The proposed method (Blue) shows significantly faster damping and lower overshoot at transient events (e.g., at t=6s and t=38s, corresponding to sudden turns in the leader's path).
