Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Coherent Optical ISAC Transceiver)
---
Components:
- Tunable Laser Source (TLS): Wavelength-tunable CW laser.
- I/Q Modulator: Dual-parallel MZM for complex modulation.
- DAC: Digital-to-analog converter for waveform generation.
- EDFA: Erbium-doped fiber amplifier for power boost.
- Circulator: 3-port device for Tx/Rx separation.
- Fiber Channel: SMF transmission medium.
- Target/User: Reflection point or communication endpoint.
- Coherent Receiver: Dual-polarization 90° hybrid.
- Balanced Photodetectors: I/Q detection with common-mode rejection.
- ADC: High-speed digitizer.
- DSP: Joint radar/communication signal processing.

Flow:
1. TLS generates CW optical carrier at λ = 1550 nm.
2. DAC creates digital ISAC waveform (OFDM with sensing pilots).
3. I/Q Modulator imprints waveform onto optical carrier.
4. EDFA amplifies signal to +20 dBm.
5. Circulator routes signal to Fiber Channel.
6. Signal reaches Target/User; reflections return.
7. Circulator directs received signal to Coherent Receiver.
8. 90° hybrid mixes received signal with LO for coherent detection.
9. Balanced PDs output I/Q photocurrents.
10. ADC digitizes at 64 GSa/s.
11. DSP extracts range, velocity, and demodulates data.

Type: System Diagram / Schematic (OFDM-Based ISAC Frame Structure)
---
Components:
- OFDM Symbol Grid: N_FFT × N_sym resource blocks.
- Preamble: Synchronization and channel estimation.
- Sensing Pilots: Distributed reference signals for ranging.
- Data Subcarriers: Communication payload.
- Guard Band: Spectral protection.
- CP (Cyclic Prefix): ISI mitigation.

Description:
- Subcarrier spacing (SCS): 120 kHz (5G FR2 compatible).
- FFT size: 4096.
- Bandwidth: 491.52 MHz effective.
- Symbol duration: T_sym = 8.33 µs.
- CP duration: T_CP = 2.08 µs (25% overhead).
- Sensing pilot density: Every 12th subcarrier (Δf = 1.44 MHz).
- Frame duration: 10 ms (120 symbols).
