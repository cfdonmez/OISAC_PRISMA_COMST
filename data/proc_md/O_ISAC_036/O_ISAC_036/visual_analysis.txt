Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Coherent LiDAR with Communication)
---
Components:
- Tx Laser: Continuous-wave or frequency-modulated laser source.
- FMCW Modulator: Frequency modulation for ranging.
- Optical Coupler: Splits signal for Tx/Rx paths.
- Tx Optics: Collimating lens or beam expander.
- Target Scene: Objects reflecting optical signal.
- Rx Optics: Collection aperture and focusing optics.
- 90° Hybrid: Coherent mixing of received signal with LO.
- Balanced Photodetectors: I/Q detection with common-mode rejection.
- ADC: High-speed analog-to-digital conversion.
- DSP: Range/velocity extraction and data demodulation.
- Communication Data Input: Data stream for modulation overlay.

Flow:
1. Tx Laser generates optical carrier.
2. FMCW Modulator applies chirp for ranging (+ communication data overlay).
3. Optical Coupler splits signal: Tx path and LO reference.
4. Tx Optics shape and direct beam toward Target Scene.
5. Reflected signal collected by Rx Optics.
6. 90° Hybrid mixes received signal with LO for coherent detection.
7. Balanced Photodetectors output I/Q beat signals.
8. ADC digitizes I/Q channels.
9. DSP extracts range, velocity, and demodulates communication data.

---
Axes:
- X-axis: Range (m). Range: 0 to 200 m.
- Y-axis: Velocity (m/s). Range: -30 to +30 m/s.
- Color Scale: Intensity (dB). Range: -40 to 0 dB (normalized).

Observations:
- Target 1: Range = 45 m, Velocity = +12 m/s (approaching), Intensity = -5 dB.
- Target 2: Range = 85 m, Velocity = -8 m/s (receding), Intensity = -10 dB.
- Target 3: Range = 130 m, Velocity = 0 m/s (stationary), Intensity = -15 dB.
- Background noise floor: ~-38 dB.
- Range resolution (visible): ~1 m.
- Velocity resolution (visible): ~0.5 m/s.

Data Points (Peak Values):
- SNR Target 1: ~33 dB (peak - noise floor).
- SNR Target 2: ~28 dB.
- SNR Target 3: ~23 dB.
- Clear separation between all three targets in range-Doppler space.

Trend:
- Detection capability demonstrated up to 130 m with >20 dB SNR.
- Moving targets clearly distinguishable from stationary objects.

Image: _page_2_Figure_6.jpeg
Type: Chart (BER vs Range with Concurrent Sensing)
---
Axes:
- X-axis: Target Range (m). Range: 0 to 150 m.
- Y-axis: BER. Range: 10^-7 to 10^-1 (log scale).

Legend:
- Communication Only (Blue solid): No sensing overhead.
- ISAC 1 Gbps Data (Red dashed): Low data rate ISAC.
- ISAC 5 Gbps Data (Green dotted): Medium data rate ISAC.
- ISAC 10 Gbps Data (Orange dash-dot): High data rate ISAC.

Data Points:
- At 25 m: Comm = 10^-7; 1G = 10^-6; 5G = 10^-5; 10G = 10^-4.
- At 50 m: Comm = 10^-6; 1G = 10^-5; 5G = 10^-4; 10G = 10^-3.
- At 100 m: Comm = 10^-4; 1G = 10^-3; 5G = 10^-2; 10G = 10^-1.
- At 150 m: Comm = 10^-3; 1G = 10^-2; 5G = 5×10^-2; 10G = floor (error floor).

Trend:
- BER degrades with range due to reduced received power (~1/R² dependence).
- Higher data rates incur ~1 order of magnitude BER penalty per 5 Gbps step.
- Practical range for BER <10^-3: ~100 m @ 1 Gbps; ~50 m @ 10 Gbps.
- Communication-only mode achieves ~50 m additional range vs 10 Gbps ISAC at same BER.
