### Title: 3-Channel Photonic THz-ISAC System Setup

### Panel (a) Setup:
- **Source:** Laser Diode -> PM (Optical Frequency Comb).
- **Modulation:** IQ-Modulator driven by 120 GSa/s AWG.
- **Spectrum:** 3 Channels spaced by 25 GHz (250, 275, 300 GHz).
- **Tx/Rx:** UTC-PD (Photodiode) -> THz Lens -> Air (0.5m) -> Schottky Mixer.

### Panel (b) Principle (CFP):
- **Coherent Fusion Processing:** Fusing 3 narrow bands to synthesize a wide bandwidth.
- **Result:** Virtual Bandwidth = 3x Single Bandwidth -> High Resolution (c/2B_eff).
