### Components Identified
1.  **Transmitter Block (Collocated ISAC TX & Sens. Rx):**
    *   **Source:** LED/Light Source symbol.
    *   **Receiver Array:** Vertical array of 4 photodetectors/lenses.
    *   **Adder:** Adds Noise signal $Z_s$ to the array output $\mathbf{Y}_s$.
2.  **Use Case Elements:**
    *   **Target:** Solid circle at distance $r_s$.
    *   **Comm. Rx:** Receiver unit (Camera/Lens icon) at distance $r_c$, producing output $Y_c$ after adding noise $Z_c$.
3.  **Signal Flow:**
    *   Optical signal radiates from TX.
    *   **Sensing Path:** Reflects off Target ($r_s$) and returns to the Collocated Rx ($\Delta r_s$ indicated for array spacing).
    *   **Comm Path:** Travels directly to Comm. Rx ($r_c$).
    *   **Angles:** $\phi_s, \psi_s$ (Sensing angles), $\phi_c, \psi_c$ (Comm angles).

Type: Performance Plot (Variance)

### Chart Details
*   **X-Axis:** Transmit Signal ($x$) [Linear: 0 to 30].
*   **Y-Axis:** Variance $\text{Var}(\hat{R}_s | X=x)$ [$m^2$] [Log Scale: $10^{-6}$ to $10^{-1}$].
*   **Series:**
    *   **Blue Square (Solid):** MLE, $\lambda=0.5$.
    *   **Red Star (Solid):** MAP, $\lambda=0.5$.
    *   **Black Circle (Solid):** BCRB, $\lambda=0.5$.
    *   **Blue/Red/Black (Dashed):** Corresponding methods with $\lambda=0.3$.
*   **Data Points:**
    *   **At x=0:** All curves start high (~$0.1$ to $0.5$).
    *   **At x=15:**
        *   MLE (0.5) $\approx 4 \times 10^{-3}$.
        *   MAP (0.5) $\approx 7 \times 10^{-4}$.
        *   BCRB (0.5) $\approx 5 \times 10^{-4}$.
        *   MLE (0.3) $\approx 8 \times 10^{-5}$.
    *   **At x=30:**
        *   MLE (0.5) $\approx 1.5 \times 10^{-3}$.
        *   MAP (0.5) $\approx 3 \times 10^{-4}$.
        *   BCRB (0.5) $\approx 2 \times 10^{-4}$.
        *   Dashed curves ($\lambda=0.3$) are $\approx 10^{-5}$.
*   **Trend:** Variance decreases with increasing transmit signal. Lower $\lambda$ (0.3) yields significantly lower variance (better performance). MAP outperforms MLE and approaches BCRB.

### Chart Details
*   **X-Axis:** Transmit Signal ($x$) [0 to 30].
*   **Y-Axis:** Probability $P(X \le x)$ [0 to 1].
*   **Series:**
    *   **Solid Lines (Sens. Opt. / Com. Opt.):**
        *   **Com. Opt. - CF (Green):** Rises smoothly, reaches 1.0 at $x \approx 15$.
        *   **Sens. Opt. - BCRB (Black):** Step-like jump at $x \approx 10$.
        *   **Sens. Opt. - MLE (Blue):** Step-like jump at $x \approx 10$.
        *   **Sens. Opt. - MAP (Red):** Step-like jump at $x \approx 10$.
    *   **Dashed Lines (ISAC):**
        *   Rise more gradually, reaching 1.0 at $x \approx 30$.
*   **Key Insight:** Sensing-optimized strategies allocate power in a binary/step-like fashion (likely on/off or threshold-based), while Communication-optimized strategies use a continuous distribution.

Type: Capacity vs Distortion Trade-off
---
### Chart Details
*   **X-Axis:** Distortion (MSE) / BCRB [$m^2$] [Log Scale: $10^{-5}$ to $10^{-2}$].
*   **Y-Axis:** Capacity [bps/Hz] [0 to 6].
*   **Bounds (Dashed Horizontal Lines):**
    *   **Com. Opt. (UB):** $\approx 4.8$ bps/Hz (Cyan).
    *   **Com. Opt. (BAA):** $\approx 2.8$ bps/Hz (Red).
    *   **Com. Opt. (LB):** $\approx 1.35$ bps/Hz (Magenta).
*   **Curves (ISAC Schemes):**
    *   **BCRB (Black), MAP (Red), MLE (Blue), CF-BCRB (Green).**
    *   **Low Distortion ($< 2 \times 10^{-5}$):** Capacity drops sharply towards 0.
    *   **High Distortion ($> 10^{-4}$):** Capacity saturates around the **Com. Opt. (BAA)** level ($\approx 2.8$ bps/Hz).
*   **Specific Points:**
    *   **Sens. Opt. Points (Squares/Circles):** Located at very low distortion ($\approx 10^{-5}$) but very low capacity ($\approx 0.2 - 1.0$ bps/Hz).
*   **Conclusion:** There is a fundamental trade-off. Achieving the lowest sensing distortion requires sacrificing capacity. The proposed ISAC schemes converge to the BAA capacity bound at moderate distortion levels.
