Type: Experimental Setup (Recirculating Loop)
---
**Components:**
* **(i) Transmitter DSP:** PRBS Generation -> QPSK Mapping -> Upsampling -> RRC Shaping -> Resampling.
* **(ii) Receiver DSP:** Clock Data Recovery -> CD Compensation -> CMA Equalizer -> Freq. Compensation -> DD-LMS.
* **Loop-1 & Loop-2:** Dual-loop recirculating setup with 51.17 km and 5.0 km SMF spans.
* **HLLB 1 & 2:** Integration points for sensing and transmission.
* **Pulse Generator:** Controls AOMs (Loading, Loop, Sensing).
* **(iii) Waveform:** Timing diagram for Loading/Loop AOMs and Sensing AOM relative to the Trigger pulse.

Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Performance Results (Sensing & Transmission)
---
**Panels:** 
* **(a) Rayleigh Trace vs Distance (km):** 0 to 15 km. Comparison of different attenuation levels (w/o, 3dB, 5dB, 10dB). 10dB attenuation (purple) effectively kills the signal after 5 km.
* **(b) SNR after 10 loops vs Launch Power (dBm):** Range 0 to -5 dBm. Peak SNR (~15 dB) is achieved at -2 dBm for both Loop-1 and Loop-2. 
* **Constellation Inset:** 4-point QPSK constellation with clear cluster separation.
* **(c) BER/SNR vs Transmission Distance (km):** 2500 to 4000 km. 15% SD-FEC threshold is at BER $\approx 2 \times 10^{-2}$. Distance limit is ~3750 km (red star).
