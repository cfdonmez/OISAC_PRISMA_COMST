Type: System Diagram / Principle Model
---
**Components:**
- **Transmitted Signal:** Represented by a solid blue line with a negative frequency slope (down-chirp).
- **Received Signal:** Represented by a dotted blue line, delayed by time τ relative to the transmitted signal.
- **Beat Frequency ($f_b$):** The constant frequency difference between the transmitted and received chirps.
- **Time (τ):** Delay representing the round-trip flight time to the target.

**Signal Flow:**
1. A linear frequency-modulated (FMCW) chirp is generated and transmitted.
2. The signal reflects off a target and is received after a delay τ.
3. Mixing (heterodyning) the transmitted and received signals produces a beat signal with a constant frequency $f_b$.
4. Bottom graph shows the **Beat Frequency** ($f_b$) remains constant over the sweep period, enabling range estimation via $R = (cT/2nB)f_b$.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **Transmitter (Tx):** Includes Bits $\rightarrow$ S/P $\rightarrow$ QAM map $\rightarrow$ Subcarrier modulation $\rightarrow$ IDFnT $\rightarrow$ P/S $\rightarrow$ Up Conversion $\rightarrow$ MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator).
- **Optical Source:** Laser connected to a 1:1 Optical Coupler (OC).
- **Amplification:** EDFA (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier).
- **Routing:** Circulator for separating transmitted and reflected (echo) signals.
- **Channel:** FSO Channel over distance R.
- **Target End-user:** PD (Photodetector) $\rightarrow$ Down Conversion $\rightarrow$ S/P $\rightarrow$ DFnT $\rightarrow$ Subcarrier demodulation $\rightarrow$ QAM demap $\rightarrow$ P/S $\rightarrow$ Bits.
- **FWCM Ranging Receiver:** Optical Coupler (mixing echo with local reference) $\rightarrow$ PD $\rightarrow$ Frequency Detector $\rightarrow$ Range (R).

**Signal Flow:**
1. Data bits are modulated into an OCDM baseband signal via IDFnT.
2. The RF signal drives an MZM to modulate a CW laser carrier.
3. Signal is amplified by EDFA, split by OC (one part to ranging mixer), and sent through the FSO channel via a circulator.
4. The reflected echo returns through the circulator and is mixed with the local reference for sensing.
5. The forward signal is coherently detected and demodulated by the target end-user using a standard OCDM receiver chain.

Image: _page_5_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Experimental Setup (Architecture)
---
**Components:**
- **Transmitter Section:** Computer $\rightarrow$ AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator) $\rightarrow$ RF Driver $\rightarrow$ MZM.
- **Optical Section:** Laser (1550 nm) $\rightarrow$ MZM $\rightarrow$ EDFA $\rightarrow$ 1:1 Optical Coupler.
- **Medium/Delay:** Fiber Delay Line (mimicking FSO propagation) $\rightarrow$ OC nodes.
- **Target End-user Section:** Optical hybrid/mix $\rightarrow$ Local Laser $\rightarrow$ PD $\rightarrow$ Oscilloscope $\rightarrow$ Computer.
- **FWCM Ranging Receiver Section:** Optical mixing stage $\rightarrow$ PD $\rightarrow$ Oscilloscope $\rightarrow$ Computer.

**Signal Flow:**
1. AWG generates the OCDM-FMCW waveform (1024 chirps, 1 GHz BW).
2. Pure laser carrier is modulated by MZM (null-biased for CS-ODSB), amplified by EDFA.
3. Signal is split; one path travels through Fiber Delay Line (3m to 20m tests).
4. Receiver capture occurs at both the ranging head (echo) and the user terminal (forward signal) via high-speed Oscilloscopes.
