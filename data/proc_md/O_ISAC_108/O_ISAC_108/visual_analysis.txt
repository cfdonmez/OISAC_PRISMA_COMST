Image: _page_5_Figure_3.jpeg
Type: Performance Comparison Charts
---
**Axes:** X: Eb/N0 (dB) [-20 to 80], Y: BER/MSE (Log).
**Panels:**
* (a) **BER Performance:**
    * Separated system (BPSK-OFDM): Drops below $10^{-4}$ at ~75 dB.
    * Directionless O-ISAC: Similar to separated, drops at ~73 dB.
    * Directional O-ISAC (16QAM-OFDM): Drops below $10^{-4}$ at ~13 dB.
    * **Gain:** Directional ISAC achieves a 63.35 dB gain over directionless at $10^{-4}$ BER.
* (b) **MSE Performance:**
    * Separated system: $10^{-4}$ MSE at ~58 dB.
    * Directionless O-ISAC: $10^{-4}$ MSE at ~55 dB.
    * Directional O-ISAC: $10^{-4}$ MSE at ~18 dB.
    * **Gain:** 40.42 dB improvement with directional approach; additional 3.03 dB gain over separated system.
