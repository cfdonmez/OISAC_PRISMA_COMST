# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_011

## Figure 1: RO-ISAC System Architecture
**File:** [_page_0_Figure_15.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_011/O_ISAC_011/_page_0_Figure_15.jpeg)
- **Type:** System Diagram
- **Description:** Illustrates a Retroreflective Optical ISAC (RO-ISAC) system with 4 transceivers at the ceiling and a receiver with a Corner Cube Reflector (CCR).
- **Signal Flow:** 
  - **Downlink:** Transceiver 2 (example) performs OFDM modulation -> D/A -> DC bias -> LED/LD.
  - **Sensing:** Signal reflects from CCR in the receiver -> PD in Transceiver -> A/D -> Cross-correlation with transmitted bits to estimate distance.
  - **Communication:** Receiver PD -> A/D -> OFDM Demodulation -> Output bits.
- **Components:** OFDM mod/demod, DAC/ADC, LED/LD, PD, CCR (geometry: $L_x \times L_y \times D_r$).

## Figure 4: BER Performance vs. Transmitted SNR
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_21.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_011/O_ISAC_011/_page_2_Figure_21.jpeg)
- **Trends:**
  - **M=2 (BPSK):** BER $\sim 10^{-7}$ at **106 dB** SNR.
  - **M=4 (QPSK):** BER $\sim 10^{-7}$ at **109 dB** SNR.
  - **M=8 (8-QAM):** BER $\sim 10^{-7}$ at **114 dB** SNR.
  - **M=16 (16-QAM):** BER $\sim 10^{-7}$ at **116 dB** SNR.
- **Insight:** 3 dB penalty between BPSK and QPSK; larger gap for higher orders.

## Figure 5: Distance RMSE vs. Symbol Count
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_1.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_011/O_ISAC_011/_page_3_Figure_1.jpeg)
- **Data (Range Resolution = 1cm):**
  - **10 Symbols:** RMSE $\approx 6.8$ cm.
  - **16 Symbols:** RMSE $\approx 1.0$ cm.
  - **20 Symbols:** RMSE $\approx 0.6$ cm.
- **Insight:** Modulation order $M$ has no impact on ranging accuracy; precision improves sharply with averaging over more symbols.

## Figure 6: Distance RMSE vs. SNR (Different Symbol Lengths)

## Figure 8: Spatial RMSE Distribution (Tx at 2.5m Height)
