Type: Neural Network Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Input Layer:** Input features x1, x2, ... x_n[0] and feedback delays d1, d2, ... dk. Weights w[1] and w^d connect to the hidden layer.
* **Hidden Layer:** Neurons h1, h2, ... h_n[1] with activation function f[1](.) and bias b[1]. Delay elements z^-1, z^-2, ... z^-k feedback the output layer signal.
* **Output Layer:** Output neuron y with activation function f[2](.) and bias b[2]. 
* **Feedback Mechanism:** A switch selects between "Label" (training) and "Decision" (application). Output y(n) is used to calculate "Improved Weight" gamma_n.
**Flow:**
The diagram illustrates an Autoregressive Recurrent Neural Network (AR-RNN) with a weighting mechanism. Inputs are processed through a hidden layer, and previous outputs are fed back through delay units (z^-k) to the input and hidden layers to compensate for non-linear impairments in a time-sequenced signal.

---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** N_train, Range: 5000 to 50000.
* **Y-axis:** BER, Range: 10^-3 to 10^-1.
**Data Points:**
* **WD-RNN (blue diamonds):** Reaches steady state (~5x10^-4) at N_train = 30000.
* **AR-RNN (red triangles):** Saturates at ~0.9x10^-3 after 30000 samples.
* **FNN (green circles):** Saturates at ~0.9x10^-2, well above the 7% HD-FEC threshold.
**Trends:** WD-RNN (Weighted Delay RNN) provides the fastest convergence and best asymptotic performance.
