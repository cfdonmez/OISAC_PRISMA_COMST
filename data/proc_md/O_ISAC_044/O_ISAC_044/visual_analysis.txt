Type: System Schematic
---
### Components Identified
1.  **Central Office (Tx Side):**
    *   **AWG:** Arbitrary Waveform Generator.
    *   **ECL-1:** External Cavity Laser.
    *   **MZM-1:** Mach-Zehnder Modulator.
    *   **VOA:** Variable Optical Attenuator.
    *   **EDFA -> OF -> PD -> ADC:** Feedback/Monitoring loop at point A?
2.  **Optical Link:** 10km Fiber.
3.  **BS/RRU (Remote Node):**
    *   **Circulator:** 3-port.
    *   **Local Oscillator Branch:** **ECL-2** -> **Coupler** -> **PD**.
    *   **Modulation Loop:** **MZM-2**.
    *   **Amplification:** **PM-EDFA** (Polarization Maintaining EDFA).
    *   **Wireless Tx:** **PD** -> **PA** (Power Amp) -> **Antenna**.
    *   **Wireless Rx:** **Antenna** -> **LNA** (Low Noise Amp) -> **ED** (Envelope Detector?) -> **MZM-2**.
4.  **Target:** Triangle at 1m wireless distance.
5.  **UE (User Equipment) / Rx:**
    *   **Antenna** -> **LNA** -> **ED** -> **EA** -> **ADC & DSP**.

### Signal Flow
*   Optical signal generated at CO is transmitted to BS/RRU.
*   At BS, it is converted to wireless (PD -> Antenna) and radiates to Target.
*   Echo from Target is received, amplified, and re-modulated onto the optical carrier via MZM-2 (Radio-over-Fiber loopback?).
*   Simultaneously, UEs receive the wireless signal.
