# O_ISAC_149 Visual Analysis

## 1. Flight Test Hardware
- **Fig. 1 (_page_4_Picture_0.jpeg)**: Research External Vision Device (REVD) on F-104.
    - **Visuals**: A high-performance F-104 jet with a specialized periscope flaring in the back seat area.
    - **Context**: Used to evaluate landing performance with restricted external vision.
- **Fig. 2 (_page_4_Picture_2.jpeg)**: Precision Automated Tracking System (PATS).
    - **Visuals**: A towable trailer housing the laser tracking mount.
    - **Specs**: Operates at 1.064 microns, 1 MW peak power, 100 PPS. Uses 18-bit shaft angle encoders.
- **Fig. 3 (_page_4_Picture_3.jpeg)**: Retroreflector Arrays.
    - **Visuals**: Three arrays (two side, one forward) mounted on the REVD flaring.
    - **Context**: Passive optical devices returning >90% of incident laser energy to the PATS tracker for automatic space position acquisition.

## 2. Acquisition Aids & Control
- **Fig. 4 (_page_4_Picture_5.jpeg & _page_5_Figure_0.jpeg upper left)**: Manual Optical Acquisition Aid.
    - **Visuals**: A portable mount with a 7x optical telescope and 10-bit synchro-to-digital readouts.
    - **Context**: Used to slave the PATS tracker to the aircraft when the boresight camera field of view (15 deg) is insufficient.
- **Fig. 6 (_page_5_Figure_0.jpeg lower left)**: Digital Parameter and Plot Displays.
    - **Visuals**: Workstation monitors showing real-time telemetry, x-y/x-z trajectory plots, and 3D pictorials generated by Silicon Graphics/Sun systems.

## 3. Mission Deployment & Site Geometry
- **Fig. 7 (_page_5_Figure_0.jpeg right)**: Dryden Runway Map.
    - **Visuals**: Map of Edwards Air Force Base / NASA Dryden.
    - **Site Layout**: Laser site is located ~2000 ft south of the coordinate origin (approach end of runway 22) and 1000 ft west.
    - **Safety**: Shows the "300 ft Radius" and "Eye Hazard Area" related to Class IV laser operation.
    - **Reference**: A 15000 ft long runway (Runway 22) is the primary landing corridor.
