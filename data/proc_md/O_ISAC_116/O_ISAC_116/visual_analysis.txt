Type: Chart (SNR and Bit Allocation)
---
**Axes:** X: Subcarrier Index [0 to 500], Y: SNR (dB) [left, 5 to 25] and Bit Allocation (bit/sym) [right, 0 to 7].

Type: Chart (SNR vs Time with Smoke)
---
**Axes:** X: Time Lapse (min) [0 to 90], Y: SNR (dB) [5 to 12].
**Observations:**
* **No Smoke (red dashed):** Constant baseline at ~5.1 dB.
* **With Smoke (blue circles):** SNR starts high (~11.3 dB) due to added scattering paths providing more signal to the NLOS receiver, then gradually decays to ~8.8 dB as smoke absorbs more light over 90 minutes.
