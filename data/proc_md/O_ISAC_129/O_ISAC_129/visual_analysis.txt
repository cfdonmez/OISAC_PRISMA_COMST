Type: VehicleTalk Framework Overview
---
**Components & Links:**
* **Vehicle B (Leader):** Uses White LED (Blue arrows / VL Link) to transmit to Vehicle A.
* **Vehicle A (Follower):** Uses IR LED (Red arrows / IR Link) to transmit to Vehicle B.
* **Interference:** Non-Platooning Vehicle C can break (X) the link.
* **System Logic:** Sensing -> VL Signals -> PD Receiver -> LED Driver / Message Generation -> Message Reception.

Type: V2V Status Awareness Scenarios
---
**Schematics:**
* **(a) Emergency Braking:** Following vehicle (A) detects rapid approach of leader (B).
* **(b) Turning:** Relative angles change, causing link breaks (X) on path segments.
* **(c) Cutting-in:** A third vehicle (C) occupies the safety distance, blocking (X) the LoS link.

Type: Channel Properties
---
** (a) Distance Impact:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (m) | Range: **1 to 5 m**.
* **Y-axis:** Channel Property (Error rate).
* **Trend:** PER (orange) stays near zero until **3 m**, then rises steeply to **0.6** at 5 m. BER stays below 0.1.
** (b) Angle Impact:**
* **X-axis:** Angle (degree) | Range: **10 to 50**.
* **Trend:** Stable performance until **30 degrees**, after which PER rises to ~0.15.

Type: Goodput Comparison
---
**Charts:** (a) k=50 bits, (b) k=100 bits.
**X-axis:** Distance (m) | Range: **1 to 5 m**.

**Numerical Data:**

---
**Box Plot:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (m) | Range: **2 to 6 m**.
