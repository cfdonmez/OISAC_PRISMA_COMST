IMAGE: _page_0_Figure_15.jpeg
TYPE: System Diagram
DESCRIPTION: Schematic of the experimental setup for integrated sensing and telecommunication based on a tapered few-mode fiber (FMF).
COMPONENTS:
- SC (Supercontinuum): Broadband source for sensing.
- CW (Continuous Wave): Laser for communication.
- DP-IQ-MOD: Dual-polarization IQ modulator.
- AWG: Arbitrary Waveform Generator.
- MUX: Mode multiplexer (LP01, LP11).
- Tapered FMF2: Sensing area where evanescent field interacts with the environment (depicted with a water droplet).
- SMF: Single-mode fiber connections.
- OSA: Optical Spectrum Analyzer for sensing data extraction.
- Coherent Receiver + RTOS: For communication signal demodulation.
