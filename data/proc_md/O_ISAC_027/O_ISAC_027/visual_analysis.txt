Type: System Diagram / Schematic (DAS System Overview)
---
Components:
- Laser Source: Narrow-linewidth CW laser for coherent detection.
- AOM (Acousto-Optic Modulator): Creates pulsed probe signal.
- EDFA (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier): Amplifies probe pulses.
- Circulator: Routes transmitted and backscattered signals.
- Sensing Fiber: Standard SMF deployed along the monitored structure.
- Coherent Receiver: Mixes backscattered signal with local oscillator.
- ADC: High-speed digitizer for signal capture.
- DSP Unit: Processes phase/amplitude data for vibration sensing.

Flow:
1. Laser Source generates CW light split into probe and LO paths.
2. AOM pulses the probe beam with defined repetition rate.
3. EDFA amplifies pulses to required power level.
4. Circulator directs pulses into the Sensing Fiber.
5. Rayleigh backscattered light returns through Circulator.
6. Coherent Receiver beats backscatter with LO.
7. ADC digitizes the mixed signal.
8. DSP extracts distributed phase changes indicating vibration/strain.

Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Experimental Setup - DAS + DWDM)
---
Components:
- ECL (External Cavity Laser): 1550.12 nm, 100 kHz linewidth.
- IQ Modulator: Generates chirped probe pulses.
- AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator): Drives IQ modulator with LFM waveform.
- EDFA1: Boosts probe power to +20 dBm.
- BPF (Bandpass Filter): Removes ASE noise.
- Circulator (C1): Port 1→2 for probe, Port 2→3 for backscatter.
- SSMF (Standard Single Mode Fiber): 50 km sensing fiber.
- VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator): Controls received power.
- EDFA2: Preamplifier for coherent receiver.
- 90° Optical Hybrid: Combines signal and LO for I/Q detection.
- BPD (Balanced Photodetectors): 40 GHz bandwidth.
- RTO (Real-Time Oscilloscope): 80 GSa/s capture.
- DWDM Channels: 8 × 100 Gbps DP-QPSK channels co-propagating.

Flow:
1. ECL outputs CW at 1550.12 nm, split 90:10 for probe:LO.
2. IQ Modulator imprints 800 MHz LFM chirp (10 µs duration).
3. EDFA1 amplifies to +20 dBm, filtered by BPF.
4. Circulator routes probe into 50 km SSMF.
5. Backscatter returns through Circulator to VOA → EDFA2.
6. 90° Hybrid mixes with LO; BPDs detect I/Q components.
7. RTO captures at 80 GSa/s for offline DSP.
8. DWDM channels (1550.92-1557.36 nm, 100 GHz spacing) co-propagate for communication.
