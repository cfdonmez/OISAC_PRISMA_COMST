IMAGE: _page_1_Picture_8.jpeg
TYPE: System Diagram
DESCRIPTION: Schematic of a UAV-assisted optical integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) network.
COMPONENTS:
- FSO Gateway: Central backhaul node (top right).
- Foggy FSO Channel: Wireless optical link between Gateway and UAV.
- UAV: Airborne node performing joint relaying and sensing.
- Ground BS (Base Station): Central tower managing a circular service area (Radius R).
- Users: Distributed at edge regions between inner radius R_in and outer radius R_o.
- Sensing: Backscattered light (red arrows) used for object detection/tracking.
