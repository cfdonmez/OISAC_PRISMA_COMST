Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **RO-ISAC transceiver:** MLS generation, OFDM mod., Power allocation, D/A, DC bias, LED/LD (Tx), PD (Rx), A/D, Up-sampling, Cross correlation.
* **RO-ISAC receiver:** PD, A/D, Sync, De-meaning, OFDM demod.
* **Sensing Target:** Corner Cube Reflector (CCR) with parameters Ls (shield length), L (reflector depth), and Dr (diameter).
**Flow:**
1. **Transmitter:** Input bits are OFDM-modulated and PPC (Physical Parameter Control) bits generate a Maximum Length Sequence (MLS).
2. **Integration:** Power allocation integrates OFDM and MLS into a hybrid waveform. After D/A conversion and DC biasing, the signal is transmitted via LED/LD.
3. **Communication Rx:** The "Transmitted signal" is received by the RO-ISAC receiver's PD, digitized, synchronized, and demodulated to recover output bits.
4. **Sensing Rx:** The "Reflected signal" from the CCR is captured by the transceiver's Sensing PD, digitized, and cross-correlated with the up-sampled reference hybrid signal to estimate distance.

Type: Experimental Setup Diagram and Photo

**Section (a) - Block Diagram:**
* **Components:** AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator), BLED module (Blue LED), PD module (Photodiode), DSO ports 1 & 2 (Digital Storage Oscilloscope), CCR (Corner Cube Reflector), APD (Avalanche Photodiode).
* **Flow:** Tx signal from AWG drives the BLED. Transmitted light is split: one path goes to the PD for direct monitoring (Rx signal 1 on DSO port 1). The other path reflects off the CCR and is detected by the APD (Rx signal 2 on DSO port 2).
**Section (b) - Setup Photo:**
* Lists: DC supply (two units), AWG, PD module, DSO, BLED module, CCR, APD.
* **Observation:** Lab-bench optical wireless communication (OWC) setup with professional-grade test and measurement equipment.

Type: Sensing Performance Charts (RMSE)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (cm), Range: 48, 72, 96, 120 cm.
* **Y-axis:** RMSE (cm), Range: 0 to 8 cm (a), 0.5 to 2 cm (b).
**Data Points:**
* **Plot (a):** At 120cm, OFDM-only RMSE is ~8cm, while MLS-only (black) is ~5.8cm.
* **Plot (b):** At 120cm, β=10 RMSE is ~1.6cm, compared to β=1 which is ~1.8cm.
**Trends:** Ranging RMSE consistently increases with distance. Increasing the power ratio β (more sensing pulse power) results in a proportional decrease in ranging error (RMSE).

Type: Communication Performance Charts (BER)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (cm), Range: 48, 72, 96, 120 cm.
* **Y-axis:** BER, Range: 10^-4 to 10^-1.
**Data Points:**
* **Plot (a):** At 72cm, β=1 BER is ~10^-3. β=10 BER is ~2x10^-2.
* **Plot (b):** At 96cm, OFDM-only BER is ~1.5x10^-3.
**Trends:** There is a performance trade-off compared to Figure 4. As β increases, the BER degrades significantly. The "3.8x10^-3" line (FEC threshold) indicates that at 120cm, only low β values (e.g., β=1, 2) or pure OFDM maintain communications below threshold.
