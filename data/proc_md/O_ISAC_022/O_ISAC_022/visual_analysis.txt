# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_022

## Figure 1: Joint VLC Sensing and Communication Architecture
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Geometry:** 4m × 4m room with 9 Access Points (APs) on ceiling ($z=2.5$m). User Equipment (UE) plane at $z=0.85$m (vertical link distance $h=1.65$m).
- **Transmitter (AP):** $m$-CAP modulation -> Rescaling to fit LED linear range -> DC Bias ($I_b$) addition -> LED Driver.
- **Receiver (UE):** Photodiode (PD) -> (1) Removal of DC for data recovery ($m$-CAP filtering/demapping) and (2) RSS algorithm using variance of electrical signals.

## Figure 2: Medium Access Control (MAC) Cycle
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Phases:** 
  1. **Common Preamble:** All 9 APs transmit simultaneously for robust synchronization.
  2. **Sequential Location Frames:** AP1 through AP9 transmit coordinates and radiated power $\Phi_{tx,i}^r$ in dedicated time slots to avoid mutual interference during RSS measurement.
  3. **Data Payload:** Standard communication phase.
- **Strategy:** Decouples sensing/positioning from data payload to ensure high-accuracy trilateration.

## Figure 7: Signal Standard Deviation and Scaling
**File:** [_page_8_Figure_9.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_8_Figure_9.jpeg)
- **Original $\hat{I}_i(t)$:** Scales with QAM order $M$; ~4A at $M=16$ up to ~17A at $M=256$.
- **Drive $I_{tx,i}(t)$:** Constant at **~2.82A** regardless of $M$ or roll-off $\alpha$.
- **AC-only component:** Constant at **~0.32A**.
- **Conclusion:** LED linear region constraints (400mA - 1A per chip) normalize total power, making positioning accuracy independent of modulation order.

## Figure 8 & 9: Bit Error Rate (BER) Analysis
**File:** [_page_9_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_9_Figure_2.jpeg) & [_page_9_Figure_8.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_9_Figure_8.jpeg)
- **FEC Limit:** Horizontal line at $3.8 \times 10^{-3}$ (HD-FEC overhead).
- **Trends:** 
  - Wider roll-off $\alpha$ (smoother filters) reduces non-linear clipping distortion, lowering BER. 
  - Increasing sub-bands $m$ from 4 to 8 mitigates LED low-pass attenuation effects.
- **Complexity Trade-off:** $L_{SPAN}=10$ provides a balance between ISI suppression and hardware footprint.

## Figure 10: Connectivity and Sensing Coverage
**File:** [_page_10_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_022/O_ISAC_022/_page_10_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) $M=64$:** Uniform coverage over the 16 $m^2$ floor; central zones allow simultaneous sensing from 4 APs.
- **(b) $M=256$:** Higher data rate (32 Mbps) causes shrunken coverage footprints; sensing fails in dead zones where fewer than 4 AP signals meet the BER target.
- **Interdependence:** Higher communication throughput directly compromises sensing availability in RSS-based VLC.
