Type: System Diagram / Schematic

**Components:**
- **Electrical processing:** Baseband signal s(t), Digital precoding (x(t)=Ws(t)).
- **Optical processing:** Laser source, SOA (Semiconductor Optical Amplifier), Splitter network.
- **Transmitter/Edge:** SOA, EOPM (Electro-optic Phase Modulator), Edge emitters.
- **Sensing/Comm Environment:** Optical beam, Comm. UE 1 ... Comm. UE K, Scanning, Sensing targets (Car, Tree, Cone).

**Flow:**
The baseband signal s(t) undergoes Digital precoding to produce precoded signals x_1(t) through x_M(t). Simultaneously, a Laser source passes through an SOA and a Splitter network to distribute the optical carrier. For each channel (m), the precoded electrical signal x_m(t) drives an EOPM to modulate the optical signal (which may also be amplified by an SOA). The modulated optical signals A_t x_m(t) are emitted by the Edge emitters (array with spacing d). The emitted Optical beams perform Scanning to detect Sensing targets and provide communication to UEs.
