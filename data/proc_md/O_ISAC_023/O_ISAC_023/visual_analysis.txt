# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_023

## Figure 1: DCO-OFDM FSO-ISAC System Model
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_023/O_ISAC_023/_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Architecture:** 
  - **Transceiver:** Tx bits -> m-QAM -> Hermitian symmetry -> IDFT/CP -> DAC -> DC bias/Clipping -> Laser Diode -> LiDAR.
  - **Sensing Path:** Backscattered light (Uncooperative/Cooperative targets) -> Photodiode -> ADC -> Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) for Target Distance.
  - **Communication Path:** LoS link to Cooperative Target -> Photodiode -> ADC -> DFT -> Equalization -> Demodulation -> Rx bits.
- **Key Insight:** Common waveform (DCO-OFDM) facilitates dual-service; clipping noise is handled via the Bussgang theorem and equivalent frequency-selective channel modeling.

## Figure 2: Original Signal vs. Clipping Noise PSD
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_023/O_ISAC_023/_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) Even Power Allocation:** Original signal PSD is flat (~3x10^-3). Clipping noise PSD is also white (~2.5x10^-4).
- **(b) Uneven Power Allocation:** Original signal PSD increases with subcarrier index k. Clipping noise PSD becomes **colored (ACGN)**, decreasing with k.
- **Physics:** Non-linear clipping elicits frequency-selective distortion even in flat channels, necessitating colored noise modeling for Accurate ISAC metrics.

## Figure 3: Iterative Algorithm Convergence
**File:** [_page_7_Figure_14.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_023/O_ISAC_023/_page_7_Figure_14.jpeg)
- **Type:** 2D Parameter Space Sketch ($\mu$ vs $\eta$).
- **Trajectories:** Shows zigzag convergence of dual variables $(\mu^{(j)}, \eta^{(j)})$ towards the optimal intersection $(\mu_c^*, \eta_c^*)$ defined by $S_1$ (rate) and $S_2$ (sensing) constraints.
- **Constraint:** Trajectories are bounded by $\mu = \gamma_s(l_2)l_2^2\eta$ (lower) and $\mu = \gamma_s(l_1)l_1^2\eta + \gamma_c(l_1)$ (upper), ensuring stability.

## Figure 6: Subcarrier Power Allocation
**File:** [_page_10_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_023/O_ISAC_023/_page_10_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Behaviors:** 
  - **Optimal Comm:** Flat/uniform distribution to maximize Shannon capacity.
  - **Optimal Sensing:** Pure high-pass; all power pushed to maximum $k$ to maximize Fisher Information (frequency-weighting $k^2$).
  - **Tradeoff (CC/SC):** Curves transition smoothly. As $c/2\varsigma_0$ decreases (sharper ranging), power shifts dramatically to the right margin of the subcarrier index.

## Figure 7: Spectral Efficiency vs. Sensing Precision Tradeoff
**File:** [_page_11_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/proc_markdowns/O_ISAC_023/O_ISAC_023/_page_11_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Frontiers:** Pareto optimization curves for $N=128, 256, 512$.
- **Saturnality:** 
  - Right Margin: Spectral efficiency plateaus (e.g., ~3.38 bps/Hz for $N=512$).
  - Left Margin: Critical collapse of communication rate occurs when attempting to push precision beyond ~2.85 cm.
- **Duality:** CC and SC scenarios converge on the same physical frontier.
