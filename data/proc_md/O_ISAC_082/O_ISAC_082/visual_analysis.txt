**Components:**
* **(a) Square-law detection (SLD):** Shows the transformation from an optical signal (centered at fc) to a baseband signal (0 to B) which includes the desired signal and Signal-to-Signal Beat Interference (SSBI).
* **(b) Interference Mitigation Techniques:**
    * **High pass filtering:** Shows the signal at an Intermediate Frequency (fIF) to separate it from the low-frequency SSBI.
    * **DSP elimination (KK/Iteration):** Shows the original baseband signal with SSBI and then the filtered signal where SSBI is removed via Kramers-Kronig (KK) or iterative algorithms.
* **(c) Nonlinear matched filtering:** Illustrates the proposed method where SSBI is exploited (matched) to improve the SNR of the baseband signal.

Image: _page_5_Figure_3.jpeg
Type: Signal Processing Flowchart (NMF)
---
**Components:**
* **Input:** Received signal after ED (contains Signal + SSBI).
* **MF Path:** Conventional Matched Filter produces R11(t) (peak with sidelobes) and noise R21(t). Output R_MF(t).
* **NMF Path:** Nonlinear Matched Filter produces noise R12(t) and a sharper peak R22(t). Output R_NMF(t).
* **Combined:** The two paths are summed: R(t) = R11(t) + k*R11(t)*exp(j*theta).
**Final Output Characteristics:**
The Composite Sensing Signal shows:
- Increased 3dB Resolution (Delta R3dB arrow up).
- Increased PSLR (arrow up).
- Increased SNR (arrow up).

Type: Detailed System Block Diagram
---
**Components:**
* **Central Office (CO):**
    * **Transmitter:** LD2 (Laser), AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator), IQMZM, EDFA (Amplifier), OF (Optical Filter), LD1 (LO Laser), OC (Coupler), VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator), PC (Polarization Controller), THz PD (Photodiode), PA (Power Amplifier), TxA (THz Antenna).
    * **Sensing Receiver (Sens. End):** RxA-S (Antenna), LNA (Amplifier), THz ED (Envelope Detector), DSP-S block (comprising Resample, LPF, Down Convert, NMF).
* **Communication Receiver (Comm. End):**
    * RxA-C (Antenna), LNA, THz ED, DSP-C block (comprising Resample, KK, Synchronization, LPF, Down Convert, Equalization, QAM DeMod, BER Calculation).
* **Wireless Channel:** Signal is transmitted to a Sensing Target and reflected back to CO, or sent to a separate Comm. End.

Type: Communication Performance (BER vs CSPR)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** CSPR (dB) [for (a)] or CSPR-Tx (dB) [for (b)] (0 to 15).
* **Y-axis:** BER (10^-4 to 10^0).
* **Legend:** 
    - w/o KK (blue circle dashed)
    - w/ KK (blue circle solid)
    - TxRCF-w/o KK (red triangle dashed)
    - TxRCF-w/ KK (red triangle solid)
**Data Points:**
* **(a) B2B Configuration:** 
    - Conventional w/o KK fails to reach the 3.8e-3 FEC threshold for almost all CSPR.
    - With KK, the BER drops sharply, hitting a minimum of ~2.2e-4 at 8 dB CSPR. 
    - Using Tx-RCF (Radio Frequency Carrier-like filtering) slightly increases BER compared to raw KK but significantly improves PAPR.
* **(b) 1.5m Wireless Configuration:**
    - Minimum BER achieved is ~1.5e-3 at 6 dB CSPR with KK processing.
    - Performance degrades slightly at higher CSPR due to signal-signal beat noise.
**Constellations:**
16QAM constellations are shown. At the optimal point (circle with arrow), the clusters are sharp and distinct. Away from the optimal (dashed circle), clusters are blurred by interference.
