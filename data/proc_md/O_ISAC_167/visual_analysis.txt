### Type: System Diagram (Multi-Service ISAC for IIoT)

### Components:
- **Transmitter Side:**
  - Narrow-linewidth laser (NLL, <1 kHz linewidth)
  - Wide-linewidth laser (WLL, ~150 MHz)
  - Mach-Zehnder Modulator (MZM)
  - Acoustic Optical Modulator (AOM)

- **Fiber Link:**
  - 12-km SSMF
  - PZT at 10-km position (vibration source)
  - Rayleigh backscattering (RBS)

- **Receiver Side:**
  - Remote IM/DD receiver
  - Coherent receiver
  - Sensing receiver (BPD)

### Operation Modes:
1. **Normal (no vibration):** NLL active, continuous transmission
2. **Vibration detected:** Switch to WLL, pulse mode for localization

### Service Types:
- PAM-4 (25 GBaud)
- 16-QAM UFMC (25 GBaud)
- PDM 16-QAM coherent (20 GBaud)

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Multi-service ISAC on same wavelength channel
- Fiber-based distributed sensing + communication

## Image: _page_2_Picture_4.jpeg
### Type: Diagram (Strain-Induced Fiber Behavior)

### Physical Model:
- Fiber under strain from PZT
- Refractive index change: δn
- Length change: δL
- Strain: V = δL/L

### Key Equations:
- Phase variation: Δφ = (4π/λ)(n + C_ε)VL
- C_ε = refractive index strain coefficient (-0.1649 /με)

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Fundamental physics for fiber vibration sensing
