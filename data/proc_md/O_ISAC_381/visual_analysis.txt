## Image: _page_4_Figure_5.jpeg
### Type: Algorithm Schematic

### Title: LED-DCC Point Clustering Scheme

### Visuals:
- **Concept:** Instead of modulating 1 pixel (weak signal), modulate a cluster of N pixels (2x2, 3x3) with the same data.
- **Benefit:** Increased Emitter Area -> Higher SNR at the Camera -> Longer Range.

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Fundamental trade-off in OCC: Spatial Resolution (Display quality) vs Communication Range.
