Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (VLC-based ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
- LED Array: Multi-element visible light transmitter.
- Driver Circuit: Modulates LEDs with communication/sensing signal.
- Optical Channel: Free-space propagation with multipath.
- Photodetector (PD): Receives optical signal.
- TIA (Trans-impedance Amplifier): Converts photocurrent to voltage.
- ADC: Digitizes received signal.
- DSP: Joint demodulation and sensing extraction.
- User Device: Mobile terminal with VLC receiver.
- Illumination Zone: Coverage area for dual-function operation.

Flow:
1. LED Array driven with modulated signal (communication + sensing pilots).
2. Optical signal propagates through channel to User Device.
3. PD converts optical power to photocurrent.
4. TIA amplifies signal for ADC.
5. DSP extracts data and performs positioning/sensing.

Image: _page_4_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Hybrid RF-VLC ISAC)
---
Components:
- RF BS (Base Station): mmWave/sub-6GHz transmitter.
- VLC AP (Access Point): LED-based optical transmitter.
- UE (User Equipment): Dual-mode receiver.
- Radar Module: Integrated with RF BS for sensing.
- Positioning Engine: Combines VLC and RF for localization.
- Central Controller: Coordinates RF and VLC resources.
- Backhaul: Fiber connection to core network.

Flow:
1. Central Controller allocates resources between RF BS and VLC AP.
2. RF BS transmits data and radar waveforms to UE.
3. VLC AP provides high-rate downlink and positioning pilots.
4. UE receives both RF and VLC signals.
5. Positioning Engine fuses VLC RSS/ToA with RF AoA for localization.
6. Radar Module extracts target information from RF reflections.
