Image: _page_1_Figure_14.jpeg
Type: Signal Transformation Principles (Wigner-Ville and FrFT)
---
**Components:**
* **(a) Wigner Plane Transformation:** Shows a DC signal and an Impulse peak. The DC signal is rotated by alpha=pi/2 to become an impulse.
* **(b) Chirp Signal Analysis:** Illustrates a chirp signal in the Wigner plane (f-t domain) with rotation angle alpha and beta. 
* **(c) Fractional Domain:** After a rotation FrFT, the chirp is concentrated into a sharp impulsive peak in the u-v fractional domain.
**Flow:**
The diagrams illustrate the principle of using the Fractional Fourier Transform (FrFT) to concentrate chirp-based sensing probes. By rotating the Wigner-Ville distribution by an optimal angle 'alpha', a non-stationary chirp signal becomes a stationary peak in the fractional domain, allowing for efficient sensing and Successive Interference Cancellation (SIC).

Image: _page_3_Figure_10.jpeg
Type: Experimental System Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Transmitter Side:** 90 GSa/s AWG1, ITLAs, DP-IQM.
* **Fiber Link:** 20km SSMF + PZT (Vibration source) + 12.5km SSMF.
* **Receiver Side:** West receiver (Rx DSP, DSO, Hybrid) and East receiver (120 GSa/s AWG2, DSO, Rx DSP).
* **Ancillary:** ASE sources, VOAs, Circulators.
* **DSP Blocks:** Transmitter DSP (Binary data, QAM Mapping, Upsampling, RRC Shaping, Resampling, FD-NOMA) and Receiver DSP (Synchronization, CD Comp., Sensing & SIC, Resampling & MF, MIMO Equalization, FOC, CPR, Decision & Demapping).
* **Fractional Domain Logic:** Dechirp, FFT & Filter, IFFT, Fractional domain convergence.
**Flow:**
The system generates a dual-polarized ISAC signal using FD-NOMA. The West receiver handles sensing and data recovery from the forward link, while the East receiver (West-to-East) or backscatter logic handles the return. The PZT simulates localized vibration.
