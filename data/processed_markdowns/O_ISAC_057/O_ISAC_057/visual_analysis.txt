### Panel (a): Proposed Architecture
*   **Transmitter Block (mmW-Radar/Comm Tx):**
    *   **Optical Source 1:** **LD1** (Laser Diode) -> **IM1** (Intensity Modulator, driven by **OFC module** with $f_{RF}$) -> Generates Optical Frequency Comb at Point A.
    *   **Optical Source 2:** **LD2** -> **SSB** (Single Sideband Modulator, driven by **LFM** signal $f_L$) -> Generates Sensing signal at Point B.
    *   **Combination:** Signals A and B combined via **OC** (Optical Coupler).
    *   **Data Modulation:** Combined signal -> **EDFA** -> **IM2** (driven by **OOK** data) -> **PD** (High-speed Photodiode).
    *   **Transmission:** PD output -> **PA** (Power Amp) -> **Tx Antenna**.
*   **Communication Receiver:**
    *   **Rx Antenna** -> **LNA** (Low Noise Amp) -> **Envelope Detector** -> **ADC & DSP**.
*   **Radar Receiver:**
    *   **Echo Path:** Reflection from **Target** -> **Rx Antenna** -> **LNA**.
    *   **De-chirping:** RF Echo feeds electrical port of **IM3**. Optical reference from OC feeds optical port of IM3.
    *   **Detection:** IM3 Output -> **OBPF** (Optical Bandpass Filter) -> **Low-speed PD** -> **ADC & DSP**.

### Panel (b): Spectral Evolution
*   **Point A:** Comb lines at $f_c, f_c \pm f_{RF}$.
*   **Point B:** Single sideband at $f_c - \Delta F_c - f_L$.
*   **Point C:** Combined spectrum showing frequency spacing $\Delta F_c$.
*   **Point D:** Generated electrical spectrum centered at $\Delta F_c$ with bandwidth $N \cdot B$.
