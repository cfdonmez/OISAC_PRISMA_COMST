Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Photonic Integrated ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
- Laser Source: Tunable CW laser (center wavelength not specified).
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): Electro-optic modulator for signal encoding.
- AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator): Generates baseband waveform for modulation.
- PIC (Photonic Integrated Circuit): Integrated platform hosting multiple functions.
- EDFA: Erbium-doped fiber amplifier for signal amplification.
- Circulator: 3-port optical circulator for Tx/Rx isolation.
- Tx Antenna: Optical aperture or collimator for transmission.
- Target: Reflective object for sensing.
- Rx Antenna: Collection optics for received signal.
- Photodetector (PD): Converts optical signal to electrical.
- ADC: Analog-to-digital converter for digitization.
- DSP: Digital signal processing for data recovery and sensing estimation.

Flow:
1. Laser Source generates CW optical carrier.
2. AWG provides modulating waveform to MZM.
3. MZM imprints ISAC waveform onto optical carrier.
4. EDFA amplifies modulated signal.
5. Circulator routes signal to Tx Antenna.
6. Optical beam illuminates Target and propagates to Rx.
7. Rx Antenna collects backscattered/direct signal.
8. Circulator routes received signal to PD.
9. PD converts to electrical signal.
10. ADC digitizes the signal.
11. DSP performs joint demodulation and sensing parameter extraction.

Type: Chart (BER vs Received Optical Power)
---
Axes:
- X-axis: Received Optical Power (dBm). Range: -30 to -10 dBm.
- Y-axis: BER. Range: 10^-7 to 10^0 (log scale).

Legend:
- Communication Only (Blue solid): No sensing overhead.
- ISAC 10% Sensing (Red dashed): 10% resources for sensing.
- ISAC 20% Sensing (Green dotted): 20% resources for sensing.
- ISAC 30% Sensing (Orange dash-dot): 30% resources for sensing.

Data Points:
- Communication Only: BER = 10^-3 at -22 dBm; BER = 10^-6 at -18 dBm.
- ISAC 10%: BER = 10^-3 at -21 dBm; BER = 10^-6 at -17 dBm.
- ISAC 20%: BER = 10^-3 at -19 dBm; BER = 10^-6 at -15 dBm.
- ISAC 30%: BER = 10^-3 at -17 dBm; BER = 10^-6 at -13 dBm.

Trend:
- Each 10% sensing overhead incurs ~1.5-2 dB power penalty for same BER.
- 30% sensing overhead results in total ~5 dB penalty compared to communication-only.
- All configurations achieve BER <10^-6 with sufficient received power.

Type: Chart (Range Resolution vs Bandwidth)
---
Axes:
- X-axis: Signal Bandwidth (GHz). Range: 0.1 to 10 GHz (log scale).
- Y-axis: Range Resolution (cm). Range: 1 to 1000 cm (log scale).

Legend:
- Theoretical Limit (Blue solid): c/(2B) resolution bound.
- Measured Performance (Red circles): Experimental data points.
- Post-Processing Enhanced (Green triangles): With super-resolution DSP.

Data Points:
- At 0.5 GHz: Theoretical = 30 cm; Measured = 35 cm; Enhanced = 25 cm.
- At 1 GHz: Theoretical = 15 cm; Measured = 18 cm; Enhanced = 12 cm.
- At 2 GHz: Theoretical = 7.5 cm; Measured = 9 cm; Enhanced = 6 cm.
- At 5 GHz: Theoretical = 3 cm; Measured = 4 cm; Enhanced = 2.5 cm.
- At 10 GHz: Theoretical = 1.5 cm; Measured = 2 cm; Enhanced = 1.2 cm.

Trend:
- Range resolution inversely proportional to bandwidth (ΔR = c/2B).
- Measured values 15-25% worse than theoretical due to practical impairments.
- Post-processing enhancement achieves ~15-20% improvement over raw measurements.
- 10 GHz bandwidth enables sub-2 cm range resolution.
