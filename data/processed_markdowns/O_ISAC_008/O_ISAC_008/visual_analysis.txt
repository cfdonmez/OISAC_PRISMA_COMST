Image [_page_0_Figure_12.jpeg]: Setup/Diagram.
(a) Setup: This is a block diagram of a Lidar/Communication system. It consists of a "Lidar/Commun. TX" (transmitter), a "Lidar RX" (receiver), and a "Commun. Rx" (communication receiver). The TX section uses a laser, PC (polarization controller), MZM (Mach-Zehnder modulator) driven by an AWG (arbitrary waveform generator) with a QPSK-LFM (quadrature phase-shift keying-linear frequency modulation) waveform, EDFA (erbium-doped fiber amplifier), and a splitter. One output from the splitter goes via a VOA (variable optical attenuator) to a TX OPA (optical phased array) for emission towards a target, while the other serves as an LO (local oscillator) for the Lidar RX. The Lidar RX receives light from the target via an RX OPA, combines it with the LO in a Coherent Receiver, and processes it for Target Detection using an OSC (oscilloscope). The Commun. Rx section receives signals from the target, processes them through a PD (photodiode), TIA (transimpedance amplifier), mixer with an ELO (electrical local oscillator), LPF (low-pass filter), OSC, and performs DSP-based Data Recovery.
(b) Diagram: This depicts an "OPA Antenna Array" with concentric rings. It shows both "Full ring array" elements (empty circles) and "Thinned ring array" elements (blue filled circles), indicating a sparse array configuration. Emission is shown directionally with angles $\theta$ and $\psi$.
(c) Diagram: This is a detailed diagram of a "Blazed Grating Antenna," showing a waveguide structure with a shallow etch, a length L=2.86µm, and a width W=1.2µm.

It displays a single, sharp peak at the origin (u=0, v=0), indicating a highly concentrated beam. The normalized intensity ranges from 0 to 1, with the peak reaching 1.

A dashed grey envelope indicates the overall intensity profile. A horizontal dashed blue line at approximately Normalized Intensity = 0.1 is labeled with "SNSR=12.23dB."
(c) Chart: This is a Normalized Intensity vs. $\psi$ (°) plot. Similar to (b), it displays multiple sharp, distinct peaks at different $\psi$ angles, demonstrating beam steering in the orthogonal dimension. A dashed grey envelope outlines the intensity profile. A horizontal dashed blue line at approximately Normalized Intensity = 0.2 is labeled with "SNSR=6.57dB."
