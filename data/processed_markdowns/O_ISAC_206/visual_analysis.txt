### Symbol Mapping:
- NC out of K beams activated
- C(K,NC) possible patterns
- Additional ⌊log₂C(K,NC)⌋ index bits
- M-ary PSK/QAM for data symbols

### Communication Receiver:
- WRF: Analog combiner (Nr×K)
- WBB: Digital LMMSE equalizer
- ML or 2-step quantization detector

### Echo Receiver:
- FS^H: Analog combiner (same as TX)
- Beamspace MUSIC for DoA
