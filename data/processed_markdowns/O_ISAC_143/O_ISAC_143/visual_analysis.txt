# O_ISAC_143 Visual Analysis

## 1. Hardware Architecture (PoseFly)
- **Fig. 1 & 2 (_page_0_Picture_8.jpeg / _page_1_Figure_0.jpeg)**: System block diagram and prototype.
    - **Modules**: Driving (Motors/Propellers), Communication (GCS/RF Transceiver), Flight Control (IMU/Processor), Off-board (Camera/LiDAR).
    - **PoseFly Attachment**: Arduino Nano + 4 LEDs (1 Red active label, 3 Green quick-link/sensing nodes).
    - **Prototype**: 4DV9 mini drone (14cm x 14cm).

## 2. Drone Indication & ID
- **Fig. 4(a) (_page_2_Figure_0.jpeg)**: Rolling shutter pattern recognition.
    - Binary strings encoded in LED flickering (invisible to human eye but captured as strips by camera).
    - Example: ID #9 = `0b1001`.
    - Features: Pilot symbol, On symbols, Off symbols.

## 3. Localization & Sensing
- **Fig. 4(b) (_page_2_Figure_1.jpeg)**:
    - **Distance Estimation**: Based on the captured size of the drone/LED markers.
    - **Speed Estimation**: Based on the shape variation (elongation/blur) of the rolling shutter spots.
    - **Angle Estimation**: Based on the relative positions and color arc patterns of the 4 LEDs.

## 4. Quick-Link Communication (OCC)
- **Fig. 4(c) (_page_2_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Modulated waveform.
    - **Scheme**: CP-OOK (Cyclic Pilot - On Off Keying).
    - **Data Frame**: CP segment + 30 data bits.
    - **Throughput**: 5.4 Kbps @ 60 FPS.

## 5. Dual Use: Illumination
- **Fig. 4(d) (_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)**: LEDs provide basic lighting for nighttime flight, demonstrating a 4-in-1 integrated sensing, communication, identification, and lighting system.
