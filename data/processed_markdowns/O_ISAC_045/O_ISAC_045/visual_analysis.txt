**Components:**
- ASE (Amplified Spontaneous Emission source)
- EDFA1, EDFA2, EDFA3, EDFA4 (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifiers)
- OTF (Optical Tunable Filter)
- PC (Polarization Controller)
- Modulator (receiving 1.25-Gbit/s OOK signal)
- 10 km fiber (Standard Single Mode Fiber, placed between EDFA2/DCF and likely another segment or representing the link)
- DCF (Dispersion Compensating Fiber)
- SiN Chip (Silicon Nitride Chip)
- ATT (Attenuator)
- 50:50 (Optical Coupler/Splitter)
- OSA (Optical Spectrum Analyzer)
- BERT (Bit Error Rate Tester)

**Flow:**
The experimental setup begins with an **ASE** source connecting to **EDFA1** for pre-amplification. The signal passes through an **OTF** and a **PC** before entering the **Modulator**, which is driven by a **1.25-Gbit/s OOK** electrical signal. The modulated optical signal is boosted by **EDFA2** and transmitted through **10 km fiber**. It then passes through a **DCF** and is amplified again by **EDFA3**. The signal proceeds through the DUT (Device Under Test), labeled as **SiN Chip**, and is amplified by **EDFA4**. Finally, the signal goes through an **ATT** (attenuator) and is split by a **50:50 coupler** to be monitored simultaneously by an **OSA** and a **BERT**.

**Axes Extraction:**
- **X-axis:** Received Power (dBm) [Range: -18 to -10]
- **Y-axis:** log10(BER) [Range: -6 to -3]

**Data Points:**
- At -18 dBm, B-to-B BER is approx 10^-4.8.
- At -18 dBm, DI Water BER is approx 10^-4.6.
- At -17 dBm, 3.5% NaCl BER is approx 10^-3.1.
- At -10 dBm, B-to-B BER is approx 10^-5.8.
- At -10 dBm, DI Water BER is approx 10^-5.6.
- At -10 dBm, 3.5% NaCl BER is approx 10^-4.1.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** B to B, DI Water, 3.5% NaCl.
- **Trend:** BER improves (decreases) as Received Power increases for all cases. The 3.5% NaCl solution introduces a significant power penalty/performance degradation compared to DI Water and B-to-B scenarios. At a BER of 10^-4, there is a penalty of roughly 5-6 dB for 3.5% NaCl compared to DI Water.

**Axes Extraction:**
- **X-axis:** Wavelength (nm) [Range: 1597 to 1601]
- **Y-axis:** Transmission (dB) [Range: -50 to -30]

**Data Points:**
- **DI Water:** Resonance dip observed at **1597.892 nm**, with transmission reaching approx -46 dB.
- **3.5% NaCl:** Resonance dip observed at **1599.8 nm**, with transmission reaching approx -45.5 dB.
- **Shift:** The resonance dip shifts from 1597.892 nm to 1599.8 nm, a red-shift of approx **1.908 nm**.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** DI water vs 3.5% NaCl.
- **Trend:** The introduction of 3.5% NaCl causes a significant positive wavelength shift (red-shift) in the transmission spectrum compared to DI water, indicating sensitivity of the sensor to the refractive index change.
