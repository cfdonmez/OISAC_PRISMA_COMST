**Components:**
- **Transceiver:** Ceiling-mounted unit with **PD** (Photodiode) and **LED**.
- **Users:** User 1, User 2, User 3, ... User M. Each has a desk and computer.
- **Signals:** 
    - **Communication Purpose (Blue lines):** Downlink from LED to Users.
    - **Sensing Purpose (Red dashed lines):** Signals emitted by LED, reflecting off Users, and received by PD.
- **Environment:** Indoor room with a door.

**Flow:**
The **LED** in the transceiver broadcasts optical signals. These serve two purposes:
1.  **Communication:** Direct downlink data transmission to Users (1 to M).
2.  **Sensing:** The light reflects off objects/users in the room. The reflections are captured by the **PD** in the transceiver to sense the environment (e.g., user presence/location).
