Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Photonic Radar Transceiver)
---
Components:
- MLL (Mode-Locked Laser): Generates optical frequency comb.
- Comb Lines: Multiple wavelengths for multi-target sensing.
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): Modulates comb with radar waveform.
- AWG (Arrayed Waveguide Grating): Demultiplexes comb lines to separate paths.
- PA (Power Amplifiers): Boosts individual channel powers.
- Antenna Array: Phased array for beam steering.
- Target: Object reflecting radar signal.
- LNA (Low Noise Amplifiers): Amplifies received signals.
- PD Array: Photodetector array for optical-to-electrical conversion.
- DSP: Digital signal processing for target extraction.

Flow:
1. MLL generates wideband optical frequency comb.
2. MZM modulates comb with FMCW or pulsed radar waveform.
3. AWG separates comb lines to individual spatial channels.
4. PA amplifies each channel for transmission.
5. Antenna Array emits RF signals toward Target.
6. Reflected RF signals received by Antenna Array.
7. LNAs amplify received signals.
8. PD Array converts back to optical domain.
9. DSP extracts range, velocity, and angular information.

---
Axes:
- X-axis: Offset Frequency (Hz). Range: 10^1 to 10^7 Hz (log scale).
- Y-axis: Phase Noise (dBc/Hz). Range: -160 to -60 dBc/Hz.

Legend:
- MLL-based Comb (Blue): Photonic source.
- Electronic Synthesizer (Red): Conventional RF source.
- Cavity-Stabilized Laser (Green): Ultra-low noise reference.

Data Points:
- At 1 kHz offset: MLL = -110 dBc/Hz; Electronic = -90 dBc/Hz; Cavity = -130 dBc/Hz.
- At 10 kHz offset: MLL = -125 dBc/Hz; Electronic = -105 dBc/Hz; Cavity = -145 dBc/Hz.
- At 100 kHz offset: MLL = -140 dBc/Hz; Electronic = -115 dBc/Hz; Cavity = -155 dBc/Hz.
- At 1 MHz offset: MLL = -150 dBc/Hz; Electronic = -125 dBc/Hz; Cavity = -160 dBc/Hz.

Trend:
- MLL achieves 15-25 dB lower phase noise than electronic synthesizers.
- Cavity-stabilized laser provides additional 15-20 dB improvement over MLL.
- Photonic approach enables superior Doppler resolution due to lower phase noise.
