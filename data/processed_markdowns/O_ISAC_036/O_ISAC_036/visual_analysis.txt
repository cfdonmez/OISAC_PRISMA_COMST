Image _page_1_Figure_10.jpeg:
Type: Setup
Description: Experimental arrangement for mmWave/FSO Fi-Wi links, including AWG, PIC, EDFA, demultiplexer, FSO optics, mmWave antennas, and RTO capture.
KEY DATA: Uses 7 km fiber spool, AWG-generated 2048-symbol 16-QAM and LFMCW, 1 m FSO path with collimators, V-band Tx/Rx with 6× upconversion to 62.5 GHz, allowing evaluation of both data and sensing channels.

Image _page_2_Figure_6.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: Collection of experimental results: (a)-(d) constellation/EVM for Fi-Wi and FSO 16-QAM links; (e)-(g) FFT/time/spectrogram for LFMCW sensing; (h)-(j) spectrum and performance of simultaneous ISAC transmission.
KEY DATA: Achieved EVM ≈12% for 16-QAM over mmWave and FSO (meeting 3GPP targets); LFMCW sweep 2.5–7.5 GHz delivers 0.03 m resolution at 384 m; joint ISAC demo multiplexes 25 GHz FMCW and 28 GHz 16-QAM with 11.5% EVM and 0.75 m resolution at 150 m.
