# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_027

## Figure 1: Marine Salinity Monitoring System
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_027/O_ISAC_027/_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Architecture:** 
  - **Source:** 793 nm Laser Diode pumping a 5m Thulium-Doped Fiber (TDF).
  - **Filter:** 793/2000 nm WDM to isolate the 2 μm ASE light.
  - **Probe:** Sensing head deployed underwater, connected via fiber cable to an OSA.
- **Sensing Head Design (Inset):** 
  - Adiabatic-tapered Few-Mode Fiber (FMF) spliced between SMFs.
  - Core/Cladding indices: $n_{core} = 1.4736$, $n_{cladding} = 1.4571$.
  - Modes: Interferometry between **LP01** and **LP02** modes.
- **RI vs Salinity (Inset Graph):** Linear relationship mapping Refractive Index (1.30–1.33) to Salinity (0–15%).

## Figure 2: Modal Dispersion and Sensitivity Physics
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_027/O_ISAC_027/_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) Mode Confinement:** Plot of $n_{eff}$ vs Taper Ratio. 
  - LP02 $n_{eff}$ drops sharply below a ratio of 0.8, indicating energy leakage into cladding and high evanescent coupling.
- **(b) Spectral Advantage:** Effective refractive index difference ($\Delta n_{eff}$) increases with both wavelength (1980–2020 nm) and external RI (1.306–1.314).
  - **Key Insight:** Sensitivity is fundamentally higher at 2 μm compared to C-band (1550 nm).

## Figure 3: Optimization of Waist Diameter (Theory)
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_027/O_ISAC_027/_page_4_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) 50 μm Waist:** Sensitivity $\approx 675$ nm/RIU ($S_{salinity} \approx 0.11$ nm/‰).
- **(b) 40 μm Waist:** Sensitivity nearly doubles to **1216.7–1256.7 nm/RIU** ($S_{salinity} \approx 0.20$ nm/‰).
- **Tradeoff:** Balanced sensitivity with mechanical robustness for deep-sea deployment.

## Figure 4: Deep Ocean Environment and Crosstalk
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_027/O_ISAC_027/_page_4_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **(a) Profiling:** Pressure increases ~1 MPa per 100m depth; Temperature drops to ~2 °C in the deep zone.
- **(b) Pressure Effect:** Blue shift of $\approx -25$ to $-30.9$ pm/MPa (only ~1 nm total drift at 4100m).
- **(c) Temperature Effect:** Blue shift of $\approx -13.1$ to $-13.63$ pm/°C.
- **Impact:** Crosstalk to salinity measurement is $\approx 0.1$ ‰/°C, considered negligible.
