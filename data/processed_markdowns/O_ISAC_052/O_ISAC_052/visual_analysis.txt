**Axes Extraction:**
- **X-axis:** Spectral efficiency C (bps/Hz) [Range: 0.5 to 2.5]
- **Y-axis:** Precision (cm) [Range: 2 to 16]

**Data Points:**
- **Parameters:** N_s (Noise Power) varies from -107 dB/Hz to -93 dB/Hz. Rho varies from 0.00 to 1.00.
- **Comparison:** This graph is very similar to _page_4_Figure_15.jpeg but covers a slightly different range of precision values (2-16 cm vs 4.5-7.5 cm) and possibly uses different system parameters (labels are N_s instead of N_c, maybe separating Sensing vs Communication noise).
- **Curve N_s = -107 dB/Hz (Blue circle):** Precision starts at ~2 cm (rho=0) and degrades to ~3.5 cm at C ~ 2.55 bps/Hz.
- **Curve N_s = -93 dB/Hz (Green triangle):** Precision starts at ~10 cm and degrades to ~16 cm at C ~ 2.5 bps/Hz.
- **Trend:** Similar monotonic trade-off: As spectral efficiency increases (towards the right), precision degrades (curve goes up). The degradation accelerates as rho approaches 1.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** Precision vs Spectral Efficiency for different Sensing noise levels (N_s).
- **Trend:** The vertical asymptotes indicate the theoretical capacity limits. The spacing between curves shows the significant impact of sensing noise power density on the achievable ranging precision (a 14 dB difference in noise leads to a 5x degradation in precision).
