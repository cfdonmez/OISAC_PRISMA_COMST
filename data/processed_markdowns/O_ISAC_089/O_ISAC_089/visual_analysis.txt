Type: Space-Air-Ground Integrated Network (SAGIN) Scenario
---
**Components & Layers:**
* **GEO/MEO Layer:** High-altitude satellites with GEO/MEO-Earth and Laser LEO-GEO/MEO inter-satellite links (ISL).
* **LEO Layer:** Lower-orbit satellites using Terahertz ISL, mmWave ISL, and Laser ISL for mesh connectivity.
* **Earth Layer:** Ground stations, maritime ships (marine communication), aircraft, and communication balloons.
* **Flow:** Multi-modal connectivity (Laser, THz, mmWave) providing seamless coverage from deep space to sub-surface/ground levels.

Type: Technical Roadmap Diagram
---
**Key Objectives:**
1. **Enhancing Rate:** Employs Coherent modulation, Polarization shift keying, Wavelength Division Multiplexing (WDM), and Orbital Angular Momentum (OAM).
2. **Ensuring Security:** Utilizes High directivity, Narrow beams, Anisotropy of optical channels, and Quantum secure communication (Polarized photons).
3. **Extending Distance:** Focuses on Acquisition, Pointing, and Tracking (APT) and advanced Space-borne transceiver designs.

Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Optical Transceiver Hardware Schematic
---
**Optical Module Components:**
* **CPA (Coarse Pointing Assembly):** Gimbal-mounted telescope driven by motors.
* **APT Elements:** PAA (Pointing, Acquisition, and Assembly) with FSM (Fine Steering Mirror), Dichroic mirror, Bandpass filter, and FPA (Fine Pointing Assembly).
* **Sensors:** Quadrant detectors (for tracking error) and Communication sensors (high-speed data).
* **Electronics:** Electronic module split into Communication, Compute, and Control blocks, integrated with an IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit).
**Sensing Mechanism:** A "Pointing error" zoom-in shows the displacement of the focused laser spot on the 4-quadrant detector, which is used for feedback to the FSM.

**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Distance (km, 1000 to 8000).
* **Y-axis:** Maximum Bit Rate (Mbps, $10^3$ to $10^6$ log-scale).
**Data Points (at 4000 km):**
* **32-PPM (Black triangles):** ~24,000 Mbps
* **16-PPM (Purple squares):** ~18,000 Mbps
* **8-PPM (Green crosses):** ~13,000 Mbps
* **SIM BPSK (Red plus):** ~9,000 Mbps
* **OOK (Blue circles):** ~5,000 Mbps
Trend: Pulse Position Modulation (PPM) variants significantly outperform OOK and BPSK at all distances, with higher order M-PPM providing superior throughput.

**Algorithms & Control:**
* **Routing:** RL-based Agent/Environment interaction for dynamic scheduling.
* **Beam Steering:** Advanced hardware like Optical Phased Arrays (OPA), Liquid crystals, and MEMS mirrors.
**Specific ISAC Applications:**
* **Localization:** Time-of-flight and satellite-aided navigation for maritime/aerial assets.
* **Sensing:** Laser sensing of aircraft and UAV clusters for relative location and environmental awareness.
* **Convergence:** Hybrid networking to maintain ground-to-space links during adverse weather (e.g., thick clouds).
