Image: _page_1_Figure_7.jpeg
Type: System Schematic and Power Spectra
---
**Schematic Components:**
* **CU (Central Unit):** LD (Laser), PC1, DP-MZM (Sub-MZM1/2), AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator), EDFA1, PC2.
* **Modulation:** MSG (Message source) -> Dpol-MZM (Sub-MZM3/4), PR, PC3.
* **Link:** PBS (Polarization Beam Splitter), Circulator, PS-FBG (Phase-shifted Fiber Bragg Grating), PBS.
* **RU (Remote Unit):**
    * **X-polarization:** EDFA2, PD1, OSC (Sensing path for Transverse Load).
    * **Y-polarization:** EDFA3, OBPF, OC, PD2, EA1 (Transmit Path); Receiving Antenna1, EA2, MZM, PD3, OSC (Radar Path).
    * **Comms Path:** Receiving Antenna2, Divider, Mixer, OSC.
**Signal Flow:** LD -> DP-MZM (LFM generation) -> EDFA1 -> Dpol-MZM (ASK modulation for comms) -> PBS/Circulator -> PS-FBG (Transverse load sensing) -> PBS splits X (sensing) and Y (ISAC) -> Y is transmitted via antenna and sensed back via MZM/PD3.
**Spectra Extractions:** Show ASK-LFM spectra in X and Y polarizations. Y-pol shows ASK-LFM centered at $f_c$ with sidebands at $f_c \pm 2f_s$.
