Type: System Schematic and Optical Spectra
---
**Schematic:**
* **CU (Central Unit):** ECL -> OC1 -> I/Q (Real/Imag) -> MZM (LO) -> IL1/IL2. Modulates Com/Sen signals. Ends with PBC combining polarizations.
* **DU (Distribution Unit):** SMF -> EDFA -> OS.
* **RU (Remote Unit):** OC4 -> OBPF1/2 -> PD1/2 -> PA1/2 -> HA1/2.
* **Scenario:** RU1 communicates with a User (Com.) and performs sensing (Sen.).
**Spectra (Insets e-g):** Show the wavelength allocation for Carrier, Com-OSB, and Sen-OSB across X and Y polarizations.
