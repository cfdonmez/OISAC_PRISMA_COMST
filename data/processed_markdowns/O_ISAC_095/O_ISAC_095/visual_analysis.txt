Image: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Multi-Panel: Microwave Photonics ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
Panel (a) - Transmitter:
- MLL (Mode-Locked Laser): Optical frequency comb source.
- OBPF (Optical Bandpass Filter): Comb line selection.
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): RF signal upconversion.
- EDFA: Optical amplification.
- WDM Mux: Wavelength multiplexing.
- SMF: Fiber transmission.

Panel (b) - Receiver:
- Circulator: Tx/Rx isolation.
- WDM Demux: Wavelength demultiplexing.
- PD Array: Photodetector bank.
- LNA: Low-noise amplifiers.
- ADC: Multi-channel digitizer.
- DSP: Beamforming and ISAC processing.

Panel (c) - Antenna Array:
- 4×4 Phased Array: 16-element configuration.
- Element spacing: λ/2 at 28 GHz.
- Beam steering: ±60° azimuth/elevation.

Flow:
1. MLL generates 100 GHz comb → OBPF selects 4 lines.
2. MZM upconverts OFDM-ISAC signal to optical domain.
3. EDFA amplifies → WDM Mux combines channels.
4. SMF distributes to remote antenna units.
5. At antenna: WDM Demux → PD → LNA → Antenna elements.
6. Return path: Antenna → LNA → PD → ADC → DSP.
