IMAGE: _page_2_Figure_0.jpeg
TYPE: Chart / Graph
DESCRIPTION: Measured Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) as a function of modulation index (2*pi*h) for conventional OFDM and CE-OFDM across different QAM orders.
X-AXIS: Modulation index (2*pi*h) [Range: 1 to 4].
Y-AXIS: Measured EVM (dB) [Range: -30 to -14].
LEGEND:
- CE-OFDM (Solid lines): 4QAM (blue circle), 16QAM (orange square), 64QAM (green diamond).
- OFDM (Dashed lines): 4QAM (blue circle), 16QAM (orange square), 64QAM (green diamond).
- Horizontal Dash-Dot: 16QAM FEC limit (~-18 dB), 64QAM FEC limit (~-22.5 dB).
- Annotations: "W/o PC" (Without Phase Correction), "W/ PC" (With Phase Correction).
DATA:
- CE-OFDM 4QAM: Reaches minimum EVM of ~-29 dB at 2*pi*h = 2.
- CE-OFDM 16QAM: Minimum EVM ~-28 dB at 2*pi*h = 2.
- CE-OFDM 64QAM: Minimum EVM ~-27.5 dB at 2*pi*h = 2.
- OFDM: Performance degrades significantly as 2*pi*h increases beyond 2.5 without PC.
TREND: CE-OFDM consistently outperforms conventional OFDM. EVM initially decreases as modulation index increases (due to better noise resilience) but rises again at high indices due to cycle slips, which are mitigated by the Phase Correction (PC) algorithm.

IMAGE: _page_2_Figure_4.jpeg
TYPE: Chart / Graph (Spectral Plot)
DESCRIPTION: Simulated Power Spectral Density (PSD) of CE-OFDM for different modulation indices.
X-AXIS: Frequency (MHz) [Range: 0 to 600].
Y-AXIS: Normalized PSD (dB/Hz) [Range: -60 to 0].
LEGEND:
- 2*pi*h = 1 (Blue): Narrow spectrum, high carrier peak.
- 2*pi*h = 2 (Orange): Broader spectrum, lower carrier peak relative to signal.
- 2*pi*h = 4 (Yellow): Widest spectrum, signal power distributed over ~100-500 MHz.
DATA:
- Carrier frequency: Centered at 300 MHz.
- Peak power: Max normalized to 0 dB/Hz at the carrier for 2*pi*h = 4.
- Sidelobes: Roll off to -40 dB/Hz within +/- 200 MHz of the center.
TREND: Increasing the modulation index (h) causes spectral spreading, distributing power more uniformly across subcarriers but increasing occupied bandwidth.
