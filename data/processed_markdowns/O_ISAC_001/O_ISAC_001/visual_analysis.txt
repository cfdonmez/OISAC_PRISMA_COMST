This chart displays the Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) in dB on the Y-axis against the parameter `2πh` on the X-axis. The range for `2πh` is from 1 to 4, and for EVM from -30 dB to -14 dB.

The legend distinguishes between three modulation schemes (4QAM, 16QAM, 64QAM) using different markers (blue circles, orange squares, green diamonds, respectively). It also differentiates between two modulation techniques: CE-OFDM (solid lines) and OFDM (dashed lines). Additionally, two horizontal dashed lines indicate FEC (Forward Error Correction) limits for 16QAM (~-18 dB) and 64QAM (~-22 dB). The legend also includes "W/o PC" (Without Pre-compensation, solid circle marker) and "W/ PC" (With Pre-compensation, solid square marker), which are further annotated as text labels on the plot, specifically for the OFDM curves.

**Key Data and Trends:**
*   **General Performance Trend:** All curves show an initial decrease in EVM (improvement) as `2πh` increases, reaching a minimum, and then an increase in EVM (degradation) for higher `2πh` values.
*   **CE-OFDM vs. OFDM:** CE-OFDM (solid lines) consistently achieves lower EVM (better performance) than OFDM (dashed lines) for all modulation schemes and across the entire `2πh` range.
    *   CE-OFDM minima are around -28 dB to -29 dB, occurring roughly at `2πh` = 1.8 to 2.
    *   OFDM minima are around -26 dB to -27 dB, occurring roughly at `2πh` = 2.2 to 2.4.
*   **Modulation Scheme Impact:** For both CE-OFDM and OFDM, higher-order modulation schemes result in higher EVM (worse performance), as expected. For instance, at `2πh` = 2, CE-OFDM 4QAM is approximately -29 dB, 16QAM -28 dB, and 64QAM -27.5 dB.
*   **FEC Limits:**
    *   All CE-OFDM curves remain well below their respective FEC limits for most `2πh` values (e.g., 16QAM stays below -18 dB, 64QAM below -22 dB).
    *   OFDM curves without pre-compensation ("W/o PC" region indicated by annotation) for 16QAM and 64QAM exceed their FEC limits at `2πh` values greater than approximately 3.2, indicating unacceptable error rates.
*   **Pre-compensation Effect (OFDM):** The labels "W/o PC" and "W/ PC" refer to different performance regions for the OFDM (dashed) curves. "W/o PC" points to the region where EVM significantly increases at higher `2πh` values (e.g., above -20 dB). "W/ PC" points to a region where the OFDM curves maintain lower EVM at higher `2πh` values (e.g., around -26 dB to -27 dB), suggesting an improvement in performance due to pre-compensation, though still generally worse than CE-OFDM.
