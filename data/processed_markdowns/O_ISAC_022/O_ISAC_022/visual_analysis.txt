Image _page_8_Figure_4.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: CDF of RMS positioning error when the DC component is filtered out before RSS processing.
KEY DATA: Removing DC reduces usable optical power by ~7× and degrades the 90th-percentile error to 64.81 cm, motivating DC retention or compensation when ambient light exists.

Image _page_8_Figure_9.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: Standard deviation of the transmitted signal versus QAM order with and without subtracting the DC bias.
KEY DATA: Original data current σ ranges 7.6–9.4 A (α from 1→0) while scaled LED drive current stays ≈2.82 A with DC and ≈0.32 A without DC, showing limited sensitivity to m, M, α, or L_SPAN due to LED current limits.

Image _page_9_Figure_2.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: BER versus roll-off factor α for various filter lengths L_SPAN and subband counts m at M=64.
KEY DATA: BER monotonically decreases as α increases (smoother filters) and longer FIR filters improve BER when α≤0.2; all curves drop below the 3.8×10^-3 FEC limit for α≥0.35.

Image _page_9_Figure_8.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: BER versus α for different modulation orders (M=16…256) and subband counts m (4 or 8).
KEY DATA: Higher M raises BER for a fixed α, but α>0.2 keeps BER below the FEC limit even at M=256; increasing m from 4→8 narrows bandwidth per subband and reduces BER by mitigating LED low-pass distortion.
