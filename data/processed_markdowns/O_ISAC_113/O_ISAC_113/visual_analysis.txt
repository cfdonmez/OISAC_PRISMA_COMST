Image: _page_2_Figure_8.jpeg
Type: Calibration Curves (Frequency vs Temperature)
---
**Axes:** X: Temperature T ($^\circ C$) [25 to 65], Y: Frequency/$\Delta f^*$ (MHz) [0 to 500].
**Equations:**
* Linear fits for frequency shifts.
* $\Delta f = 1.6 \cdot (T - 25) + 50$ (Panel a).
* $\Delta f = 3.2 \cdot (T - 25) + 100$ (Panel b, steeper slope).
* Illustrates the sensitivity of sensing frequency to environment temperature for different harmonic components.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Estimation Error Plot
---
**Axes:** X: L [$0$ to $1.2 \times 10^6$], Y: Frequency error (MHz) [-1 to 1].
**Legend:** Compares "With analogue frequency multiplier" (blue circles) vs "Without" (red triangles).
**Result:** Both methods show error converging toward 0 as L increases. Error bounds $\Delta T < 0.125^\circ C$ and $\Delta T < 0.0625^\circ C$ are achieved.

Type: Performance (Q factor) & Constellations
---
**Axes:** X: Launched optical power (dBm) [-3 to 5], Y: Q factor (dB) [5 to 10].
**Data:** $20^\circ C$ and $60^\circ C$ curves overlap almost perfectly, showing temperature independence of the communication performance. Q factor increases from ~7.6 to ~9.3 dB.
**Constellation (Inset):**
* **Type:** 16-QAM.
* **Polarization:** Showing both X-Pol and Y-Pol.
* **Quality:** Sharp, well-separated clusters, indicating high signal quality at 3 dBm launched power.
