Type: Performance Analysis (Wavelength & Power)
---
**Axes:** Y (left): BER [Log $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-5}$], Y (right): ROP (dBm) [-18.2 to -17.6], X: Wavelength (nm) / IOP (dBm).
**Legend/Trends:**
* (a) **BER vs Wavelength:** Minimum BER (~$1.5 \times 10^{-7}$) at 1549.58 nm. ROP fluctuates between -17.7 and -18.2 dBm.
* (b) **BER vs IOP:** BER decreases from $10^{-2}$ to ~$4 \times 10^{-7}$ as IOP increases from -18 to -3 dBm. Crosses SD-FEC threshold ($2.2 \times 10^{-2}$) at ~-16 dBm.
* (c) **BER vs IOP (2nd configuration):** BER increases with IOP, from $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-5}$ as IOP rises from -18 to 3 dBm.

Type: Sensing Profiles (Point Spread Function)
---
**Axes:** X: Relative Range (mm) [-50 to 50], Y: Normalized Amp. (a.u.) [0 to 1].
**Panels/Trends:**
* (a) **Without homologous LO (10 mm interval):** Broad peaks, poor resolution, cannot resolve the 10 mm spacing clearly.
* (b) **With homologous LO (11.25 mm interval):** Two distinct sharp peaks with 11.25 mm separation clearly visible.
* (c) **Without homologous LO (15 mm interval):** Better visibility than (a) but still significant sidelobes.
* (d) **With homologous LO (15 mm interval):** Two very distinct sharp peaks with 15 mm separation.
**Conclusion:** Homologous LO significantly improves spatial resolution and sidelobe suppression.
