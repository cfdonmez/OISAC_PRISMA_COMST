TYPE: Schematic / Principles Diagram
DESCRIPTION: Principles of typical ISAC waveform implementation, comparing OFDM, OTFS, OCDM, DSSS, and LFM-PSK.
COMPONENTS:
- Top Row (Multi-carrier): OFDM (frequency overlapping subcarriers), OTFS (Time-frequency grid), OCDM (Chirp division via Inverse Fresnel Transform).
- Middle Row (Spread Spectrum): DSSS principle showing "Sequence" (data bits) combined with a "Spread Spectrum" gain/wideband signal to produce "Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum".
- Bottom Row (Pulse Compression): LFM (Linear Frequency Modulation / Chirp) combined with "Single-carrier Modulation" (symbol pulses) to produce "LFM-PSK Waveform".

  - Left/Right Charts: Normalized Amplitude (dB) vs Frequency (GHz). Broadband flat-top spectrum from -10 to 10 GHz.
