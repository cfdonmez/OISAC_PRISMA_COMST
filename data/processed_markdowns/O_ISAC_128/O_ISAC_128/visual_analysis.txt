Type: Dual-Comb and Matched Filter Principles
---
**Schematic Flow:**
* **(a) Dual Comb Generation:** Two combs with spacing $f_0$ and $f_0 + \Delta f$ are combined ("beat") to produce a difference frequency $\Delta f$.
* **(b) Matched Filter DAS:**
    - Transmitter signal $p(t)$ (FMCW) is convolved (*) with channel response $h(t)$.
    - Received mixed signal $p(t)$ is correlated ($\otimes$) with $P(t)$ to recover the DAS trace $h(t)$.
