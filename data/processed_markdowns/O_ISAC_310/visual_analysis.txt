### Type: System Schematic

### Title: Schematic of CE THz ISAC System

### Components:
- LD1/LD2 (Laser Diodes)
- DPMZM (Dual-Parallel MZM) driven by IF ISAC signal
- 90° Hybrid
- EDFA + OBPF
- OC (Optical Coupler)
- PD1/PD2 (Photodetectors for heterodyne)
- Transmitting/Receiving Antennas (Tx-At1/2, Rx-At1/2)
- DSP / OSC (Oscilloscope)

### Signal Flow:
1. Baseband CE-OFDM + LFM-QPSK sub-bands generated
2. Up-conversion to IF
3. Optical CS-SSB modulation via DPMZM
4. Heterodyne with LO (LD2) to generate THz ISAC signal (132-142 GHz)
5. Splitting to Tx-At1 and Tx-At2

### Key Concept:
- Constant Envelope (CE) OFDM to reduce PAPR
- LFM sub-bands for radar and extra QPSK comms
