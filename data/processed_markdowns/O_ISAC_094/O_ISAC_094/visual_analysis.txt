Type: Multimodal ISAC Framework
---
**Components:**
* **UAV Side:**
    * Multimodal Sensing: HSI ($D^{HSI}$) and LiDAR ($D^{LiDAR}$).
    * Encoders: Two parallel blue blocks converting data to $S^{HSI}$ and $S^{LiDAR}$.
    * PE (Perception Enhanced) Module: Includes a Classifier yielding $C^{pre}$.
    * Semantic Fusion Encoder: Combines $C^{pre}$, $S^{HSI}$, and $S^{LiDAR}$ into semantic vector $S$.
* **Channel:** Transmits $S$ to the BS side as $\hat{S}$.
* **BS Side:**
    * Decoders: Two parallel green blocks yielding reconstructed HSI ($\hat{D}^{HSI}$) and LiDAR ($\hat{D}^{LiDAR}$).
    * Classifier: Central blue block yielding final classification $C^{fin}$.

---
**Structure:** Internal view of the "Perception Enhanced Fusion" block.
**Inputs:** $C^{pre}$, $S^{HSI}$, $S^{LiDAR}$.
**Operations:** Shows cross-attention or gated fusion between the classification priors ($C^{pre}$) and the sensing streams ($S^{HSI}, S^{LiDAR}$). Output is the fused vector $S$.

Type: Performance Evaluation Grid (4 charts)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** SNR (dB) (0 to 18).
* **Y-axis:** Test Accuracy (%) (30 to 90) or Test NMSE (0.12 to 0.45).
**Summary:**
* **Fig 3 (Accuracy/SNR multi-modal):** EndNet with PE (Orange) outperforms base EndNet (Blue) by approx 5-10% at low SNR. Accuracy saturates at ~90% for SNR > 10 dB.
* **Fig 4 (Accuracy/SNR single-modal):** single-HSI with PE (Green) is highest (~78% at 18 dB). single-LiDAR (Orange) is lowest (~62%).
* **Bottom Panes (Test NMSE):** EndNet with DeeperNet (Green) achieves lowest error (~0.12 at 18 dB). EndNet with PE (Orange) is consistently lower than base EndNet.

Type: Scaling Performance Chart
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** SNR (dB) (0 to 18).
* **Y-axis:** Test Accuracy (%) (40 to 90).
**Variables:** $K=32, 64, 128, 256$ (Sensing/Communication parameter).
**Performance:** Increasing $K$ significantly improves accuracy. At 0 dB SNR, $K=256$ (blue) achieves ~81% accuracy, while $K=32$ (red) is only at 40%. All saturate near 90% at high SNR (17.5 dB), except $K=32$ which stays around 84%.
