- Type: Chart  
- Description: Logarithmic BER (log(BER)) vs. launch power curve comparing CD compensation-only vs. DNN-based DBP schemes at varying learning steps (80 km, 40 km, 20 km).  
- KEY DATA: BER values below ~10⁻³ are achieved by DNN-based DBP schemes, with faster convergence at smaller learning steps (e.g., 20 km achieves -5 BER at 6 dBm). CD compensation-only requires higher launch power (~6 dBm at 10⁻³ BER vs. ~4 dBm for DNN-based 20 km).
