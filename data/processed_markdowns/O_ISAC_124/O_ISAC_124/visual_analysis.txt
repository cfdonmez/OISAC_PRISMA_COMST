**Components & Scenario:**
* **Vehicle ISAC System:** Centralized control unit within a modern vehicle cockpit.
* **AoD ISAC Interface:** Integrated display panel showing multi-beam scanning icons.
* **User #1 Sensing:** Internal/Driver-centric sensing beam focused on the steering wheel/driver area.
* **User #2 (V2P):** External pedestrian detected by the Vehicular ISAC system at a crosswalk.
* **Vehicle (V2V):** Leading vehicle in traffic being tracked for cooperative awareness.
* **Functional Flow:** The LWAoD aperture generates simultaneous beams for internal sensing (driver monitoring) and external communication/sensing (V2X/Radar) to enhance traffic safety and situational awareness.

** (a) Stack-up Configuration (Top-to-Bottom):**
* **L6 (Glass):** Protective cover glass layer.
* **L5 (OCA):** Optically Clear Adhesive.

* **L2 (OCA):** Second adhesive layer.
* **L1 (Display Panel):** OLED/LCD base panel serving as the electrical ground.

** (b) 2D Spatial Beam Scanning Concept:**
* **Frequency Scanning (Freq. Scan.):** Beam steering occurs in the **xoz-plane** as a function of frequency. Labeled points: **f1, f2, f3**.
* **Phase Scanning (Pha. Scan.):** Beam steering happens in the **yoz-plane** via phase shift control. Labeled points: **φ0, φ1, φ2**.
* **Operational Goal:** Simultaneous frequency-dependent and phase-controlled beam steering enabling full spatial coverage for vehicular ISAC.

Type: FPC Feeding Network Schematics
---
**Variants:**
* **(a) FS Type (Frequency Scanning):** Utilizes a **1x8 Corporate Power Divider** (Binary tree structure) fed by a single input to provide in-phase amplitude.
* **(b) PS Type (Phase Scanning):** Replaces the power divider with **8 independent CPWG feeding lines** terminated in an 8-port WMX interface for individual element phase control.
* **Common Component:** Both types utilize a **1x8 Balun** array at the output to convert signals for EH1-mode differential excitation.
