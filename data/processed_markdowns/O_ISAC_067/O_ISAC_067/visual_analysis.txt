**Components:**
- **TX (Transmitter):** Located at the bottom right of the central area.
- **RX (Receiver):** Located at the bottom left, separated from TX by **3.378 m**.
- **CNC Machine:** Large industrial component centered at the top.
- **Forklift Truck:** Moving object on the right side.
- **Path:** Indicated by a green dashed arrow labeled "d = 0 m" to "d = 5 m".
- **Vertex:** Labeled with a height of **1.689 m**.
- **Angles:** Measurement sectors shown from **-60° to 60°**.

**Flow:**
The diagram illustrates an indoor industrial propagation scenario. A forklift moves along a linear path (d = 0 to 5 m). Signals from the TX reflect off the CNC machine and the forklift to reach the RX. The geometry defines a bistatic sensing/communication setup with a 1.689 m depth and 3.378 m baseline.
