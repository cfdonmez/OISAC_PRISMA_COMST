## Figure 7: Positioning RMSE vs. SNR (LoS Scenario)

  - **M=1024:** Radar-RSS achieves $\sim 0.005$ m error.

- **Observation:** Radar-based methods (Radar/Radar-RSS) drastically outperform base OOK/RSS methods which plateau or remain high in noisy conditions.
