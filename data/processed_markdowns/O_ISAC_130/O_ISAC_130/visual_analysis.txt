Type: Research Methodology and Comparison
---
** (a) Proposed Workflow:**
1. Selection (Colors: Blue to Red | Samples: E. marina, I. galbana).
2. Data Training: Characterization + BER measurement -> Mapping Function $F_B(X_C, X_D, X_{DR})$.
3. Density Measuring: Calculate unknown density from BER.
4. Accuracy Evaluation: Feedback comparison with manual counting.
** (b) Comparative Model:** 
* **Conventional:** Sampling -> Characterization (Manual/Dry weight) -> Storage/Mod/Trans -> Decoding.
* **Proposed:** Integrated Sensing & Communication in one step (Transfer BER directly to density).
