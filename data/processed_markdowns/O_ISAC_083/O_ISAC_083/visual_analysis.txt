The architecture depicts a heterogeneous 6G-ready network backbone in a metropolitan area, utilizing a mix of high-speed optical fiber (WDM/SSMF) and high-bandwidth wireless (THz, FSO, RFW) to provide pervasive connectivity, cell-free d-MIMO, and distributed AI processing.

Image: _page_2_Figure_1.jpeg
Type: Confluent Network Resource Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Endpoints:** Base Station A and Base Station B.
* **Parallel Transmission Paths:**
    1. **Single-segment FSO:** Utilizing optical laser heads for line-of-sight wireless.
    2. **Single-segment active DWDM:** Features a tube containing ROADMs (Reconfigurable Optical Add-Drop Multiplexers), optical fibers, and amplifiers.
    3. **Multi-segment passive CWDM:** Segmented fiber links using Coarse WDM.
    4. **Multi-segment dark fiber:** Segmented unlit fiber links.
* **Monitoring Inset:** A spectral plot (Power/Frequency) with a magnifying glass highlighting the "RoF" (Radio over Fiber) peak and the "OSaaS" (Optical Spectrum as a Service) band.
**Flow:**
The diagram illustrates how diverse physical layer resources (Fiber, FSO, WDM) are converged between base stations to provide resilient and high-capacity transport for 6G services. Station B further distributes signals to multiple downstream nodes (C through H).

Image: _page_2_Figure_3.jpeg
Type: Probed DWDM Link Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Test Equipment:**
    - **C-OTDR:** Coherent Optical Time-Domain Reflectometer.
    - **S-BVT:** Sliceable Bandwidth Variable Transmitter.
* **Switching:** 1:16 Optical Switch for selecting between multiple links.
* **Links:** Shows Link 1 (Detailed) and Links 14, 15, 16.
    - **Link 1 Details:** Contains ROADMs, booster/preamplifiers, and segments of fiber.
* **Legend Symbols:**
    - **Triangle:** Booster/preamplifier.
    - **Blue Curved Arrow:** Amplifier bypass and 1650nm reflector (used for C-OTDR probing).
    - **Red U-turn Arrow:** OSC (Optical Service Channel) Loopback.
**Flow:**
This schematic details the telemetry and sensing infrastructure. A C-OTDR and S-BVT probe multiple DWDM links via an optical switch. The 1650nm reflector allows the probing signal to bypass amplifiers and return to the CO for real-time monitoring of fiber health and latency.

**Flow:**
This block diagram presents the unified management and orchestration (MANO) framework for the ISAC network. It shows how software-defined control and AI orchestration are applied across a hierarchical physical topology (CU -> DU -> RU) using heterogeneous optical and wireless transport.
