**Components:**
- **Input:** Optical frequency comb (OFC) shown as a series of discrete lines in the frequency domain.
- **Spectrum:** A broad Gaussian-like envelope with labeled bandwidth **BW**.
- **Time Domain:** A sequence of narrow pulses (solitons/pulses) with constant period.

**Flow:**
Illustrates the principle of generating an optical frequency comb. A broad spectral bandwidth (BW) in the frequency domain, consisting of discrete lines separated by repetition rate $f_r$, corresponds to a periodic train of narrow pulses in the time domain.

Image: _page_2_Figure_0.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **Comb Signals Generator:** CW Laser, Driven by 2.5 GHz, MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator), EDFA.
- **Data Channels (1, 2, 3):** CW Laser, MZM (driven by AWG data), emitting at different wavelengths $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3$.
- **Link:** WDM (Multiplexer), SMF 10km, WDD (Demultiplexer).
- **Receivers (Antenna 1, 2, 3):** WDM, PD (Photodiode), Amp, LBF (Low-pass Filter), BPF (Band-pass Filter), Mixer, Antenna (2.5, 5.0, 7.5 GHz).

**Flow:**
The system generates a multi-tone optical comb (2.5, 5, 7.5 GHz) and three independent data channels at different wavelengths. These are multiplexed via WDM and transmitted over 10km of fiber. At the receiver, they are demultiplexed. Each data channel is mixed with a specific comb line in a PD to generate multi-band THz/RF signals (2.5 GHz, 5 GHz, 7.5 GHz) for simultaneous wireless transmission.
