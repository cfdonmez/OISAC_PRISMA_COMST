Image: _page_6_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
- **Tx DSP:** Quantity Mapping, Pre-distortion (Algorithm 1), Norming Constants Mapping, Soliton Signal Generation (INFT), Add Sync. Header.
- **Fiber-optic Link:** NZDSF (Non-Zero Dispersion Shifted Fiber, 50 km per span), EDFA (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier), OBPF (Optical Bandpass Filter). Total **40 spans** (2000 km).
- **Rx DSP:** Timing Sync., Signal Decomposition, Discrete Spectrum Computation (NFT), Quantity Detection (Algorithm 2), L-MMSE, Decision.

**Flow:**
Data is mapped to soliton parameters in the Tx DSP, transformed via Inverse Nonlinear Fourier Transform (INFT), and transmitted through a 2000 km link consisting of 40 spans of 50 km NZDSF and EDFA/OBPF modules. The receiver reverses the process using NFT and Algorithm 2 for quantity detection.
