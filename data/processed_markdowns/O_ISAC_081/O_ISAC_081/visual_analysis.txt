---
**Components:**
* **Scenario #1 (Car Interior):** Shows a TWAoD (Transparent Wavelength-Aware of Device) array integrated into a car. It highlights "Sensing Beam at f1 GHz" (blue dashed) and "Communication Beam at f2 GHz" (pink dashed). Arrows point to User #1 (Driver) and User #2 (Passenger).

The proposed TWAoD array enabled dual-functionality (ISAC) by steering distinct beams for sensing and communication at different frequencies (f1 and f2), supporting multiple users or tracking position updates in real-time.
