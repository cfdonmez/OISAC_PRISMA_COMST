# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_029

## Figure 1: THz Fiber-Wireless Architecture
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_029/O_ISAC_029/_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Concept:** Centralized Unit (CU/DU) linked via 10-20 km fiber to Active Antenna Units (AAUs).
- **Signal Flow:** 
  - Downlink: ISAC signals (TDM of 16-QAM + LFM).
  - Uplink: De-chirped sensing signal (low bandwidth tone) sent back from AAU to CU/DU.
- **AAU Detail:** Uses an O/E PD for transmission and a "Full-Photonic De-chirping" block for echo reception.

## Figure 2: Experimental Setup
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_029/O_ISAC_029/_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Source:** ECL-1 (Signal) and ECL-2 (LO) heterodyning.
- **Radar Rx:** TFLN-MZM (Broadband, 110+ GHz capable) receives THz echo directly.
- **Network Integration:** 20 km SMF loop for both downlink signal and uplink de-chirped tone.
- **UE Side:** 12x Frequency multiplier for communication down-conversion to IF.

## Figure 4: Sensing Performance Floors
**File:** [_page_8_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_029/O_ISAC_029/_page_8_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) Center Frequency:** Optimal SNR (~24 dB) at **129-134 GHz**. Drops outside due to electronics and TFLN bandwidth limits.
- **(b) Vpp:** SNR increases linearly with transmit voltage from 200 mV to 900 mV.

## Figure 5: Range Resolution vs. Fiber Length
**File:** [_page_9_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_029/O_ISAC_029/_page_9_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) Theoretical:** 30 GHz LFM bandwidth $\rightarrow$ **6 mm** resolution. Measured value perfectly tracks theoretical from 5 GHz to 30 GHz.
- **(b, c) Fiber Impact:** 20 km fiber only negligible resolution degradation (~0.1 mm) and ~4 dB SNR penalty compared to back-to-back.

## Figure 6: Ranging Accuracy and Error Calibration
**File:** [_page_9_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_029/O_ISAC_029/_page_9_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **Calibration:** Systematic error of **6.5 cm** removed.
- **Result:** After calibration, the mean error is **< 3 mm** for targets at 0.26, 0.42, 0.51, and 0.77 m. Standard deviation is $\approx 4$ mm.

## Figure 7: Communication Capacity Results
