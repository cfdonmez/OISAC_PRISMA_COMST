Type: System Diagram / Schematic (THz Fiber-Wireless ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
- CW Laser (1550 nm): Continuous-wave optical source.
- MZM (Mach-Zehnder Modulator): Modulates optical carrier with THz signal.
- EDFA (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier): Boosts optical power.
- UTC-PD (Uni-Traveling-Carrier Photodiode): O/E conversion for THz generation.
- THz Antenna (Tx): Transmits THz signal wirelessly.
- Target: Reflective object for sensing.
- THz Antenna (Rx): Receives reflected THz signal.
- LNA (Low Noise Amplifier): Amplifies weak received THz signal.
- Mixer: Downconverts THz signal using LO.
- LO (Local Oscillator): Provides reference frequency for mixing.
- ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter): Digitizes the baseband signal.
- DSP (Digital Signal Processing): Performs sensing/communication decoding.

Flow:
1. CW Laser generates 1550 nm optical carrier.
2. MZM modulates the carrier with the THz-frequency signal (data + sensing waveform).
3. EDFA amplifies the modulated optical signal.
4. UTC-PD converts the optical signal to a THz electrical signal.
5. THz Antenna (Tx) transmits the signal wirelessly.
6. Signal reflects off the Target and is captured by THz Antenna (Rx).
7. LNA amplifies the received signal.
8. Mixer downconverts using the LO reference.
9. ADC digitizes the baseband signal.
10. DSP performs joint sensing (range/velocity estimation) and communication (data decoding).

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Experimental Setup)
---
Components:
- AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator): Generates baseband ISAC waveform.
- MZM: Modulates the optical carrier.
- EDFA: Amplifies the optical signal.
- VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator): Controls optical power level.
- UTC-PD: Generates THz signal (centered at 300 GHz).
- Horn Antennas (Tx/Rx): Transmit and receive THz signals.
- Target (Metal Plate): Reflective target for sensing experiments.
- LNA: Amplifies received THz signal.
- Subharmonic Mixer: Downconverts THz signal with LO (x12 multiplier chain).
- Oscilloscope/DSP: Captures and processes the downconverted signal.

Flow:
1. AWG generates the OFDM ISAC waveform (e.g., 4 GHz bandwidth).
2. MZM modulates the 1550 nm CW laser with this waveform.
3. EDFA amplifies the signal, VOA attenuates to set power.
4. UTC-PD generates the 300 GHz THz carrier.
5. Signal is transmitted via Horn Antenna (Tx).
6. Target reflects the signal, which is received by Horn Antenna (Rx).
7. LNA amplifies, Subharmonic Mixer downconverts (using x12 LO chain from ~25 GHz source).
8. Oscilloscope captures and offline DSP decodes communication data and extracts sensing parameters.

---
Axes:
- X-axis: MZM Bias Voltage (V). Range: 0 to 10 V.
- Y-axis (Left): Optical Power (dBm). Range: -10 to 0 dBm.
- Y-axis (Right): RF Power at 300 GHz (dBm). Range: -50 to -30 dBm.

Data Points:
- Optical Power (Blue): Follows a sinusoidal curve with minima at ~2V (null point) and ~7V, maxima near 0V, 4.5V, 9V.
- RF Power at 300 GHz (Red): Peaks at the null-bias point (~2V), achieving approx -32 dBm.
- Optimal Bias: 2V (null-biased operation) maximizes THz signal generation efficiency due to carrier-suppressed double-sideband (CS-DSB) modulation.
