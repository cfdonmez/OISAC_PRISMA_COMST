## Figure 3: Robustness against LOS Blockers

## Figure 5: Communication vs. Sensing Trade-off
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_015/O_ISAC_015/_page_4_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **Variables:** Sum Rate (Red/Magenta) vs. MI (Blue/Green) across Communication Priority ($\Omega_c$, 0 to 1).
- **Crossover:** At $\Omega_c \approx 0.65$ (10 users), the average sum rate exceeds the sensing MI.
- **Range:** Sum rate increases from ~30 to ~175 Gbps; MI decreases from ~230 to ~30 Gbps.
