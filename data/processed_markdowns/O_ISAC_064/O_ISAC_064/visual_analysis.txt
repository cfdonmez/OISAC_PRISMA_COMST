**Components:**
- **Transmitter Side:**
  - Optical Frequency Comb Generator (OFCG).
  - CW Laser.
  - Mach-Zehnder Modulators (MZMs).
  - Wavelength Division Multiplexer (WDM).
  - Power Splitters.
- **Link/Atmosphere:**
  - Free-Space Optical (FSO) channel.
  - Turbulence simulation.
- **Receiver Side (ISAC Receiver):**
  - Photodetectors (PDs).
  - Transimpedance Amplifiers (TIAs).
  - Digital Signal Processing (DSP) unit.
  - Ranging and Communication output blocks.

**Flow:**
The system uses an OFCG and a Laser to create multi-carrier signals. These are modulated via MZMs and combined using a WDM. The signal propagates through an FSO link with simulated atmospheric turbulence. At the ISAC Receiver, it is captured by PDs, amplified by TIAs, and processed in a DSP block to extract data and ranging/sensing information.
