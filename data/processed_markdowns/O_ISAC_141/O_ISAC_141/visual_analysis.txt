## Image 1: _page_2_Picture_9.jpeg
- **Type**: System Illustration
- **Description**: System model for ABC-enabled PD-NOMA cellular network.
- **Components**:
    - **Base Station**: Central hub.
    - **Backscatter Tags**: Distributed throughout the urban area (buildings).
    - **Users**: Categorized as "Near User" and "Far User".

## Image 2: _page_4_Figure_2.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Silhouette values vs Number of clusters (Z).
- **Key Data**: 
    - At Z=2, Silhouette value is ~1 (Optimal).
    - As Z increases (up to 15), the value fluctuates and generally decreases towards 0.87.
- **Observation**: Confirms Z=2 as the optimal number of clusters for subcarrier allocation.

## Image 3: _page_2_Figure_2.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Comparison of Aggregated Uplink EMF Exposure [Joule/kg] vs User Required bits (40k to 60k).
- **Curves**: OFDMA (Solid line) vs PD-NOMA (Dashed line).
- **Observation**: PD-NOMA shows lower EMF exposure than OFDMA due to better spectral efficiency.

## Image 4: _page_5_Figure_8.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Detailed EMF exposure comparison (with and without ABC) vs Required Bits.
- **Key Data**:
    - OFDMA Without Backscatter (top): ~10^-4 at 80k bits.
    - PD-NOMA Without Backscatter (middle): ~2x10^-5 at 80k bits.
    - **PD-NOMA With Backscatter** (bottom): ~10^-5 at 80k bits.
- **Finding**: Inclusion of backscatter (ABC) reduces EMF by ~75% compared to PD-NOMA alone at 40kb/s.

## Image 5: _page_5_Figure_10.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: EMF Exposure vs Number of Users in the network (10 to 22).
- **Key Data (at 22 users)**:
    - OFDMA Without Backscatter: ~1.5x10^-4.
    - PD-NOMA Without Backscatter: ~3x10^-5.
    - PD-NOMA With Backscatter: ~2x10^-5.
- **Finding**: 33% reduction in EMF due to ABC among PD-NOMA schemes.

## Image 6: _page_6_Figure_2.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: EMF exposure vs Required Bits for different time slot counts (J).
- **Key Data**: J=10 (top), J=20 (middle), J=30 (bottom).
- **Observation**: Spreading transmission over more time slots (higher J) significantly reduces peak and aggregated EMF exposure.

## Image 7: _page_6_Figure_9.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Verification of Fig. 4 using experimentally calculated SAR values (cheek position).
- **Curve**: PD-NOMA with Backscatter remains consistently below other schemes, validating the model's superiority.
