## Image: _page_1_Picture_12.jpeg
### Type: System Schematic

### Title: Chirp-Polarity Coded LFM Signal Generator

### Components:
- LD (Laser Diode)
- DP-QPSKM (Dual-Polarization QPSK Modulator)
- PBC (Polarization Beam Combiner)
- OF (Optical Filter)
- PolM (Polarization Modulator)
- Pol (Polarizer)

### Signal Flow:
1. LD → DP-QPSKM (driven by LFM + RF)
2. Orthogonal sidebands generation (x/y axes)
3. OF filters lower sidebands
4. PolM encodes binary data (0/1) onto chirp polarity
5. Pol combines axes for final waveform

### Key Concept:
- Chirp polarity (up/down) represents bits (1/0)
- Photonics-based generation
