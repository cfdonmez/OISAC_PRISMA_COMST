**Section (a) - Frame structure:** System Diagram.
* **Components:** Previous frame, Current frame (X-pol payload, Y-pol payload), Next frame, k slope training sequence (TS), -k slope training sequence (TS).
* **Flow:** Input optical frames are structured with a dual-polarization dual-chirp training sequence (X-pol with +k slope, Y-pol with -k slope) preceding the respective polarization payloads.

**Section (b) - t-f distribution:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Time (t). Y-axis: Frequency (f_X-SOP, f_Y-SOP). Range: 0 to 10 (normalized units).
* **Trends:** Shows linear frequency modulation (LFM) chirps. X-SOP has a negative slope (-k), while Y-SOP has a positive slope (+k), both covering the full normalized frequency band (0 to 10) over the TS duration.

**Section (c) - FrFT Processing:** Diagram.
* **Flow:** Illustrates the application of p-order and -p order Fractional Fourier Transform (FrFT) to the X and Y State of Polarization (SOP) components to transform chirps into impulse-like peaks (u, v) for synchronization and sensing.

**Section (d) - FrFT Intensity:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Samples (x100), Range: 0 to 20 (Total 2000 samples). Y-axis: Intensity (x10dB), Range: -8 to 0.
* **Data Points:** A sharp peak for the p-order FrFT is observed at sample ~1700 with an intensity of 39.8dB above the background floor.
* **Trends:** The FrFT effectively compresses the chirp signal into a high-intensity peak suitable for precise timing.

**Section (e) - Zoomed Intensity:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Samples, Range: 1637 to 1687. Y-axis: Intensity (10dB), Range: -4 to 0.
* **Data Points:** The main peak shows a height of 31.1dB. A "3dB" marker indicates the full-width at half-maximum (FWHM) of the compressed pulse.

**Section (f) & (g) - RBS retrieved:** Charts.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (km), Range: 0 to 11 km. Y-axis: Intensity (10dB), Range: -8 to 0.
* **Trends:** Displays the Rayleigh Backscattering (RBS) signal profile along the 11km fiber length, retrieved using p-order (blue) and -p order (orange) FrFT respectively, showing consistent backscatter intensity across the link.

**Section (h) - Zoomed RBS:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (km), Range: 4.0 to 4.02 km. Y-axis: Intensity (10dB), Range: -4 to 0.
* **Data Points:** Shows a 20-meter window of the backscattered signal, highlighting the spatial correlation between the two polarization/slope components used for vibration localization.

**Section (a) & (b) - Absolute tangent function:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: SOP rotation (rad), Range: π/8 to 17π/8. Y-axis: The absolute tangent function, Range: -6 to 14.
* **Data Points:** Features sharp peaks at rotation angles of ~21π/40 and ~61π/40, reaching a magnitude of ~12.
* **Trends:** Compares reference values (blue squares) with RMSE for Signal-to-Chirp Power Ratio (SCPR) of -32dB (a) and -22dB (b). Higher SCPR (-22dB) shows better alignment with references.

**Section (c) - RMSE vs SCPR:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: SCPR (dB), Range: -37 to -12 dB. Y-axis: 10log10(RMSE), Range: -2 to 2.
* **Trends:** The Mean RMSE (squares) decreases linearly from ~0.2 (at -37dB) to ~-0.4 (at -12dB). RMSE for SOP=π/40 (circles) and SOP=-π/40 (triangles) follow similar downward trends, showing SOP estimation improves with higher TS power.

**Section (d) - PSD SNR & BER vs SCPR:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: SCPR (dB), Range: -14 to 0 dB. Y-axis: PSD SNR (dB) [Left: 10 to 25], -log10(BER) [Right: 1 to 4].
* **Data Points:** At SCPR = -2dB, PSD SNR is ~23.5dB and -log10(BER) is ~2.1.
* **Trends:** Both communication quality (BER) and sensing SNR improve as SCPR increases. BER reaches 10^-2 at approximately -3dB SCPR.

**Section (e) - Normalized Intensity:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (km), Range: 0 to 11 km. Y-axis: Normalized Intensity (x10dB), Range: -8 to 0.
* **Data Points:** Peak-to-floor ratio is marked at ~17 dB, with inherent signal fluctuations of ~4.9 dB shown.

**Section (f) - Delta Phase:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (km), Range: 0 to 11 km. Y-axis: ΔPhase (rad), Range: -4 to 4.
* **Trends:** Compares single slope (red) vs. dual slope (blue) chirp TS. The dual-slope (k & -k) method significantly suppresses phase noise fluctuations over the fiber length.

**Section (g) - Vibration Waterfall:** Waterfall/Color Plot.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (m), Range: 9997 to 10007. Y-axis: Time (ms), Range: 0 to 12.
* **Trends:** Shows a localized vibration signal at ~10002.5m, oscillating over time at a constant frequency.

**Section (h) - PSD vs Length:** Chart.
* **Axes:** X-axis: Fiber length (m), Range: 9993 to 10001. Y-axis: PSD (10dB rad^2/Hz), Range: 5 to 45.
* **Data Points:** A dominant peak at 9999.7m (vibration location) shows a PSD SNR of 20.3dB (inset). The peak width at the base corresponds to a 1m spatial resolution range.

**Section (a) - Experimental Setup:**
* **Components:** Laser (Source), DP-IQM (Dual-Polarization IQ Modulator), AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator), SOA (Semiconductor Optical Amplifier), EDFA1/2/3 (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifiers), OBPF (Optical Bandpass Filter), Optical Circulator (ports 1, 2, 3), 10km SSMF, PZT (Piezoelectric Transducer), 400m SSMF, VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator), ICR (Integrated Coherent Receiver), LO (Local Oscillator), 80GSa/s DSO (Oscilloscope), Rx DSP.
* **Flow:** Signal generated by Laser and DP-IQM (driven by AWG) is amplified by SOA/EDFA1 and filtered (OBPF). It enters the 10km fiber link via Port 1->2 of the circulator. Rayleigh backscattering from the fiber (with vibration from PZT) returns through Port 2->3 to EDFA2 for the sensing receiver path. The downstream signal reaches the ICR beat with LO, digitized by DSO, and processed by Comm Rx DSP.

**Section (b) - Signal Visualization:**
* **Components:** P_X-SOP, P_Y-SOP, Frequency (f).
* **Trends:** Shows the allocation of training sequences (X-SOP TSs and Y-SOP TSs) as frequency chirps flanking the central communication payload in a 3D power-frequency-polarization space.
