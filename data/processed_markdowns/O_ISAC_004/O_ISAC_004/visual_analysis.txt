Image [_page_0_Figure_15.jpeg]: Setup/Diagram: This image illustrates an experimental setup for an optical fiber sensing and communication system.
The setup consists of:
1.  **Light Sources:** A Continuous Wave (CW) laser connected to a Dual-Polarization In-phase/Quadrature Modulator (DP-IQ-MOD), driven by an Arbitrary Waveform Generator (AWG), providing an LP01 mode. Separately, a Supercontinuum (SC) source generates both LP01 and LP11 modes.
2.  **Multiplexing:** A MUX combines the LP01 mode from the modulator and the LP01/LP11 modes from the SC source.
3.  **Sensing Element:** The combined light then passes through a fiber structure composed of Few-Mode Fiber 1 (FMF1), a Tapered Few-Mode Fiber 2 (FMF2) which serves as the sensing region where a droplet (representing an analyte) interacts with the light, and another FMF1.
4.  **Demultiplexing & Detection:** A (De)MUX separates the modes.
5.  **Receivers:**
    *   The LP01 mode is directed to an Optical Spectrum Analyzer (OSA) for spectral analysis.
    *   Another LP01 mode path goes to a Coherent receiver, connected to a Real-Time Oscilloscope (RTOS), for advanced signal processing and demodulation.
The system is designed to investigate the interaction of different optical fiber modes with an external medium in a tapered fiber structure, combining spectral analysis with coherent detection for potential sensing or communication applications.
