## Figure 1: SSMF-based PoF Experimental Setup

- **Type:** System Diagram.
- **Architecture:** 
  - **TX (Central Office):** High Power Laser Diode (HPLD, 1064nm, 10W) + Signal TX (SFP, 1310nm, 155 Mbps) -> WDM MUX.
  - **Link:** 1 km Standard Single Mode Fiber (SSMF).
  - **RX (Remote Unit):** WDM DEMUX -> Photovoltaic Power Converter (PPC) + Signal RX (SFP, 1310nm, 155 Mbps).
- **Insight:** Demonstrates simultaneous high-power delivery and standard data transmission for power grid monitoring.

## Figure 3: Transmission Performance (Power & Efficiency)
**File:** [_page_1_Figure_6.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_014/O_ISAC_014/_page_1_Figure_6.jpeg)
- **Data (HPLD Input vs. PPC Input):**
  - **10W Input:** Results in **4.9W** collected power.
  - **Efficiency:** Optical PTE remains stable at **$\approx$ 49 - 52%** across the 0-10W range.
- **Loss Breakdown:** Total loss $\approx 3$ dB (1.94dB WDM insertion, 0.8dB fiber loss, 0.2dB fusion splice).

## Figure 4: Electrical Power Conversion Results
**File:** [_page_1_Figure_8.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_014/O_ISAC_014/_page_1_Figure_8.jpeg)
- **Data:** PPC Input Optical Power vs. Converted Electrical Power.
- **Practical Result:** For 5W optical input, the PPC generates **$\approx$ 500 mW** of electrical power (Efficiency $\approx 10\%$).
- **Theoretical Gap:** Theoretical efficiency of 35% would yield $\sim 1.7W$.
