Type: System Diagram / Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Transmitter (Top):** IDFnT (IDFT flanked by theta2 and theta1 phase pre-emphasis), CP+ (Cyclic Prefix insertion), P/S (Parallel to Serial), PN+ (Phase Noise/Pseudorandom sequence).
* **Radar Path (Middle):** CP removal, (2N-1)DFT, Conjugate multiplication logic (* x ()), DFT (down arrow), IDFT (vertical arrow).
* **Communication Path (Bottom):** Sync (Synchronization), S/P (Serial to Parallel), CP- (CP removal), DFnT (DFT flanked by phase correction), IDFT.
**Flow:**
1. The transmit signal is processed via an IDFnT structure to generate an integrated waveform.
2. For Radar: The backscattered signal is processed using a (2N-1) autocorrelation logic in the frequency domain, followed by a range-Doppler estimation (DFT/IDFT).
3. For Communication: The signal is received (mobile handset icon), synchronized, and processed via a DFnT structure to recover data subcarriers.

Type: Experimental Schematic and Spectral Charts
---
**Schematic Components:**
* **Source:** ECL (External Cavity Laser), PC1 (Polarization Controller), PM (Phase Modulator), RF input.
* **Modulation:** WSS (Wavelength Selective Switch), EDFA1/2 (Amplifiers), PC2, IQM (IQ Modulator), AWG.
* **Fiber Link:** 10km SMF, EDFA3, OBF (Optical Bandpass Filter), OC1/2 (Optical Circulators), VOA (Variable Optical Attenuator).
* **THz Section:** UTC-PD (Uni-Traveling Carrier Photodiode), THz Lenses, Mixer (driven by LO), DSO (Oscilloscope).
* **Sensing Section:** Meter.
**Flow:**
The system uses optical heterodyning to generate THz signals. The PM and ECL create a multi-tone source. After processing through the IQM and fiber link, the photonic THz signal is emitted via UTC-PD, reflected from a target at 1.57m, and detected by a heterodyne receiver (Mixer/LO) for DSO capture.
**Spectral Charts:**
* **(a) PSD:** X: Frequency (-1 to 1 GHz), Y: PSD (dB). Shows a broad communications spectrum.
* **(b) Power:** X: Frequency (~193.1 to 193.25 THz), Y: Power (dBm). Shows filtered optical carriers.
* **(c) Power:** X: Frequency (192.95 to 193.4 THz), Y: Power (dBm). Displays an optical frequency comb.
* **(d) Power:** X: Frequency (193.07 to 193.27 THz), Y: Power (dBm). Highlights two optical tones separated by 0.14 THz.

Image: _page_1_Figure_16.jpeg
Type: Communication Performance Chart (BER)
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Photocurrent (mA), Range: 1.0 to 3.5 mA.
* **Y-axis:** BER, Range: 10^-3 to 10^-1.
**Data Points:**
* At 1 mA photocurrent, OFDM BER is ~0.06 and OCDM BER is ~0.05.
* At 2.5 mA, OCDM reaches the 3.8x10^-3 HD-FEC threshold, while OFDM is still at ~10^-2.
* At 3.5 mA, OCDM reaches ~2x10^-3 and OFDM reaches ~6x10^-3.
**Legend/Trends:**
* **Variables:** OFDM (blue circles) vs OCDM (orange asterisks).
* **Comparison:** OCDM consistently outperforms OFDM, achieving the FEC threshold at a significantly lower photocurrent (~2.5 mA vs > 3.5 mA).
