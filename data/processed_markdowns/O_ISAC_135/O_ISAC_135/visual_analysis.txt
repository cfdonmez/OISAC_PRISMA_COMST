## Image 8: _page_10_Figure_4.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Retrieval analysis along the CALIOP track.
- **Key Data**:
    - Left: Ash Mass loading [t/km²] vs Cloud transect [km]. Best correlation (r=0.906) between MODIS and VIIRS.
    - Right: Wind Direction and Speed profiles. Average wind speed ~11.2 m/s at 16-18 km altitude.
