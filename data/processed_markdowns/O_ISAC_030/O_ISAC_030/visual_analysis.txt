Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Optical Fiber Sensing Overview)
---
Components:
- Light Source: Broadband or tunable laser source.
- Fiber Under Test (FUT): Standard single-mode fiber or specialty sensing fiber.
- Sensing Region: Section of fiber exposed to measurand (strain, temperature, etc.).
- Detector: Photodetector or optical spectrum analyzer.
- DSP/Processing Unit: Signal analysis for parameter extraction.

Flow:
1. Light Source injects optical signal into FUT.
2. Signal propagates through sensing region.
3. Measurand modulates optical properties (phase, intensity, wavelength).
4. Backscattered/transmitted light collected by Detector.
5. DSP extracts sensing parameters from optical signal.
---

Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Distributed ISAC Architecture)
---
Components:
- Central Office (CO): Contains transmitter and receiver equipment.
- OLT (Optical Line Terminal): Manages downstream/upstream traffic.
- Splitter: 1:N passive optical splitter.
- ONUs (Optical Network Units): Customer premises equipment.
- Sensing Fiber: Fiber segments used for distributed sensing.
- OTDR Module: Optical time-domain reflectometer for sensing.

Flow:
1. CO/OLT generates communication signals for ONUs.
2. Splitter distributes signals to multiple ONUs.
3. OTDR Module injects sensing pulses during guard times.
4. Backscattered light from sensing fiber returned to CO.
5. DSP separates communication data and sensing information.
