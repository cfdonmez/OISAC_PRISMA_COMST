# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_030

## Figure 1: Reflected HGR (r-HGR) Concept
**File:** [_page_1_Picture_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_1_Picture_2.jpeg)
- **Concept:** Ring-shaped LED sections emitting four distinct carriers (modulation frequencies) enable a single PD to capture intensity variations from multiple directions.
- **Physics:** Lambertian radiation pattern where reflected light channel gain depends on emission angle, receiving angle, and distances to/from the reflecting point (Eq. 1).
- **ISAC Nature:** The lamp simultaneously performs lighting, VLC data transmission, and gesture sensing.

## Figure 2: Multi-Carrier Accuracy Advantage
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_13.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_2_Figure_13.jpeg)
- **Comparison:** Two carriers vs. One carrier.
- **Data Point:** Two carriers maintain **> 90% accuracy** at a 0.7 train set proportion, whereas one carrier drops significantly.
- **Robustness:** Even at 0.4 PTD, two-carrier accuracy stays near 90%, proving the multi-carrier design is essential for directional sensing.

## Figure 3: System Architecture
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_3_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **Transmitter:** Includes carrier frequency selecting (40, 50, 60, 70 kHz) and dimming control.
- **Receiver:** Five-stage preprocessing pipeline: Carrier filtration $\rightarrow$ Envelope extractor $\rightarrow$ Smoothing $\rightarrow$ Downsampling $\rightarrow$ Normalization.
- **Full-Duplex:** Bidirectional VLC paths (Uplink/Downlink) integrated with the HGR sensing loop.

## Figure 4: Data Preprocessing Pipeline
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_8.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_4_Figure_8.jpeg)
- **(a) Raw Data:** Pulse-wave light intensity with low-frequency fluctuations from gestures.
- **(b) Carriers:** Four band-pass filtered signals ($c_1$ to $c_4$) with peak amplitudes $\approx 0.06V$.
- **(c, d) Envelopes:** Upper-part envelope extraction followed by downsampling to **100 samples** for classification.

## Figure 5: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) Framework
**File:** [_page_5_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_5_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Structure:** Feature extractor ($G^f$), Gesture recognizer ($G^r$), and Domain discriminator ($G^d$).
- **Technique:** Uses a **Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL)** for adversarial domain adaptation, enabling "cReflexGest" to generalize across table surfaces.

## Figure 6: Confusion Matrices for bReflexGest
**File:** [_page_7_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_030/O_ISAC_030/_page_7_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **0.8 PTD Matrix:** Near-perfect recognition for LR, RL, FB, BF, UP, DN, FR, FL.
- **Error Modes:** Slight confusion between **Palm-Fist (96.9%)** and **Fist-Palm (93.8%)** at 0.8 PTD, worsening to 76.6%/85.9% at 0.2 PTD.

## Figure 7: Height Sensitivity (In-height vs. Cross-height)

- **Range:** 5 cm to 25 cm height.
- **(a) In-height:** bReflexGest approaches **100% accuracy** at all heights.
