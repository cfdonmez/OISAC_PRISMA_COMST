### Image Analysis: Experimental Setup for Integrated OWC and Signal Processing
**File:** _page_3_Figure_9.jpeg
**Type:** System Diagram / Schematic
**Components:**
- **Optical Section:** Tunable Laser Source (TLS), Arbitrary Waveform Generator (AWG), I/Q Modulator.
- **THz Section:** UTC-PD (Uni-Traveling-Carrier Photodiode) for O/E conversion, THz Horn Antenna.
- **Transmission:** Wireless link (distance d).
- **Receiver:** THz Mixer, Local Oscillator (LO), Low Noise Amplifier (LNA), Oscilloscope.
- **DSP:** Offline signal processing (resampling, equalization, phase recovery).
**Modulation:** 128 Gbit/s PDM-QPSK/16QAM signals.

### Image Analysis: Integrated Radar and Comm (ISAC) Setup
**File:** _page_13_Figure_2.jpeg
**Type:** System Diagram / Schematic
**Components:**
- **Transmitter:** Radar/Comm Tx using UTC-PD.
- **Receiver:** Comm Rx (Mixer, EA, OSC) and Radar Rx (PA, Mixer, EA, OSC).
- **Target:** Reflective object at 1 m wireless distance.
- **Signal Peaks:** Peak 1 (4.5 GHz) and Peak 2 (3.3 GHz) in the frequency domain, representing echo signals.
