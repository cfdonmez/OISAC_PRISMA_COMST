* **Layout (b):** RU1-RU5 distributed linearly from Y = -110 to +110 m. Targets show velocities of 10.0, 20.0, 30.0, and 40.0 m/s.
* **Heatmaps (c, d):** Range-Doppler plots mapping Sum Range (0 to 300 m) against Frequency (-1000 to 1000 Hz). High-intensity peaks indicate detected target echos.

Image: _page_1_Figure_0.jpeg
Type: Sensing Data Multiplexing Schematic
---
**Flow:**
1. **Input:** OFDM Frame (10 ms) with 1 ms subframes.
2. **IQ Data Acquisition:** Parallel IQ sensing data from multiple RUs (RU1, RU2, RU3).
3. **Processing:** MxN Optical Switch performs time-slot assignment (t0, t1, t2).
4. **Output:** A serialized "Sensing IQ stream" where different RUs' sensing data is interleaved in time for efficient transport to next-tier processing nodes.

Type: Network Capacity vs. Target Count Comparison
---
**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Number of moving targets [1, 2, 3, 4, 5].
* **Y-axis:** Optical Network Capacity (Gbps) [0 to 100].
**Legends:**
* **Optical Switching Aggregation:** Dark grey bars.
* **Without Aggregation:** Dotted bars.
**Data Points:**
* **1 Target:** ~5 Gbps (With) vs ~25 Gbps (Without).
* **3 Targets:** ~30 Gbps (With) vs ~50 Gbps (Without).
* **5 Targets:** ~98 Gbps (With) vs ~100 Gbps (Without).
Aggregation significantly reduces per-target transport overhead until the system approaches full capacity.
