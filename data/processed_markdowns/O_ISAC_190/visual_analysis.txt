## Image: _page_2_Figure_8.jpeg
### Type: Conceptual Diagram (Pulse Compression DAS)

### LFM Pulse Compression:
- Input: LFM pulse (wide, low power)
- Matched filter: h(t) = exp(j(2πft + πκt²))
- Output: Compressed pulse (narrow, high power)

### Benefits:
- Resolution by bandwidth, not pulse width
- Breaks distance vs resolution trade-off
- Compatible with CB-DSCM

### Key Equation:
- θ(t) = (2πft + πκt²) rect(t/T)
- κ = chirp rate
- SR = c/(2nB)

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Foundation for LFM-based DAS in IM/DD
