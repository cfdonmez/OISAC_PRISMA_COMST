# O_ISAC_142 Visual Analysis

## 1. 6G Vision and Scenarios
- **Fig. 1 (_page_1_Picture_6.jpeg)**: 6G usage scenarios hexagon.
    - Core: eMBB, mMTC, URLLC (5G).
    - 6G Extensions: Immersive Communication, Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC), Integrated AI and Communication, Ubiquitous Connectivity, Massive Communication, Hyper Reliable and Low-Latency Communication.

## 2. Historical Context
- **Fig. 2 (_page_2_Figure_1.jpeg)**: IS timeline from 1963 (Reflectarray) to 2023 (Beyond Diagonal RIS). Key milestones: IRS (2018), STAR-RIS (2021).

## 3. Reflection and Beamforming Design
- **Fig. 5 (_page_8_Figure_9.jpeg)**: Framework for IS design based on power measurements. Processes: Blind reflection, Channel recovery, and Beam training.
- **Fig. 6 (_page_10_Figure_1.jpeg)**: SNR Boost vs Elements (N).
    - CSM (Conditional Sample Mean) scales at $N^2$.
    - RMS (Random-Max Sampling) scales at $N \log N$.
- **Fig. 7 (_page_10_Figure_8.jpeg)**: Effective Channel Gain vs Measurements.
    - SDP-based recovery is the most sample-efficient.
- **Fig. 8 (_page_11_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Beam training types.
    - Far-field (DFT codebook).
    - Near-field (Polar domain codebook, range-sensitive).

## 4. Deployment Architectures
- **Fig. 9 (_page_14_Figure_1.jpeg)**: IS-integrated BS with elements embedded in the antenna radome.
- **Fig. 10 (_page_15_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Multi-IS indoor network with graph-based modeling for path optimization.
- **Fig. 11 (_page_15_Figure_9.jpeg)**: IS-integrated relay (IS controller serves as active relay).

## 5. Modulation and Sensing
- **Fig. 12 (_page_18_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Relay-type vs Transmitter-type IS. Transmitter-type supports over-the-air modulation of an unmodulated carrier.
- **Fig. 13 (_page_20_Figure_1.jpeg)**: Sensing architectures: Passive, Semi-passive (sensors on IS), Active (IS controller probes), and Target-mounted (IS on UAV).
- **Fig. 15 (_page_21_Figure_5.jpeg)**: Rate vs Sensing Accuracy (Root-CRB) tradeoff. Longer training improves sensing but reduces effective rate.
