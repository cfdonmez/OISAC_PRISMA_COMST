Type: Signal Processing Flow (Chirp Integrated)
---
**Flow:**
1. **Mutually Coherent Channels (CH1, CH2, CH3):** Bandwidth $B$, separation $F_d$. Resolution $cT/2$.
2. **Digitally Matched Filter:** Applied to each pulse. Resolution $c/2B$.
3. **Autoregression (AR):** Bridges gaps between channels. Resolution $c/2(3B+2F_d)$.
4. **Phase Noise:** Impact on the final waveform, showing slight distortion.
