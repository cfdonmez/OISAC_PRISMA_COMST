Image [_page_0_Figure_15.jpeg]: Diagram: This is a block diagram illustrating the architecture of a Radio-Over-Integrated Sensing and Communication (RO-ISAC) system. It shows four RO-ISAC transceivers (numbered 1 through 4) communicating with a single RO-ISAC receiver and a 3D positioning system.
*   **RO-ISAC Transceiver (detailed for Transceiver 2):** Input bits undergo OFDM modulation, then D/A conversion. A DC component is added before the signal drives an LED/LD for optical transmission (red dotted arrows). Simultaneously, a portion of the signal reflects off a Corner Cube Reflector (CCR) and is detected by a Photodetector (PD), then A/D converted. This reflected signal is used for cross-correlation to determine "Distance 2", which contributes to "3D positioning."
*   **RO-ISAC Receiver:** Receives transmitted signals via a PD and A/D converter, then performs OFDM demodulation to yield output bits. It also contains a CCR (with dimensions $L_x$, $L_y$, $D_r$) that reflects signals back to the transceivers (blue solid arrows).
*   **3D Positioning:** This block receives distance measurements from each transceiver and input bits, utilizing them to determine 3D position. The system integrates both communication (transmitting/receiving input/output bits) and sensing (distance measurement for positioning).
