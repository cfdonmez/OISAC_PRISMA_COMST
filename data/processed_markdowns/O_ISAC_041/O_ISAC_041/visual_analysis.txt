Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Schematic
---
### Components Identified:
1.  **Light Source:** **LTLS** (Linearly Tunable Light Source) -> **NM** (Null Modem? likely Intensity Modulator or similar, context suggests specific component). *Correction: In fiber optics, possibly Noise Module or Modulator.*
2.  **Coupler:** Splits signal into probe and LO (Local Oscillator).
3.  **Upper Branch (Probe):** **AOM** (Acousto-Optic Modulator) -> **EDFA** (Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier) -> **Circulator** (Port 1->2).
4.  **Sensing Fiber:** "Buried Communication Optical Cable" with "Probe fiber" (Yellow loop).
5.  **Return Path:** Circulator (Port 2->3) -> **EDFA** -> **Coupler** (Mixes with LO).
6.  **Detection:** **BPD** (Balanced Photodetector) -> **DAQ** (Data Acquisition) -> **Signal processing** (Laptop).
7.  **Lower Branch (Reference/LO):** Connects directly from first Coupler to the mixing Coupler.
8.  **Feedback/Control:** **BM** (Balanced Receiver/Monitor?) connected to Circulator 3 and back to LTLS (possibly for locking).

### Flow Description:
*   A coherent DAS (Distributed Acoustic Sensing) setup.
*   Light from **LTLS** is split.
*   One path (Probe) is frequency-shifted by **AOM**, amplified (**EDFA**), and launched into the "Buried Communication Optical Cable" via a **Circulator**.
*   Rayleigh backscattering from the cable returns through the **Circulator**, is amplified (**EDFA**), and mixed with the **LO** path at a **Coupler**.
*   The beat signal is detected by **BPD**, digitized by **DAQ**, and processed on a computer.
