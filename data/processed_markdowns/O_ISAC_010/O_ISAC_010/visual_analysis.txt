## Figure 1: PoF Characterization over SMF
**Files:**
- [(a) Setup Diagram](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_010/O_ISAC_010/_page_1_Figure_6.jpeg)

- **Type:** (a) Schematic, (b) Line Chart.
- **Description:** Preliminary test of 1064nm high-power fiber laser (HPFL) transmission over single-mode fiber (SMF) to identify Stimulated Raman Scattering (SRS) thresholds.
- **Data (Collected Power at 10W Input):**
  - **1 km:** ~7.6 W (Linear, no SRS).
  - **2 km:** ~5.2 W (Saturation starts at ~9W input).
  - **3 km:** ~2.9 W (Strong SRS saturation starts at ~6W input).
- **Outcome:** 1 km link chosen for subsequent multi-core experiments to maintain linearity.

## Figure 2: Integrated PoF and DMT Transmission Setup
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_0.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_010/O_ISAC_010/_page_2_Figure_0.jpeg)
- **Type:** System Diagram
- **Description:** Experimental architecture for simultaneous transmission of 60W power and high-speed data over a 7-core Weakly Coupled Multi-Core Fiber (WC-MCF).
- **Core Allocation:** 1 core for 1550nm DMT signal (IM-DD), 6 cores for 1064nm PoF signals.
- **Key Components:** AWG (120 GSa/s), MZM (45 GHz), Fan-In/Fan-Out modules, 1-km WC-7CF (41.5 µm pitch), PPC (Photovoltaic Power Converters).
- **Insets:** (a) Microscope cross-section of the 7-core fiber, (b) physical FIFO device.

## Figure 5: Communication Performance across Cores
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_010/O_ISAC_010/_page_3_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **SNR Analysis:**
  - **50 GBaud:** SNR consistently $\sim 22.0$-$23.5$ dB. 5/7 cores meet 3GPP requirement (EVM < 8%).
  - **60 GBaud:** SNR drops to $\sim 21.7$-$23.3$ dB.
  - **Feed Light Impact:** Negligible performance degradation when PoF is active.
- **BER Analysis:** All cores stay below the SD-FEC threshold ($2 \times 10^{-2}$). At 50 GBaud, BER is $\sim 3 \times 10^{-3} - 8 \times 10^{-3}$.
- **Constellations:** 64-QAM clusters remain well-defined, verifying high-speed signal integrity under 60W optical power.
