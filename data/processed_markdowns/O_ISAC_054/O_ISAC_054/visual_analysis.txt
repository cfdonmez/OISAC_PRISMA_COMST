Type: SYSTEM DIAGRAM / SCHEMATIC
---
**Components:**
*   **Ceiling Transmitters:** Multiple "O-AP" (Optical Access Point) units labeled O-AP 1, O-AP m, O-AP µ.
*   **Light Sources:** Depicted as light bulbs emitting cones of light (yellow). Detailed inset shows an "LED" on a PCB.
*   **User Devices:** Located on the floor (z=0). Labeled:
    *   "Device 1" (Laptop) with a sensor/tag.
    *   "Device 2" (Headphones) with a sensor/tag.
    *   "Device v" (Smartphone/Tablet) with a sensor/tag.
*   **Receiver:** Inset shows a "PD array" (Photodiode) package. A line connects it to Device 1.
*   **Environment:** Rectangular room with dimensions labeled Length ($\mathcal{L}$), Width ($\mathcal{W}$), and Height ($\mathcal{H}$).
*   **Coordinate System:** Cartesian axes (x, y, z). Origin (0,0,0) at the floor center. Ceiling reference (0,0,$\mathcal{H}$).

**Flow / Geometry:**
*   The O-APs are distributed on the ceiling plane (at height $\mathcal{H}$). They transmit optical signals downwards (cones of light).
*   Geometrical angles $\xi_m$ and $\epsilon$ describe the angular position/orientation of the O-APs relative to the center.
*   User devices are mobile on the floor plane. The system implies Visible Light Communication (VLC) or Optical Positioning, where the O-APs serve as anchors/transmitters and the devices receive signals via PD arrays.
