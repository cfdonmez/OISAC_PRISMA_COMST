Image _page_3_Figure_1.jpeg:
Type: Chart
Description: Plots showing (a) BER vs received optical power (ROP) at point F, (b) sensing resolution vs ROP at point G, and (c) peak-to-maximum-noise ratio (PMNR) vs ROP.
KEY DATA: Communication BER improves with higher ROP and ISAC waveform outperforms communication-only under equal power because SCM resides in low-frequency band; sensing resolution degrades sharply when ROP drops below threshold, and PMNR<6 dB indicates loss of target detectability due to SNR limits.
