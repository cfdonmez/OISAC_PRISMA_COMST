# Visual Analysis for O_ISAC_026

## Figure 1: W-Band Photonic ISAC Concept
**File:** [_page_1_Figure_7.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_026/O_ISAC_026/_page_1_Figure_7.jpeg)
- **Architecture:** Centralized Fiber-to-the-BS (Fiber-Wireless) integration.
- **Waveform:** Frequency Division Multiplexed (FDM) signal containing a Subcarrier Modulated (SCM) channel for communication and a Linear Frequency Modulated (LFM) probe for sensing.
- **Functionality:** Centralized DSP handles generation/processing; BS performs optical-to-electrical conversion; User End (UE) recovers data; BS performs photonic de-chirping for sensing feedback.

## Figure 2: Experimental W-Band ISAC Testbed

- **Spectral Allocation (Inset A):** 
  - **SCM Bandwidth ($B_c$):** 2 GHz.
  - **LFM Bandwidth ($B_r$):** 9.6 GHz.
  - **Guard Band:** 400 MHz.
  - **Total Shared Bandwidth:** 12 GHz.
- **Hardware Profile:** 
  - **Carrier:** 96.5 GHz (W-band) generated by heterodyning ECL-1 (193.1 THz) and ECL-2 (193.1965 THz).
  - **Fiber Link:** 10 km Single Mode Fiber (SMF).
  - **Wireless Link:** 1 m via Horn Antennas (HA).
  - **Modulation:** 16-QAM for communication; LFM ($T_{LFM} = 66.13$ ns) for sensing.

## Figure 3: System Performance vs. Optical Power
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_1.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_026/O_ISAC_026/_page_3_Figure_1.jpeg)
- **(a) BER Analysis:** 
  - **ISAC Waveform:** Reaches a BER floor of $\sim10^{-4}$ at Received Optical Power (ROP) $> -5$ dBm. Crosses the $10^{-2}$ threshold at ROP $\approx -24$ dBm.
  - **Comm-Only:** Exhibits worse BER at identical ROP (crosses $10^{-2}$ at $\approx -19$ dBm) due to the higher frequency components of the 48 Gbit/s signal compared to the ISAC subcarrier.
- **(b) Sensing Resolution:** 
  - Measured resolution is stable at **2.75 cm** for ROP from $-10$ to $-35$ dBm.
  - Degrades to $\approx 3.1$ cm at ROP of $-38$ dBm.
- **(c) Peak-to-Maximum-Noise Ratio (PMNR):**
  - PMNR remains high ($> 12$ dB) for ROP $> -30$ dBm.
  - Drops to the detection threshold (**6 dB**) at ROP $\approx -35$ dBm; below this, targets become indistinguishable from noise (PMNR $\approx 2.4$ dB at $-38$ dBm).

## Figure 4: Ranging Accuracy and Spectra
**File:** [_page_3_Figure_5.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_026/O_ISAC_026/_page_3_Figure_5.jpeg)
- **(a) Multi-Target Spectra:** Shows de-chirped peaks for targets at 65, 96, 114, 146, and 160 cm. Normalization demonstrates high side-lobe suppression ($> 10$ dB).
- **(b) Calibration Residuals:** 
  - **Fixed Bias:** 26 cm (removed via external calibration using a corner reflector at 114 cm).
  - **Post-Calibration Error:** Absolute errors are constrained within **$\pm 1$ cm** (specifically ranging from $-0.4$ cm to $+0.6$ cm) across the 1.6 m test range.
