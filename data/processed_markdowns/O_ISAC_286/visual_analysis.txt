## Image: _page_5_Figure_7.jpeg
### Type: MUSIC Algorithm Diagram

### Title: Multiple Signal Classification for Ranging

### Steps:
1. Radar transfer vector Hradar
2. Covariance: RHH = HradarHradar^H
3. Eigenvalue decomposition
4. Noise subspace En
5. Steering vector a(τ̂)
6. Spectrum: F = 1/(a^H En En^H a)
7. Peak search → delay τ

### Key Concept:
- Subspace orthogonality
- Super-resolution ranging
