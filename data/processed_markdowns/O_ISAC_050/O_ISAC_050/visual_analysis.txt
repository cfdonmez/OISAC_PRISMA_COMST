**Axes Extraction:**
- **X-axis:** The number of subcarriers [Range: 0 to 20]
- **Y-axis:** Performance gain [Range: 0 to 25]

**Data Points:**
- **Proposed MM-based location estimate (Pink solid line):**
    - Starts at ~1.
    - Increases roughly linearly to ~20 at 20 subcarriers.
- **Proposed MM-based orientation estimate (Green dashed line):**
    - Starts at ~1.
    - Increases roughly linearly to ~21.5 at 20 subcarriers.
- **Trend:** Both curves track each other closely, showing a significant positive correlation between the number of subcarriers used and the performance gain achieved.

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** Performance gain vs Number of Subcarriers.
- **Trend:** Increasing the number of subcarriers linearly improves the performance (gain) of both location and orientation estimation, verifying the benefit of broadband/multi-carrier signals for high-resolution sensing.
