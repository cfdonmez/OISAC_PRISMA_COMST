Image: _page_4_Figure_0.jpeg
Type: Deep Learning Architecture (Neural Network Schematic)
---
**Components:**
* **Preprocessing:** 
    * Vision -> Masking.
    * Radar -> BEV (Bird's Eye View).
    * Lidar -> RDM (Range-Doppler Map).
    * Position -> Normalization.
* **Encoding:** $3 \times 7 \times 7$ Conv followed by $1 \times 3 \times 3$ Maxpool.
* **Feature Extraction 1:** Layer 1 & Layer 2 (ResNet based).
* **Adaptive Fusion:** Transformer-Merged-Attention 1.
* **Feature Extraction 2:** Layer 3 & Layer 4 (ResNet based).
* **Adaptive Fusion:** Transformer-Merged-Attention 2.
* **Output Stage:** Shared MLP -> Beam.
**Layer Details (Inset):**
* Layer 1: Two $3 \times 64$ blocks.
* Layer 2: Two $3 \times 128$ blocks.
* Layer 3: Two $3 \times 256$ blocks.
* Layer 4: Two $3 \times 512$ blocks.
