Type: System Schematic (Fig. 1)
---
**Components & Service Area:**
- **Room Dimensions:** 4m x 4m x 3m (X from -2 to 2, Y from -2 to 2, Z from 0 to 3).
- **Transmitters:** 4 LEDs mounted on the ceiling at Z=3m. Positions: (-1,1), (1,1), (1,-1), (-1,-1).
- **Reflective Surfaces:** Dual Optical IRS arrays (IRS I and IRS II) mounted on opposing walls at the midpoints. Each array consists of 21x21 passive reflective units.
- **Reference Grid:** Location Fingerprints distributed on the floor (Z=0m plane in diagram, Z=1m plane height mentioned for targets) with spacing S=20cm.
---

Image: _page_2_Figure_11.jpeg
Type: System Schematic (Fig. 2)
---
**Propagation Geometry:**
- **Components:** LED transmitters (LED 1 to M), Optical IRS units (n_s = 1 to N_s), and Photodiodes (PD of User 1 to L).
- **Angular Definition:** 
  - phi_{m,n_s}: Emission angle from LED.
  - alpha_{m,n_s}: Incident angle at IRS unit.
  - beta_{m,n_s}: Reflection angle from IRS unit.
  - psi_{m,n_s}: Incident angle at PD receiver.
- **Flow:** Light signal from LED m -> reflect via IRS n_s -> receive at PD User l.
