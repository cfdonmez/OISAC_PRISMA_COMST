    - **Integral delay channel (Blue Square):** Sinusoidal (Phase shifted). Peak 1.0 at index 6 and 30. Minimum -1.0 at index 18.
    - **Fractional delay channel (Red Circle):** Slowly increasing from 0 (index 0) to ~0.5 (index 40).
- **Coherent bandwidth:** Indicated for the first ~12 subcarriers (from peak to null).

**Legend/Trends:**
- **Variables:** Real/Imaginary channel gain vs Subcarrier index for Integral and Fractional excess delay scenarios.
- **Trend:** Integral delays cause periodic frequency-selective fading (sinusoidal gain), while fractional delays (in this model) result in much flatter, frequency-non-selective behavior for the real part but a phase slope in the imaginary part.

- **Vectors:** Arrows showing transition from tangent space back to manifold.

**Flow:**
Illustrates a Riemannian optimization or tracking algorithm (likely a Recursive Gauss-Newton or Kalman Filter on a manifold). The algorithm calculates an update in the Tangent space (linearized) and then maps it back to the SO(3) Manifold to find the updated state R_[t+1] from R_[t].
