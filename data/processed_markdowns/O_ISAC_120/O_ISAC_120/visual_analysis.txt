Type: System Schematic and Beam Squinting Analysis
---
**Components:**
* **(a) Phased Array:** Phase shifters (φ), Amplifiers, and Antenna elements with spacing 'd'.
* **(b) Time Delay Array:** True Time Delay (TTD) units (Δt), Amplifiers, and Antennas.
* **Flow:** Signal input -> distributed to parallel branches -> processed by φ or Δt -> amplified -> transmitted.
* **Math:** Δφ = 2πf0d sinθ/c and Δt = d sinθ/c.

**Chart Extraction (c-f):**
* **Axes (c):** X-axis: θ (°) [0 to 90], Y-axis: Normalized Gain (dB) [-20 to 0].
* **Data Points (N=16, θ=30°):**
    * 32.5 GHz peak at ~26°.
    * 30.0 GHz peak at ~30°.
    * 27.5 GHz peak at ~35°.
* **Trends:** Phased arrays exhibit frequency-dependent beam squinting (deviation ~9° across 5GHz). Time-delay arrays (b) show no such dispersion, with all frequency components aligned at the target angle. Increasing antenna count N from 8 to 32 narrows the main beam from ~25° to ~6° FWHM.
