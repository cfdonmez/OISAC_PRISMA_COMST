Type: System Schematic (V2V ISAC)
---
**Components:**
* **$1^{\#}$ car:** Transmitter (ACO-OFDM-LFM) and Receiver (APD, Matched filtering, Demodulator).
* **Link:** Atmospheric turbulence channel.
* **Integrated Signal Generation:** Combines Binary data stream -> 4QAM -> Hermitian symmetry -> IFFT -> P/S + CP with LFM signal generation.
* **Integrated Signal Processing:** Demodulator path (Remove CP, FFT, 4QAM demod) and Sensing path (Matched filtering).

Image: _page_3_Figure_1.jpeg
Type: Chart (BER Performance)
---
**Axes:** X: SNR (dB) [0 to 25], Y: BER [$10^{-6}$ to $10^0$].
**Trends:**
* Compares ACO-OFDM-4QAM-LFM vs standard 4QAM-LFM under Weak, Moderate, and Strong turbulence.
* ACO-OFDM approach shows significantly better performance (lower BER) at the same SNR. For Weak turbulence, it reaches $10^{-5}$ at ~17 dB, whereas standard 4QAM-LFM needs > 23 dB.
