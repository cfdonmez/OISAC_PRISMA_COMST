# O_ISAC_148 Visual Analysis

## 1. SAR Processing Platform
- **Fig. 1 (_page_1_Figure_7.jpeg)**: ESA Geohazards Exploitation Platform (GEP) Interface.
    - **Visuals**: Map view of the Middle East (Cyprus, Lebanon, Syria) with a Sentinel-1 SAR data frame overlay.
    - **Tools**: Shows "COIN" (S-1 coherence) and "SNAC" (SNAP S-1 GRD amplitude change) processing services selected.
    - **Context**: Demonstrates the cloud-based workflow for non-expert users to process Copernicus data.

## 2. Flood Detection in Sergiopolis
- **Fig. 2 (_page_1_Figure_12.jpeg)**: Multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SAR Analysis (October 2018).
    - **(a) Pre-event ratio**: 16 vs 22/10/2018; mostly dark/neutral.
    - **(b) Cross-event RGB composite**: 28/10 vs 16/10. Red patterns indicate decreased backscatter (water presence).
    - **(c) Interferometric Coherence**: Low coherence (blue) matches the flooded areas.
    - **(d-e) Recovery Monitoring**: 03/11 showing increased backscatter (white arrows) near fortification walls, suggesting moisture or receding water.
    - **(f) Post-event ratio**: 04/11 vs 29/10; recovery phase.
    - **Map Metadata**: Scale bar 0-900m; Legend for Ratio (-47.1 to 56.2 dB) and Coherence (0 to 1).

## 3. Fire Monitoring in Apamea
- **Fig. 3 (_page_2_Figure_4.jpeg)**: Sentinel-2 Multi-temporal Spectral Analysis (May-June 2021).
    - **Format**: Grid of 6 dates (10/05, 15/05, 20/05, 25/05, 30/05, 04/06) vs 4 modalities (True-colour, NDVI, NBR, BAI).
    - **True-colour**: Shows progressive browning of the site.
    - **NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)**: Scale -1 to +1. High green (+1) in early May, fading to neutral.
    - **NBR (Normalized Burn Ratio)**: Scale -1 to +1. Sharp dark red patterns appear on 15/05 and 04/06, indicating burnt soil.
    - **BAI (Burnt Area Index)**: White patterns (values ~ -1) highlight the burnt scars, especially prominent on 15/05 and 04/06.
    - **Spatial Info**: Scale bar 1 km; yellow outline marks the archaeological site boundary.
