## Figure 2: Experimental ISAC Setup
**File:** [_page_1_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_024/O_ISAC_024/_page_1_Figure_4.jpeg)
- **Topologies:** 
  - **Transmitter:** Laser -> OC -> DP-IQM (for 2x16-QAM DSCM via AWG2) and IQM (for Pre-compensated LFM via AWG1).
  - **Fiber Link:** 39.1 km SMF + 1m PZT (200 Hz actuator) + 255 m SMF.
  - **Receivers:** Shared local oscillator (LO) feeds both the **Communication Receiver** (DP-Hybrid + BPD) and the **Sensing Receiver** (Hybrid + BPD).
- **Feedback Loop:** Communication DSP extracts the "Response Extraction" data and feeds it back to the "Distorted Probe Gen." at the transmitter (Tx DSP).

## Figure 3: Experimental Performance Metrics
**File:** [_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_024/O_ISAC_024/_page_2_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **(a) Rayleigh Traces:** Intensity vs. Distance (0-40 km). The pre-distorted (PD) probe shows a consistently higher and flatter trace compared to the standard LFM probe.
- **(b) Phase Waterfall:** 2D plot (Time vs. Distance) showing a clear sinusoidal vibration trace at the distal end (~39.1 km).
- **(c) Phase Variance:** Inset shows a **0.1 m** improvement in the falling edge resolution, supporting the 1 m spatial resolution claim.
- **(d) PSD curves:** Power Spectral Density of the 200 Hz vibration. Peak-to-base ratio is **22.1 dB** for the PD probe, showing a **2.4 dB SNR improvement** over the uncompensated LFM probe.
- **(e) Impact of STPR:** 
  - **Communication:** BER of subcarrier 2 (SC2) rises faster than SC1 as STPR increases, approaching the SD-FEC limit ($1.5 \times 10^{-2}$) at STPR = 12 dB.
  - **Sensing:** SNR improves with STPR. Optimal point is identified at STPR = 10 dB where both services coexist effectively.
