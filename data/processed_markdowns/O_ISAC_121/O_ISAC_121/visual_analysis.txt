Type: Advanced System Schematic (S-DNN for Sensing)
---
**Content:**
* Illustrates a Spectro-Diffractive Neural Network (S-DNN) architecture for wireless sensing.
* **Input:** Incident broadband wave ($k_z$) from a target signal source.
* **Architecture:** Multi-layer intelligent surfaces (diffractive layers) that perform optical/RF signal processing.
* **Processing:** Includes 1D/2D estimation modes, backpropagation training with task loss, and FDTD (Finite-Difference Time-Domain) validation loop to correct model deviations.

Type: Experimental Setup and Matrices
---
<b>Panels:</b>
* (a) **Hardware components:** Horn antennas (target sources), VNA (Vector Network Analyzer), S-DNN stack (labeled Input Plane, layers, Output Plane), and a mechanical probe for spatial sampling.
* (b) **Evaluation Matrices:** Confusion matrix and Energy matrix for two targets across 10 detection regions ($0-9$). High diagonal values in the confusion matrix indicate accurate target spatial interval detection.
* (c) **Angle Sensitivity:** Plots Energy ratio (dB) vs Incident planewave angle ($^\circ$) [-5 to 5]. S-DNN shows sharper peaks compared to standard Lens.
* (d) **Bandwidth Analysis:** Plots Bandwidth (GHz) vs Target number. Shows that a Five-layer S-DNN maintains higher bandwidth (> 7 GHz) for multiple targets compared to a Single-layer version.
