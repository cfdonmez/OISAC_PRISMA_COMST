## Image: _page_7_Figure_3.jpeg
### Type: Multi-Panel Diagram (TDS-SS Algorithm Principle)

### Panel (a) - Original CCF:
- Target peak with sidelobes
- Sidelobes at τ₀, τ₁, 2τ₀, 2τ₁, τ₂
- τ₂ = LCM(τ₀, τ₁) has higher sidelobe

### Panel (b) - Delayed CCF (by τ₀):
- Shifted version aligns sidelobe with target

### Panel (c) - Subtraction Result:
- Negative values at sidelobe positions
- Set negative to zero → suppress sidelobes
- δT threshold for pulse width filtering

### Algorithm Steps:
1. Delay by τ₀, τ₁, τ₂
2. Subtract to identify sidelobe positions
3. Zero out negative positions
4. Filter pulses narrower than MLW

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Novel sidelobe suppression for chaotic DFRC
- Leverages TDS knowledge for cleanup
