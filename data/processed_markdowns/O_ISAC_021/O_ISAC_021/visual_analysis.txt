## Figure 4: Numerical Performance Metrics
**File:** [_page_4_Figure_1.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_021/O_ISAC_021/_page_4_Figure_1.jpeg)
- **Type:** Line Graphs (Monte-Carlo Simulation).
- **(a) BER vs SNR:** 
  - **Clipping Effect:** For $b=1\sigma$ (low DC bias), non-negative clipping causes an error floor at $10^{-1}$ (4QAM) and $10^{0}$ (16QAM).
  - **Optimal Bias:** $b=2\sigma$ or $3\sigma$ allows BER to reach $10^{-5}$ at ~11 dB (4QAM) and ~20 dB (16QAM).
- **(b) RMSE vs SNR:** 
  - **Threshold Effect:** Sharp transition to precision region. 
  - **Robustness:** Sensing is more resilient to low DC bias than communication.
  - **Precision:** Converges to an RMSE of ~0.05m (5 cm) for a 1 GHz bandwidth signal over a 200m FSO link (normalized latency estimation).
