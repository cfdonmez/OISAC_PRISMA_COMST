Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Fiber-Wireless ISAC for 5G/6G Fronthaul)
---
Components:
- Central Office (CO): BBU pool, OLT.
- Feeder Fiber: 25 km SMF-28.
- Remote Radio Head (RRH): ONU, LNA, PA.
- Analog RoF Link: 1550 nm, DWDM 100 GHz grid.
- Antenna Array: 8×8 massive MIMO.
- User Equipment (UE): Multiple mobile devices.
- Radar Target: Vehicle in coverage area.
- Frequency: mmWave 28 GHz / sub-6 GHz dual-band.

Sub-components:
- MZM: Mach-Zehnder modulator at CO.
- EDFA: Booster and pre-amplifier.
- PD: High-speed photodetector at RRH.
- DSP: Centralized at CO for joint processing.

Flow:
1. BBU → MZM → EDFA → Feeder Fiber → RRH.
2. RRH: PD → PA → Antenna Array → UE + Radar targets.
3. Return: Antenna → LNA → PD → Fiber → CO → DSP.

Type: Chart (Fiber Dispersion Impact on mmWave)
---
Axes:
- X-axis: Fiber Length (km). Range: 0 to 50 km.
- Y-axis: RF Power Penalty (dB). Range: 0 to 20 dB.

Legend:
- 28 GHz (Blue).
- 39 GHz (Red).
- 60 GHz (Green).

Data Points:
- At 10 km: 28 GHz = 1.5 dB; 39 GHz = 3 dB; 60 GHz = 7 dB.
- At 25 km: 28 GHz = 4 dB; 39 GHz = 8 dB; 60 GHz = 18 dB.
- At 50 km: 28 GHz = 8 dB; 39 GHz = 16 dB (saturates).

Observation:
- Higher mmWave frequencies suffer more dispersion penalty.
- 28 GHz viable to 40+ km; 60 GHz limited to <15 km.
