Type: System Diagram / Schematic (VLC-based Positioning Architecture)
---
Components:
- LED Array: 4 ceiling-mounted LEDs at known positions (L1, L2, L3, L4).
- Photodetector (PD): Receives optical signals for positioning/communication.
- ADC: Digitizes received signals.
- DSP Unit: Performs RSS estimation and trilateration.
- User Device: Mobile terminal with integrated PD.
- Ceiling Reference Frame: Coordinate system origin at room corner.

Flow:
1. LEDs transmit unique modulated signals (FDMA or TDMA separated).
2. PD on user device captures superimposed optical signals.
3. ADC digitizes the received waveform.
4. DSP separates LED contributions via filtering/correlation.
5. RSS from each LED is computed.
6. Trilateration algorithm estimates 2D/3D user position.
7. Communication data is simultaneously decoded from the modulated signals.

Image: _page_2_Figure_21.jpeg
Type: System Diagram / Schematic (Lambertian Emission Model)
---
Components:
- LED: Point source with Lambertian radiation pattern.
- Emission Angle (φ): Angle from LED normal to receiver direction.
- Irradiance Angle (ψ): Angle from receiver normal to LED direction.
- Distance (d): Euclidean distance from LED to PD.
- Receiver Aperture: Effective collection area Aeff.
- FOV (Field of View): Half-angle acceptance cone of PD (Ψc).

Parameters:
- Lambertian Order (m): Derived from LED half-power angle (Φ1/2).
- m = -ln(2) / ln(cos(Φ1/2)).
- Typical Φ1/2 = 60° → m = 1.
- For Φ1/2 = 30° → m ≈ 4.82.
