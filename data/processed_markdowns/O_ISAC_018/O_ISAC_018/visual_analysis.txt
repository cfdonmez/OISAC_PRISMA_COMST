## Figure 3 & 4: System Architecture and Detailed Setup
**Files:** [_page_2_Figure_21.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_018/O_ISAC_018/_page_2_Figure_21.jpeg), [_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_018/O_ISAC_018/_page_3_Figure_2.jpeg)
- **Setup:** A single narrow-linewidth laser source is split for both Sensing (ICR LO) and Transmission.
- **Pathways:** 1.6 GBaud 16QAM signal (AWG) is modulated onto a 50 MHz LFM carrier (AWG). 
- **Fiber Link:** 10 km FUT ends with a 10 m PZT vibration source.
- **Sensing:** RBS signal is captured by a polarization-diverse coherent receiver (ICR + RTO2).
- **Communication:** Optical signal detected by PD, amplified by LNA, and transmitted over 1 m wireless mmW (28 GHz) channel to a Telecom Receiver.

## Figure 9 & 10: Communication and Nonlinearity Mitigation
**Files:** [_page_5_Figure_4.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_018/O_ISAC_018/_page_5_Figure_4.jpeg), [_page_5_Figure_8.jpeg](file:///g:/Drive'ım/AKU_WorkSpace/survey_fdgit/OISAC_PRISMA_COMST/data/processed_markdowns/O_ISAC_018/O_ISAC_018/_page_5_Figure_8.jpeg)
- **Carrier Comparison:** LFM carrier outperforms CW carrier at low CSPR (high power) by suppressing Stimulated Brillouin Scattering (SBS).
- **Distortion:** BER degrades at low CSPR due to CDM modulator nonlinearity (small-signal approximation $\sin(x) \approx x$ fails).
- **Optimization:** 
  - Adjusting voltage ratio **$\alpha = 3.2$** (optimal) minimizes BER.
  - **Probabilistic Constellation Shaping (PCS)** (with $\lambda=0.05$) and pre-compensation improve BER well below the 7% FEC threshold.
