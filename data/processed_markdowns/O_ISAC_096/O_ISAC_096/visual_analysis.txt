---
**Components:** 
* **808 nm Laser:** Excitation source.
* **PEG-Coated Gold Nanoantennas:** Functionalized sensing elements.
* **Drug-loaded Thermosensitive Liposomes:** Delivery mechanism.
* **Doxorubicin (Nucleus):** Visualized in fluorescent microscopy (blue/purple).
