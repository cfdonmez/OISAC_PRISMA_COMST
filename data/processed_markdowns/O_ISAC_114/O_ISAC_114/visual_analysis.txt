Type: System Schematic & Component Models (Fig. 1)
---
**Components & Architecture (a):**
- **Transmitter:** OFDM Modulator, D/A Converter, Drive Circuit, Laser Diode (LD), Optical Power Splitter (OPS).
- **Receiver (EH):** Photovoltaic (PV) cell connected to a Resistor/Load.
- **Receiver (ISAC):** Corner Cubic Reflector (CCR) for sensing, Photodiode (PD1) for communication, A/D Converter, OFDM Demodulator.
- **Sensing Path:** Reflected light from CCR captured by PD2, leading to Cross Correlation and Distance Estimation.

**Functional Models (b-f):**
- **(b) OFDM Modulation:** Data in -> QAM mapping -> Training sequence -> Hermitian symmetry -> IFFT -> Add CP.
- **(c) OFDM De-modulation:** Remove CP -> FFT -> Channel estimation & Equalization -> QAM de-mapping -> Data out.
- **(d) OPS Model:** Splits incident beam into sub-beams with factors µ (ISAC) and 1-µ (EH).
- **(e) Sensing Model:** Approximates LD as point source; CCR with diameter D reflects signal back for ToF estimation.
- **(f) PV Circuit:** Equivalent model with photogenerated current (Iph), dark current (I0), shunt resistor (RSH), and series resistor (RS).

Image: _page_7_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: System Schematic (Experimental Layout)
---
**Components & Flow:**
- **Source:** PC -> AWG & OSC -> Electrical Amplifier (EA) -> Bias-Tee.
- **Optical Front-end:** Red/Green/Blue LD coupled with OPS.
- **Splitting:** OPS diverts beam 90° to PV (connected to Resistor/Power meter) for EH.
- **Link:** Incident beam travels 1.5m to Rx unit containing APD and CCR.
- **Sensing/Comm:** Rx OSC captures PD1 signal; reflected signal from CCR captured by PD2 (APD) for ranging analysis.
