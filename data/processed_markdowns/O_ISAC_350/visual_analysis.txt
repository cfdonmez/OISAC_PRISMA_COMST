Key Technical Findings:
1. **Monolithic Integration:** Single GaN-on-Sapphire chip performs both LED (Tx) and PD (Rx) functions.
2. **Spectral Overlap:** 30 nm overlap between Emission and Detection spectra allows self-detection.
3. **Sensing:** Successfully tracked rotation speeds up to 319 rpm using reflected blue light.
4. **Communication:**
   - Tx Mode: 25 Mbps (limited by 23 MHz BW).
   - Trx Mode (Loopback): 5 Kbps (limited by weak reflected signal and Rx sensitivity).

Key Innovations:
1. Use of GaN MQW for dual-function ISAC without external photodiodes.
2. Low-cost, compact "Naked Eye" ISAC chip.
