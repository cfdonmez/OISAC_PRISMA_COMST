Type: System Schematic and DSP Block Diagram
---
**Experimental Setup:**
* **Components:** 100 kHz ECL, DP-IQ Modulator, AWG, RF Amplifier, SSMF, PZT (Vibration source), VOA, EDFA, Local Oscillator (LO) ECL, Integrated Coherent Receiver (ICR), 64 GSa/s RTO.
* **Flow:** Tx-DSP prepares 16QAM signals with FPTs. The optical signal undergoes SSMF transmission and PZT vibration. At the Rx, the ICR performs coherent detection, followed by Rx-DSP which separates communication and sensing tasks.
**DSP Blocks:**
* **Tx-DSP:** PRBS -> 16QAM -> Nyquist -> Mux -> FPTs Insertion.
* **Rx-DSP:** FPT-Based FOE & CPE -> Communication path (Pol. Demux, Equalization) and Sensing path (Down sampling, BPF).
