Image: _page_3_Picture_13.jpeg
Type: System Schematic (Dual-Polarization ISAC Setup)
---
**Components:**
- **ISAC Tx (a):**
    - **ECL1, ECL2, ECL3:** External Cavity Lasers (source).
    - **I/Q Modulator:** Driven by AWG (Arbitrary Waveform Generator).
    - **EDFA:** Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier.
    - **IL:** Interleaver.
    - **OC1, OC2:** Optical Couplers.
    - **PC1, PC2:** Polarization Controllers.
    - **SMF1, SMF2:** Single Mode Fibers.
    - **VOA1, VOA2:** Variable Optical Attenuators.
    - **PD1, PD2:** Photodiodies.
    - **LNA1, LNA2:** Low Noise Amplifiers.
    - **OMT1:** Orthomode Transducer (combines H and V polarizations).
    - **LCA1:** Lens coupled Antenna.
- **Sen./Com. Rx (b):**
    - **LCA2:** Receiver Lens coupled Antenna.
    - **OMT2:** Splits received H/V polarizations.
    - **HM1, HM2:** Harmonic Mixers.
    - **RF Source:** Local Oscillator for D/C (Down-conversion).
    - **EA1, EA2:** Electronic Amplifiers.
    - **OSC:** Oscilloscope (captures waveform).
    - **DSP:** Digital Signal Processing unit.

**Flow:**
The Tx generates independent H-path and V-path signals from partitioned laser sources. These are amplified and emitted via LCA1. The Rx captures reflections (Sen.) and line-of-sight signals (Com.) via LCA2. OMT2 splits signal polarizations for parallel down-conversion in harmonic mixers (HM1/2) before final digitization in the OSC and DSP analysis.
