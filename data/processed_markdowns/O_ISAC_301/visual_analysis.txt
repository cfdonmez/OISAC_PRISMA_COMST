### Type: System Architecture Diagram

### Title: Experimental Setup for W-Band ISAC

### Central Office (CO):
- ECL @ 193.1 THz
- AWG (60 GSa/s)
- EA + MZM
- Circulator pair

### RRU (Remote Radio Unit):
- Circulator + PC + PM-EDFA
- PM-OC (50:50 split)
- LO @ 193.1965 THz → 96.5 GHz
- PD (100 GHz) + PA
- Horn antenna (Tx)
- Servo turntable (azimuth)

### Sensing Path:
- Second horn antenna (Rx)
- LNA + ED
- MZM (dechirp modulation)
- Back to CO via fiber
- OBF + PD (10 GHz) → FFT

### User Equipment (UE):
- Horn antenna + LNA + ED
- OSC for demodulation

### Transmission:
- 10 km SMF + 1.15 m wireless

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Distributed sensing architecture
