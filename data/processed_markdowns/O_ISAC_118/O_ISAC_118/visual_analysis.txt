Type: System Schematic (MIMO-Radar Configuration)
---
**Components & Architecture:**
- **MIMO RADAR:** Central processing unit for waveform design and signal extraction.
- **RF CHAINS:** Interface between digital radar module and analog antenna array.
- **Antenna Array:** 16-element Uniform Linear Array (ULA) with critical spacing (d = λ/2).
- **Environment:** Urban intersection featuring a cyclist target.
- **Sensing Output:** $\hat{\theta}$ representing the estimated Angle of Arrival (AoA).

**Signal Flow:**
Independent waveforms are generated in the MIMO RADAR, passed through RF CHAINS, and emitted by the transmitter array. Reflected signals from the cyclist are captured by the receiver array, processed back through RF CHAINS to the MIMO RADAR for data-dependent beamforming (Capon/APES) to conclude the $\hat{\theta}$ estimation.
