## Image: _page_4_Figure_3.jpeg
### Type: System Block Diagram

### Title: Photonics-Assisted SIC for IBFD ISAC Transceiver

### Components:
- Transmitter:
  - LD0s (3 laser diodes)
  - WDM (wavelength multiplexer)
  - MZM0 (positive quadrature bias)
  - PD0 + PA
  - Tx/Rx Antennas
- Receiver (Comm + Radar):
  - LNA + ADC
  - MZM1/MZM2 (negative quadrature bias)
  - VOA + VOTDL (amplitude/delay matching)
  - WDM + PD1/PD2
  - Mixer (de-chirp for radar)

### Signal Flow:
1. CE-LFM-OFDM ISAC → MZM0
2. 3 optical signals: Tx, Comm CR, Radar CR
3. Rx signal = SI + SOI → MZM1/2
4. Counter-phase cancellation
5. SOI recovery for comm/radar

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Complete IBFD ISAC transceiver
