### Panel (a): Scenario Diagram
*   **Entities:**
    *   **Central Office:** Connected via optical fiber to "Extended Sensor" and "BS" (Base Station).
    *   **BS (Base Station):** Wireless connection to vehicles and users.

### Panel (b): Signal Principle
*   **Top Plot (FMCW?):** Frequency (f) vs Time (t).
    *   **Reference:** Red line (Linear chirp up).
    *   **Echo:** Green dashed line (Delayed copy).
    *   **Parameters:** Bandwidth ($B$ or $\Delta f$), Time duration ($\Delta t$), IF frequency ($f_{IF}$).
*   **Bottom Plot:** Similar structure showing different slopes/allocations.

### Panel (c): Resource Allocation Grid (9 Cases)
*   **Grid:** 3x3 matrix labeling "Case 1" to "Case 9", plus "Case 0" separate.
*   **Axes per Case:** Frequency ($f$) [Y-axis] vs Time ($t$) [X-axis].
*   **Blocks:**
    *   **Purple (Communication):** Occupies specific time-frequency slots.
    *   **Yellow/Orange (Radar):** Occupies slots with Chirp signal (斜线).
*   **Allocation Trends:**
    *   **Case 0:** pure Communication (All grid cells purple).
    *   **Case 1, 2, 3 (Top Row):** Radar occupies top-left sub-band. Bandwidth increases (1->3?).
    *   **Case 4, 5, 6 (Middle Row):** Radar occupies larger time/freq blocks.
    *   **Case 7, 8, 9 (Bottom Row):** Radar dominates, occupied bandwidth is maximized (Case 9 is full Radar?).
*   **Trade-off Arrows:**
    *   **Right Vertical Arrow:** "Resolution" increases downwards (Case 1 -> 7). Implies more Radar Bandwidth = Better Resolution.
    *   **Right Vertical Arrow (Up):** "Data Rate" increases upwards (Case 7 -> 1). Implies more Comm Bandwidth = Higher Rate.

### Panel (a, b, c): Waveform Modes
*   **Case 3-FDM only (Blue):**
    *   **Spectrum:** Two distinct disjoint bands. **SCM** (Single Carrier Modulation?) on left, **LFM** (Linear Frequency Modulation) on right.
    *   **Time Domain:** Constant envelope noise-like signal.
*   **Case 7-TDM only (Orange):**
    *   **Spectrum:** Broad overlapping bands?
    *   **Time Domain:** Distinct temporal slots. **LFM** pulse (first half) -> **SCM** block (second half).
*   **Case 5-Hybrid (Teal):**
    *   **Spectrum:** SCM and LFM bands adjacent/overlapping.
    *   **Time Domain:** Mixed structure.

### Panel (d): Experimental Setup
*   **Transmitter (Central Office):**
    *   **AWG:** Drives **MZM-1**.
    *   **ECL-1:** External Cavity Laser source.
    *   **MZM-1:** Modulator.
    *   **VOA:** Variable Optical Attenuator at point A.
*   **Link:** 10km Optical Fiber.
*   **BS/RRU (Base Station/Remote Radio Unit):**
    *   **Circulator:** 3-port.
    *   **Loop:** **MZM-2** -> **PM-EDFA** -> **PC** (Polarization Controller) -> **Coupler** (fed by **ECL-2** Local Oscillator) -> **PD** (Photodiode) -> **PA** (Power Amp) -> **Tx Antenna**.
    *   **Return Path:** **Rx Antenna** -> **LNA** -> **ED** (Envelope Detector?) -> **MZM-2** (closes loop?).
*   **Wireless Link:** 1m distance to **Target** (Triangle).
*   **Receiver (UE):**
    *   **Antenna** -> **LNA** -> **ED** -> **EA** -> **ADC & DSP**.
*   **Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Chain:**
    *   Common: Resample -> Synchronization -> TDM Filtering.
    *   Branch 1 (FDM Filtering): Volterra Equal. -> Down Convert -> Down-sampling Equal. -> QAM DeMod. -> BER Calculation.
    *   Branch 2: Volterra Equal. -> Down Convert...

### Panel (a): Total BER vs Case
*   **X-Axis:** Case Number (0, 1, 2, ..., 8).
*   **Y-Axis:** Total BER (Log scale: 1E-4 to 1E-2).
*   **Data Points:**
    *   Case 0: ~3E-3. (Constellation shown: somewhat noisy).
    *   Case 6: **Lowest BER** (~1.5E-4). (Constellation shown: very clear/tight clusters).
    *   Case 8: ~4E-3. (Constellation: noisy).
*   **Threshold:** Dashed line at 1E-2 (FEC limit?). All cases are below this limit.

### Panel (b): SNR vs Case
*   **X-Axis:** Case Number (1 to 9).
*   **Y-Axis:** SNR (dB) [Range: 15 - 27].
*   **Trend:** SNR increases as Case number increases (more Radar bandwidth/power?).
    *   Case 1: ~15.5 dB.
    *   Case 3: ~22 dB.
    *   Case 9: ~26.5 dB.
*   **Inset:** Frequency Spectrum (Amp vs MHz). Shows "Noise" floor and sharp "Signal" peak at ~1300 MHz.

### Panel (c): Resolution vs Case
*   **X-Axis:** Case Groups (1-3, 4-6, 7-9).
*   **Y-Axis:** Resolution (cm) [Range: 0 - 5].
*   **Data:**
    *   Cases 1, 2, 3: Resolution **~3.75 cm** (Blue bars).
    *   Cases 4, 5, 6: Resolution **~1.875 cm** (Yellow bars).
    *   Cases 7, 8, 9: Resolution **~1.25 cm** (Red bars).
    *   **Interpretation:** Resolution improves (value decreases) as bandwidth increases (Cases 7-9 have highest bandwidth).

### Panel (d): BER vs ROP
*   **X-Axis:** ROP (Received Optical Power?) (dBm) [-16 to 0].
*   **Y-Axis:** BER [0 to 0.015].
*   **Curves:**
    *   **Case 5 (Black Square):** Best performance (Lowest BER). Hits 0 BER/cutoff around -8 dBm.
    *   **Case 3 (Red Circle):** Middle performance.
    *   **Case 7 (Blue Triangle):** Worst performance. High BER until very high power.
    *   **Case 0 (Green Inverted Triangle):** Good performance, close to Case 5.

### Panel (e): SNR vs ROP
*   **X-Axis:** ROP (dBm) [-40 to -15].
*   **Y-Axis:** SNR (dB) [-5 to 27].
*   **Trend:**
    *   **Case 9 (Green):** Highest SNR (~25dB at -20 dBm).
    *   **Case 7 (Blue):** Medium SNR (~21dB at -20 dBm).
    *   **Case 5 (Black) & Case 3 (Red):** Lowest SNR (~19dB at -20 dBm).
    *   **Note:** Radar SNR (e) and Comm BER (d) show trade-offs. Case 9 is best for Radar SNR but might be worst for Comm BER (not plotted in d?). Case 5 is best for Comm BER but lowest for Radar SNR.

### Panel (f): Range Profile
*   **X-Axis:** Frequency (MHz) [0 - 1500].
*   **Y-Axis:** Amplitude (dB).
*   **Peaks:** Sharp peaks at different frequencies corresponding to Target Distances.
*   **Inset:** Error (cm) vs Target Distance (m). Error fluctuates around 0 cm (acc +/- 1cm).
