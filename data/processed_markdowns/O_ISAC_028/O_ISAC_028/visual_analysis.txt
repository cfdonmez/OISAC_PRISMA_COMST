Image _page_1_Figure_15.jpeg:
Type: Diagram
Description: Pairwise coding (PWC) and decoding block diagrams showing symbol pairing, phase rotation, I/Q interleaving, and SNR-aware reconstruction.
KEY DATA: Transmitter rotates symbols by 45°, interlaces I/Q to map one stream onto "good" subcarriers and the other onto "bad" ones; receiver rescales by measured SNRs then performs ML detection to neutralize color-SNR effects in S-CADD.
