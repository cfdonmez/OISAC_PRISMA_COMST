Type: System Architecture Schematic (S-DNN for Sensing)
---
**Components:**
* **Target Signal Source:** Transmitter node emitting broadband incident waves.
* **Input Plane:** Coordinate system (x, z) where the incident wave enters the processing volume.
* **Passive Intelligent Surfaces (S-DNN):** Cascaded diffractive layers (phase/amplitude masks) designed as a Spectro-Diffractive Neural Network.
* **Output Plane:** Detector array (labeled 0-9) for spatial-spectral output.
* **Far-field Plane:** Projection region showing 1D and 2D estimation modes within the "Super-oscillatory region."

**Operational Flow:**
Broadband wavefronts are incident on the S-DNN stack. The layers perform a learned diffraction-based transformation that maps specific Direction-of-Arrival (DOA) angles to designated spatial pixels on the output plane. This allows for high-precision DOA estimation without active RF chains at the sensor front-end.

**Axes Extraction:**
* **X-axis:** Incident planewave angle (°) | Range: **-5° to +5°**.
* **Left Y-axis:** Energy ratio (dB) [for S-DNN] | Range: **0 to 3 dB**.
* **Right Y-axis:** Energy Ratio (dB) [for Lens] | Range: **0 to 15 dB**.

**Data Points & Trends:**
* **S-DNN (Blue, starred):** Displays a "Super-oscillatory" characteristic with 7 distinct sharp peaks. Peaks are located at precisely 0°, approx ±1.5°, ±2.7°, and ±4.2°. The peak-to-null ratio is approx 1.5-2.0 dB within the sub-diffraction-limit region.
* **Standard Lens (Orange, circles):** Displays a classical diffraction-limited Airy-like response with a single broad central peak (~4° FWHM) centered at 0°.
* **Comparison:** The S-DNN enables sub-wavelength/sub-diffraction angular discrimination that is over 5x finer than the equivalent aperture lens.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Application Scenario & Performance Chart
---
**System Scenario (RIS-aided ISAC):**
* **Elements:** Base station -> Obstruction -> RC RIS -> User (moving vehicle).
* **Mechanism:** The base station transmits to an RC RIS (Reflection-Control Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface). A passive S-DNN estimates the DOA of the user. An FPGA consumes this DOA data to track the user with a focused beam, overcoming the NLOS (Non-Line-of-Sight) condition.

**Detected Amplitude Chart:**
* **X-axis:** User angle (°) | Range: **-15° to +12°**.
* **Y-axis:** Detected amplitude (dB) | Range: **-80 to -50 dB**.
* **Trend Comparison:**
    * **With S-DNN (Blue line):** Extremely stable detected power at approx **-56.5 dB** across the entire 27° angular sweep.
    * **Without S-DNN (Orange line):** Significant power fluctuations and lower average amplitude (ranging from **-70 to -78 dB**). 
* **Conclusion:** The S-DNN integration provides a **~15-20 dB link budget improvement** and eliminates tracking-induced fading.
