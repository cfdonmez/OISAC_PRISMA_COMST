Type: Application Scenario Schematic
---
**Components:**
* **Vehicle with OW-ISAC transceiver:** Primary mobile unit.
* **Cooperative target (Truck):** Equipped with a CCR (Corner Cube Retroreflector) and a "PD array" with "MRC" (Maximal Ratio Combining) and a "Lens array".
* **Uncooperative target (Van):** Tracked via diffuse reflection.
* **LiDAR unit (Inset):** Details the scanning mechanism with a rotating mirror and PDs.
**Signal Flow:** Transceiver sends "Transmitted optical signal" (orange) -> signal hits Cooperative/Uncooperative targets -> "Reflected optical signal" (green) returns to the transceiver for Sensing. Simultaneously, the signal carries data for Communication to the cooperative target's PD array.

Image: _page_3_Figure_2.jpeg
Type: Detailed Transceiver Block Diagram
---
**Components:**
* **OW-ISAC Transceiver (Tx):** ACO-OFDM/DCO-OFDM modulation, Hermitian symmetry, IFFT, Clipping, DAC, Laser Driver, Laser.
* **OW-ISAC Transceiver (Rx):** Photo-detector, ADC, Removing CP & FFT, Element-wise division, IFFT, Distance estimation.
* **OWC Receiver (Target):** Photo-detector, ADC, CCR, Removing CP & FFT, Pairwise averaging, ML detection, ACO/DCO demodulation.
**Flow:** Bits -> Tx processing -> Laser -> Channel -> (1) Reflection off CCR -> Rx processing -> Distance estimation. (2) Reception at OWC receiver -> Rx processing -> demodulation.

Image: _page_4_Figure_22.jpeg
Type: Chart (PSD of OFDM/Clipping Noise)
---
**Axes:** X: Subcarrier Index $k$ (0 to 60), Y: PSD.
**Panels:** 
* **(a) Conventional:** Uniform PSD for OFDM signal and Clipping noise (~$8 \times 10^{-4}$ and ~$3.5 \times 10^{-4}$ respectively).
* **(b) Proposed:** PSD increases with $k$ for signal (up to ~$2.3 \times 10^{-3}$) while clipping noise PSD decreases (from ~$4.5 \times 10^{-4}$ to ~$2 \times 10^{-4}$). This indicates an optimized power allocation strategy.

Type: Chart (Normalized PDF of $L_t(D)$)
---
**Axes:** X: $L_t(D)$ (normalized distance error) (0 to 3), Y: Normalized $P(L_t(D))$ (0 to 1).
