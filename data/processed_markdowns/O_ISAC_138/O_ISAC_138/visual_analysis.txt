# O_ISAC_138 Visual Analysis

## Image 1: _page_2_Figure_13.jpeg
- **Type**: System Diagram / Illustration
- **Description**: System architecture of U2U mmWave communication empowered by multimodal perception.
- **Components**:
    - Transmitter UAV: mmWave Transmitter, GPS.
    - Receiver UAV: mmWave Receiver, GPS, FMCW Radar, 3D LiDAR, 360° RGB Camera.
    - Environment: Urban scenario with buildings and vehicles.
    - Link: mmWave Communication Link between UAVs.

## Image 2: _page_4_Figure_3.jpeg
- **Type**: System Block Diagram
- **Description**: Overall architecture of MPFusionNet.
- **Workflow**:
    - Preprocessing: Semantic Segmentation (Image), Frame Alignment (LiDAR), Dynamic Feature Removal (Radar), Temporal Interpolation (GPS).
    - Stages: Dual-stage feature extraction & fusion (Layer-1/2/3 Blocks and MPFusionNet modules).
    - Components: ResNet-18, ASPP (Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling), SE (Squeeze-and-Excitation), Multi-Head Attention, MLP.
    - Output: Beam Classification MLP predicting the optimal beam.

## Image 7: _page_10_Figure_12.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph (Box Plot)
- **Description**: Beam prediction accuracy distribution across different Scenarios (1-4) and Scenario All.
- **Observations**: Scenario 1 has the highest variance (78-99%). Scenario All median is ~95%.

## Image 8: _page_11_Figure_5.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph (Box Plot)
- **Description**: Comparison of Top-1 to Top-3 accuracy for Image, Lidar, Radar, and Fusion.
- **Key Data**: Fusion (red) significantly outperforms single modalities. Top-1 Fusion ~78%, Top-3 Fusion ~95%. Single modalities (Image, Lidar, Radar) are all below 60% for Top-1.

## Image 9: _page_11_Figure_9.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph (Box Plot)
- **Description**: Top-3 accuracy of two-modality fusion schemes.
- **Key Data**: RGB + LiDAR (~94.6%) is the best-performing pair, followed by RGB + Radar and RGB + GPS.

## Image 10: _page_11_Figure_11.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph (Box Plot)
- **Description**: Top-3 accuracy distributions under (a) Daytime and (b) Nighttime conditions.
- **Observations**: Daytime median is higher (~96% for RGB+LiDAR+Radar) compared to Nighttime (~93% for LiDAR+Radar+GPS). Nighttime performance relies more on non-visual sensors.

## Image 11: _page_12_Figure_4.jpeg
- **Type**: Chart / Graph
- **Description**: Top-3 accuracy under different RGB occlusion levels (0% to 40%).
- **Observations**: Schemes involving RGB (red, blue, green) drop from ~95.8% to ~91.5%. The LiDAR + Radar + GPS scheme (purple) is robust and remains flat at ~94.2%.
