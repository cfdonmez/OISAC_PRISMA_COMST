One input to the MZ Modulator comes from an Electrical Frequency Modulator (modulating a Triangle Pulse Generator with 1 GHz frequency, 10 GHz constant). The other input is a User Defined Bit Sequence NRZ Pulse Generator (0101 sequence, 1 Gbps bitrate) passed through a Rectangular Electrical Filter (11 GHz bandwidth). The modulated optical signal then goes through a Phase Modulator and is split for distribution. Oscilloscopes and Electrical Gain elements are present for monitoring and signal conditioning.

The electrical outputs are processed by an Electrical Subtractor and Electrical Norm block. The processed sensing signal is analyzed by an Oscilloscope Visualizer 2 and RF Spectrum Analyzer 1.

This delayed signal then enters an Optical DPSK Receiver (Reference Wavelength 193 THz) for demodulation. The electrical output from the receiver is filtered by a Low Pass Rectangular Filter (3 GHz cutoff), processed by an Electrical Abs block, an Electrical Multiplier, and an Electrical Reciprocal block. The final communication signal is analyzed by an RF Spectrum Analyzer and Oscilloscope Visualizer 1.
*   **Line Types:** Optical fiber is represented by green lines, and electrical cable by blue lines, showing signal flow between components.
