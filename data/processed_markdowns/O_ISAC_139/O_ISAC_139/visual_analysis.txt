## Image 3: _page_4_Figure_1.jpeg
- **Type**: Flowchart
- **Description**: MODE+MEAN Filter Flow algorithm.
- **Steps**:
    1. Input: last 20 RSSI measurements.
    2. Count appearances of RSSI values in the window.
    3. Sort in descending order of frequency.
    4. Calculate arithmetic mean of the top 3 most frequent values.
    5. Return result.

## Image 4: _page_4_Figure_11.jpeg
- **Type**: Flowchart / Algorithm
- **Description**: Beacon Selection Algorithm for trilateration.
- **Logic**:
    - Input: Signal and metadata (LoS/NLoS, mean strength) of available Beacons.
    - Sorting criteria: 1) LoS-NLoS status (Priority to LoS), 2) Mean RSSI Descending.
    - Selection: Top 3 beacons are chosen.
    - Output: Beacons for trilateration.
