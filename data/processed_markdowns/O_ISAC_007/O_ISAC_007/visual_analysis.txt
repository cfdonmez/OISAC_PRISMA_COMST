IMAGE: _page_2_Figure_9.jpeg
TYPE: Performance Charts
DESCRIPTION: Coupling efficiency and fringe contrast as functions of working distance and tilt angle.
PANELS:
- (a) Efficiency vs Working Distance (mm): Peak efficiency ~100% at 5.5136 mm. Efficiency drops to 20% at 5.4248 mm and 5.6016 mm (yielding a ~0.18 mm dynamic range).
- (b) Fringe Contrast vs Working Distance (mm): Peak contrast 0.816 at 5.5136 mm. Stays above 0.8 within the range 5.348 mm to 5.6784 mm.
- (c) Efficiency vs Angle (degrees): Peak at 0 deg. Drops to 20% at -3.842 and 3.842 degrees (angular tolerance of +/-3.84 deg).
- (d) Fringe Contrast vs Angle (degrees): Displays a twin-peak structure. Values: 0.85 at 0 deg, peaking at ~0.9 near +/-4 deg, and dropping to 0.8 at -4.842 and 4.842 deg.
