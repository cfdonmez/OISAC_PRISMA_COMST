## Image: _page_8_Figure_8.jpeg
### Type: Flow Diagram (Communication Demodulation)

### Processing Steps:
1. IQ Down-conversion to baseband
2. De-chirp with multiple slopes
3. FFT for slope matching (peak comparison)
4. Down-sampling at matched slope rate
5. FFT for circular shift index recovery
6. Symbol decoding

### Key Principle:
- Slope-matched de-chirp → single-tone
- Slope-mismatched → LFM residue
- Circular shift → frequency in de-chirped signal

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- LoRa-like demodulation for Doppler tolerance
- Index modulation enables ISAC
