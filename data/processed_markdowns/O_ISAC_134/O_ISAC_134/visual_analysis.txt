## Image 1: _page_2_Picture_1.jpeg
- **Type**: System Diagram / Schematic
- **Description**: Overview of the multimodal transformer (MMT) system for RF beam prediction in vehicular communications. It shows the fusion of radar data (Range-Azimuth and Range-Velocity maps) and LiDAR data (3D point cloud) processed through an MMT.
- **Key Data**:
    - Modalities: 24 GHz FMCW Radar, 3D LiDAR.

- **Extracted Content**:
    - Inputs: Radar sensor data, LiDAR sensor data.
    - Processing Blocks: Multimodal Transformer, Beam Prediction head.
