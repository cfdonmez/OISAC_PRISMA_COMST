## Image: _page_8_Figure_6.jpeg
### Type: Chart (Frequency Response - Low-Pass Filter Effect)

### Axes:
- X-axis: Frequency (Hz)
- Y-axis: Amplitude |H(f,T_e)|

### Key Features:
- Sinc-shaped frequency response
- Main lobe width: 1/T_e
- Communication signal at f_c
- Sensing signal at f_x·v

### Transfer Function:
H(f, T_e) = exp(-jπfT_e) · sin(πfT_e)/(πf)

### Key Insight:
- Higher frequencies = more attenuation
- Trade-off: Short T_e → better comm, worse sensing
- Long T_e → better sensing (brightness), worse high-speed comm

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- Exposure time optimization crucial for ISAC performance
- Communication and sensing have conflicting requirements
