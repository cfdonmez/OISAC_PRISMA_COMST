## Image: _page_2_Figure_8.jpeg
### Type: Algorithm Diagram

### Title: Learned Digital Back-Propagation (LDBP) Principle

### Structure:
- Deep Neural Network based on Split-Step Fourier Method (SSFM)
- Layers: Linear Step (D) -> Nonlinear Step (N)
- **D-Step:** Dispersion compensation (Trainable weights W)
- **N-Step:** Nonlinearity compensation (Activation function g(x) = x * exp(-j * alpha * |x|^2))

### Key Concept:
- Unfolding the SSFM algorithm into a trainable neural network to compensate Fiber Nonlinearity (SPM) + Dispersion (CD) simultaneously.

### O-ISAC Relevance:
- DSP technique for enhancing Comms SNR in ISAC waveforms
