Type: Folded Reflectarray Imaging System Model
---
**Components & Schematic Flow:**
* **VNA:** Connected to Ports 1 and 2 of the feed.
* **Dual-pol Horn Antenna:** Centrally located, source of x-polarized waves.
* **Polarization Conversion Metasurface:** 34 x 34 randomly encoded array for phase control.
* **Polarizer Grid:** Placed at focal distance F/2; reflects x-pol, transmits y-pol.
* **Imaging Plane:** Located at 150 mm distance; where objects are placed.
* **Signal Flow:** 
    1. Horn radiates x-pol waves (blue).
    2. Polarizer Grid reflects x-pol back to the Metasurface.
    3. Metasurface converts to y-pol (red) with 1-bit encoding (0/180°).
    4. Y-pol waves transmit through the grid to the Object.
    5. Scattered y-pol waves return to the Horn for RX processing.

**Radiation Patterns (Heatmaps):** 
* Shows frequency-diverse field distributions at **f1, f2, f3** for both Tx and Rx paths. Tx patterns are complex/disordered, while Rx is more focused.

Type: Metasurface Unit Cell Configuration
---
**Geometry & Dimensions:**
* **Structure:** Single-layer cat-eye patch on Rogers 4003C substrate with a copper ground.
* **Periodicity (p):** **4.0 mm**.
* **Substrate Thickness (h1):** **1.524 mm**.
* **Pattern Parameters:** $R_1=1.46, R_2=1.36, R_3=1.21, R_4=1.1, w_1=0.76, L_1=0.8, L_2=0.3$ (mm).
* **Encoding States:** 
    - **State 0 (Red):** 0° reference phase.
    - **State 1 (Blue):** 180° phase shift (achieved by 90° rotation).

    - **Bandwidth:** 13 GHz to 31 GHz (shaded).
    - **PCR:** **Above 0.90** throughout the operational band.

    - **$R_{xx}$ (Cross-pol):** Dips below **-10 dB** with multiple resonances.
