# Protocol amendment — OSF registration lineage and executed-review reconciliation

Date: 2026-08-07  
Status: reporting correction and retrospective registration-lineage clarification

The review was retrospectively registered on the Open Science Framework on 12 February 2026 (registration `7f6wb`; <https://osf.io/7f6wb>; DOI <https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/7F6WB>). At registration, search and screening had already been completed, the review was in synthesis and manuscript drafting, and the record described 221 included studies. The record is therefore a retrospective registration and must not be described as a prospective preregistration.

The OSF record is a predecessor snapshot of the same O-ISAC review lineage. Its frozen files are historical rather than the canonical final-release protocol/corpus statement, and they are internally inconsistent about the date window: the registration description says 2020–2025, while an attached protocol proposes searching from 2000 and retaining eligible pre-2020 functional O-ISAC studies. The review was re-baselined in June–August 2026. Primary evidence was fixed at 1 January 2020–22 June 2026, with pre-2020 material restricted to separately labelled contextual/background use. The executed final search used Scopus, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, Wiley Online Library and Taylor & Francis Online through 22 June 2026. The final flow comprised 1,733 records identified, 1,259 screened, 330 reports sought, 58 not retrieved, 272 full-text reports assessed, 39 full-text exclusions, six contextual reports, and 227 eligible reports mapped to 206 included studies.

The OSF record specified independent duplicate human processes, third-reviewer arbitration, qualitative publication-bias assessment, sensitivity checks, and cross-study rate–range–resolution scatter/frontier analysis by modality. Across the executed final corpus, the workflow was investigator-supervised, AI-assisted, user-delegated and claim-governed; routine independent duplicate human review and third-reviewer arbitration were not performed. The implemented TQAF was a deterministic review-specific eight-dimension technical evidence and reporting appraisal. No formal missing-results/publication-bias assessment or formal sensitivity analysis was performed. A single cross-study frontier was not admissible across heterogeneous tasks, units, measurement planes and conditions; trade-offs were instead synthesized as 404 source-grounded condition-aware records grouped into prespecified families.

The OSF 221-study figure and the final 206-study figure use different search states and denominators and are not a direct attrition sequence. This amendment changes reporting only and does not alter the locked eligibility decisions, report-to-study mapping, extracted data, TQAF results or synthesis.
