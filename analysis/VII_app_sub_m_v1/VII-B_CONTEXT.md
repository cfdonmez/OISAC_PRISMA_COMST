VII-B. Indoor Environments focuses on O-ISAC deployments where indoor optical infrastructure must co-deliver communication service and environment-aware sensing from the same transceiver chain [O_ISAC_011] [O_ISAC_030]. In this vertical, one representative motif is indoor localization, where retroreflective VLC links use transmitted and reflected optical signals to support 3D positioning while retaining communication operation in the same system workflow [O_ISAC_011]. A second motif is human-centric interaction, where VLC-capable desk lamps are engineered to recognize in-air hand gestures while still carrying concurrent VLC traffic, enabling practical integrated control scenarios in occupied rooms [O_ISAC_030]. At deployment level, the evidence consistently reports communication-plane continuity under sensing operation and sensing-plane utility for localization or gesture-state extraction, which matches the application intent of indoor integrated services rather than standalone sensing or standalone links [O_ISAC_011] [O_ISAC_030].
