VII-A. Smart Infrastructure & Outdoor Urban Sensing-Communication (SCENARIOS_12)

Scenario 1: Outdoor mobility corridors with vehicular and UAV-assisted optical links
Scenario vector s: V2V distance variation and multipath geometry are reported, while UAV backhaul adds time-varying flight geometry and weather-driven attenuation under changing visibility [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005].
Sensing task + sensing metric: Target/background discrimination is quantified by target-channel received-power-ratio trends and RMS delay-spread behavior in V2V modes; atmospheric-channel sensing is realized via backscattered-light feedback for FSO channel-gain estimation [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005].
Communication task + comm metric: The communication objective is robust data delivery under mobility, reported via V2V transmission-quality behavior and end-to-end edge-user throughput under FSO backhaul-capacity constraints [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005].
Dominant component label: Conventional (no ORIS/OPA explicitly evidenced). Justification: the processed texts describe VLC/FSO/RF transceiver and control mechanisms, but do not explicitly identify ORIS or OPA [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005].
Representative works: Representative studies include visible-light V2V channel characterization and mixed FSO-RF UAV trajectory optimization [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005].

Scenario 2: Fiber-corridor sensing-communication for metro and industrial infrastructure
Scenario vector s: Demonstrated deployments use 10-10.4 km fiber spans with static infrastructure geometry and interference-fading effects in Rayleigh backscatter traces [O_ISAC_038] [O_ISAC_074].
Sensing task + sensing metric: The sensing task is distributed vibration monitoring, reported with 1 m spatial resolution and interference-fading suppression through reduced trace-intensity fluctuation [O_ISAC_038] [O_ISAC_074].
Communication task + comm metric: The communication task is coherent payload transport, evidenced by 16-QAM transmission at 50-60 GBaud with negligible or limited additional penalty in coexistence operation [O_ISAC_038] [O_ISAC_074].
Dominant component label: Conventional (no ORIS/OPA explicitly evidenced). Justification: both implementations rely on coherent-fiber DSP and waveform processing without explicit ORIS or OPA components [O_ISAC_038] [O_ISAC_074].
Representative works: Representative demonstrations include NOMA-based DAS coexistence and endogenous training-sequence reuse during coherent transmission [O_ISAC_038] [O_ISAC_074].
