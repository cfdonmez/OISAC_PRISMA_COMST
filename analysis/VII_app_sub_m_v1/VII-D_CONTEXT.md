Section VII-D is locked to the `underwater_harsh` vertical, where O-ISAC is deployed for both underwater wireless links and subsea infrastructure monitoring [O_ISAC_127], [O_ISAC_220]. In this setting, optical propagation is strongly shaped by salinity gradients, turbulence, and temperature-driven disturbances, so deployment value comes from coupling communication and sensing functions to track environmental variation during operation [O_ISAC_127]. The same vertical also covers telecommunication submarine-cable infrastructure used for subsea monitoring, where sensing information is carried alongside communication data in shared physical links [O_ISAC_220]. Representative deployment motifs therefore include secure underwater optical link support, environment-aware marine monitoring tied to salinity and temperature behavior, and SMART-style subsea cable monitoring through in-line sensing joints [O_ISAC_127], [O_ISAC_220]. When metrics are reported, comm-plane evidence is framed as coherent high-rate optical transmission, while sensing-plane evidence is framed as in-line temperature or environmental monitoring over subsea assets [O_ISAC_220].
