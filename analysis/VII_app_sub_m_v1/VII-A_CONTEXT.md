VII-A. Smart Infrastructure & Outdoor Urban Sensing-Communication (Context)

Urban smart-infrastructure deployments use O-ISAC when the same optical stack must sustain service traffic while exposing environmental or channel state for control decisions [O_ISAC_005] [O_ISAC_276]. Representative works in this vertical span visible-light V2V operation, weather-adaptive UAV backhaul, and live access-network supervision in operational fiber systems [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005] [O_ISAC_276]. In the vehicular case, V2V channel characterization reports distance-dependent target/background behavior and delay-spread differences between sensing modes, indicating deployment sensitivity to traffic geometry and range [O_ISAC_003]. In mixed FSO-RF access, adverse weather attenuation and backscattered-light feedback are explicitly used for adaptive UAV resource control and trajectory updates [O_ISAC_005]. In XGS-PON supervision, monitoring and sensing are integrated with legacy OTDR in live networks, with reported event-readout accuracy and negligible baseline traffic-impact in tested conditions [O_ISAC_276]. Throughout VII-A, sensing plane and communication plane are reported separately at scenario level [O_ISAC_003] [O_ISAC_005] [O_ISAC_276].
