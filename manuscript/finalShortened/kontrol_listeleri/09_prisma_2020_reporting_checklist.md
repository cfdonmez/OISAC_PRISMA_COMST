## Review Identity

- [ ] The manuscript clearly identifies itself as a systematic review.
- [ ] The review objective is stated explicitly and consistently across abstract, introduction, and methodology.
- [ ] The review scope is clearly bounded and aligned with the protocol.
- [ ] The time window, domain scope, and source types are stated without ambiguity.

## Protocol and Registration

- [ ] Protocol registration is reported with registry name, ID, and access path.
- [ ] The manuscript states where the protocol can be accessed.
- [ ] Any deviations from the registered protocol are disclosed and explained.
- [ ] The protocol description in the manuscript matches the frozen review workflow.

## Information Sources

- [ ] All formal databases used for the canonical PRISMA flow are listed.
- [ ] The last search date is reported for each formal source.
- [ ] Supplementary or monitoring-only sources are clearly separated from canonical PRISMA sources.
- [ ] The manuscript does not imply that non-canonical sources contributed records if they did not.

## Search Strategy

- [ ] The search logic is reported with enough detail to be reproducible.
- [ ] Any limits or filters are declared and justified.
- [ ] Search terms, field restrictions, or query templates are available in the manuscript or supplement.
- [ ] PRISMA-S expectations for search transparency are satisfied.

## Eligibility Criteria

- [ ] Inclusion criteria are explicit, technical, and domain-specific.
- [ ] Exclusion criteria are explicit, non-overlapping, and operational.
- [ ] The manuscript explains how borderline cases were handled.
- [ ] The criteria distinguish true target studies from adjacent but out-of-scope studies.

## Selection Process

- [ ] The number of reviewers at title/abstract screening is stated.
- [ ] The number of reviewers at full-text eligibility assessment is stated.
- [ ] The manuscript explains whether reviewers worked independently.
- [ ] The disagreement-resolution process is described.
- [ ] Pilot calibration or reviewer alignment is reported if used.

## PRISMA Flow Integrity

- [ ] The PRISMA flow diagram is present and readable.
- [ ] The manuscript counts match `screening/prisma_flow_counts.csv`.
- [ ] The identification, deduplication, screening, eligibility, and inclusion counts are internally consistent.
- [ ] The text and the figure report the same final included-study count.
- [ ] The upstream reconstruction limits are not overstated as stronger evidence than the archive actually supports.

## Full-Text Exclusions

- [ ] Full-text exclusions are listed with study identifiers.
- [ ] Each excluded full-text study has a concrete exclusion reason.
- [ ] The manuscript explains the transition from full-text assessed to final included.
- [ ] PRISMA 2020 Item 16b is satisfied by citing or listing excluded near-eligible studies with reasons.

## Included Studies Ledger

- [ ] The final included corpus is explicitly defined.
- [ ] The manuscript states where the full included-study ledger can be found.
- [ ] All included studies are traceable through a ledger, appendix, supplement, or equivalent archive.
- [ ] The included-study ledger is synchronized with `screening/included_studies_canonical.csv`.
- [ ] Study identifiers used in the manuscript are stable and consistent.

## Included Studies vs Main References

- [ ] If the number of included studies exceeds the number of references in the main bibliography, the manuscript explains why.
- [ ] The manuscript distinguishes between the full included corpus and the subset of studies cited narratively in the main text.
- [ ] The full set of included studies is available in a supplement or appendix if not all appear in the main bibliography.
- [ ] The wording does not imply that only the bibliography-visible studies were included in the review.
- [ ] PRISMA 2020 Item 17 is satisfied through main text, appendix, or supplementary material.

## Study Characteristics

- [ ] Key characteristics of included studies are presented in a structured table, ledger, appendix, or figure.
- [ ] The reported study-characteristic fields are enough to support synthesis decisions.
- [ ] The manuscript makes clear how studies were grouped for later syntheses.
- [ ] Representative studies are not presented in a way that hides the full study pool.

## Data Collection and Extraction

- [ ] The manuscript explains how data were extracted from included studies.
- [ ] The number of reviewers involved in extraction is reported.
- [ ] Any reconciliation or adjudication process is described.
- [ ] Any assumptions made for incomplete or unclear data are disclosed.

## Quality Assessment

- [ ] The quality or risk-of-bias framework is clearly defined.
- [ ] The assessment dimensions are explained.
- [ ] The manuscript states how many reviewers performed the assessment.
- [ ] The scoring or judgment logic is reproducible.
- [ ] The coverage of quality assessment across the included corpus is reported accurately.

## Synthesis Transparency

- [ ] The manuscript explains how studies were grouped into each synthesis.
- [ ] It is clear which studies contribute to which tables, taxonomies, or evidence summaries.
- [ ] Quantitative coverage statements are traceable to the included corpus.
- [ ] Narrative synthesis claims are not presented as if they covered more studies than they actually did.
- [ ] The manuscript distinguishes representative examples from corpus-wide evidence.

## Data Availability and Audit Trail

- [ ] Screening records, exclusion logs, and included-study ledgers are archived and traceable.
- [ ] The manuscript states where supporting data or materials can be accessed.
- [ ] File-level evidence used for the review is consistent with the manuscript text.
- [ ] Audit or reconstruction limitations are acknowledged where relevant.

## Manuscript-Specific Risk Flags

- [ ] The reader can understand how the review moved from screened records to the final included corpus.
- [ ] The reader can understand why the final included count may be larger than the main bibliography count.
- [ ] The manuscript does not leave ambiguity about whether uncited included studies were actually analyzed.
- [ ] Any appendix or supplementary ledger referenced in the methodology is actually present in the submission package.
- [ ] No unresolved PRISMA-related cross-reference remains in the final PDF build.

## Acceptance Gate

- [ ] The PRISMA counts, exclusion log, and included corpus are mutually consistent.
- [ ] The manuscript is defensible under PRISMA 2020 Items 16a, 16b, 17, 24, and 27.
- [ ] A reviewer can trace the evidence path from flow diagram to included-study ledger without guessing.
- [ ] The review package is ready for PRISMA-focused editorial scrutiny.
