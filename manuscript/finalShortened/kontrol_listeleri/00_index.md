# IEEEtran Checklist Paketi

Bu klasor, [New_IEEEtran_how-to.tex](C:/Users/fdonmez/Documents/githubRepos/manuscript/finalManuscript/New_IEEEtran_how-to.tex) kilavuzundan turetilmis parcali kontrol listelerini icerir.

2. `02_body_sections_checklist.md`

4. `04_tables_checklist.md`
5. `05_references_and_citations_checklist.md`
6. `06_equations_and_math_checklist.md`

9. `09_prisma_2020_reporting_checklist.md`

Dosya kapsamlari:

- `01_front_matter_checklist.md`: baslik, yazarlar, affiliation, running head, abstract, keyword
- `02_body_sections_checklist.md`: giris paragrafi, section/subsection yapisi, listeler, govde akisi
- `03_figures_checklist.md`: figur ekleme, caption-label sirasi, cross-reference, gorsel dosya kontrolu
- `04_tables_checklist.md`: tablo kurulumu, caption-label sirasi, `Table~\ref{...}` kullanimi
- `05_references_and_citations_checklist.md`: `\cite`, bibliyografya, BibTeX, accent kullanimi
- `06_equations_and_math_checklist.md`: equation ortamlari, numbering, multiline, matrices, cases, math tipografi
- `07_back_matter_and_biographies_checklist.md`: acknowledgments, bibliography sonu, biyografi/foto
- `08_final_pass_checklist.md`: son kalite guvencesi ve toplu kontrol
- `09_prisma_2020_reporting_checklist.md`: systematic review reporting, PRISMA flow integrity, excluded studies, included-study ledger, and supplement/bibliography consistency
- `10_prisma_flow_evidence_map.md`: repo-based PRISMA evidence map separating row-backed stages from reconstructed upstream stages
- `11_section3_impact_matrix.md`: Section III claim-by-claim edit map showing which PRISMA statements are safe, need softening, or require supplement anchoring

Kaynak section haritasi:

- Front matter: `How to Create Common Front Matter`
- Body: `How to Create Common Body Elements`
- References/back matter: `How to Create Common Back Matter Elements`
- Math: `Mathematical Typography and Why It Matters`
- Genel hata onleme: `Additional Advice`
- Son kontrol: `A Final Checklist`
