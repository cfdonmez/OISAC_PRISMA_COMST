## Stronger wording when you want to disclose reconstruction boundaries

`The canonical aggregate PRISMA counts were reconciled against stage-specific audit artifacts. In the current frozen repository snapshot, the late-stage records directly back the full-text assessment, full-text exclusion, and final inclusion ledgers, whereas earlier search-stage counts are documented through the best available reconstruction artifacts rather than a fully preserved raw export bundle.`
