Dear Editor-in-Chief,

\begin{spacing}{1.25}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication: A Systematic Review of Fiber, Free-Space, VLC/LiFi, and Photonic-THz Platforms, for consideration as a review article in \textit{IEEE Photonics Journal}.

While O-ISAC is emerging as a core 6G enabler, the field remains fragmented across different optical modalities. To bridge these research silos, we conducted a PRISMA-compliant systematic review of 220 studies, delivering a cross-modality taxonomy and a standardized reporting contract to harmonize metrics across fiber, free-space, and THz platforms.

We believe this roadmap addresses a significant gap for the journal’s audience by providing the engineering discipline needed to navigate multi-platform optical networks. This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors.

\end{spacing}
