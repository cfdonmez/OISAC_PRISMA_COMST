# Contextual-Synthesis Reporting Disposition — 2026-08-17

## Purpose

This dated reporting disposition supersedes only the reader-facing carrier
requirement in the 13 August 2026 contextual-synthesis positioning update. It
does not alter the source-function audit, the 24 sources displayed in Table I,
the 206-study primary evidence denominator, or the 227-report study lineage.

## Decision

Table I remains the complete reader-facing positioning device in the main
article. It groups 24 full-length or independently citable syntheses into six
navigation families without quality ranking or a present-review score.

The wider 38-source contextual-synthesis matrix will not be submitted as
Electronic Table ST-RS1 or as an annex. Its CSV, XLSX, and QA records are
retained internally for provenance, source-function auditing, and future
verification. They are excluded from the journal supplement allowlist and are
not referenced from the article.

## Rationale

The matrix duplicates detail already reduced to a reader-oriented form in
Table I and adds avoidable reporting burden. Removing it from the reader
package improves the survey's reading flow without concealing primary
evidence: the 38 records are contextual syntheses and remain outside the
locked 206-study technical denominator.

PRISMA study visibility and synthesis traceability continue to be supplied by
ST-01, the 227-report lineage, S-Appraisal, S-Evidence, and S-Bodies. No
included study, eligible report, synthesis membership, or report-specific
source locator is removed by this decision.

## Supersession boundary

The sentence in the 13 August update requiring Electronic ST-RS1 to retain all
38 rows is superseded. All other source-audit findings and the dated
manuscript-stage positioning amendment remain part of the internal audit trail.
