\section*{Purpose and Counting Boundary}

This supplement identifies and characterizes every study included in the
systematic survey. The review unit is the unique study: 227 eligible reports
were mapped to 206 studies, so companion reports do not appear as independent
technical demonstrations. Each study row cites its designated primary report
and every eligible companion report. ST-01B exposes the exact evidence,
metric, and tradeoff provenance of the 21 companions. A historical
\texttt{pending\_human} token triggers exclusion only when no resolved or
approved adjudication is recorded; report-specific conflict guardrails remain
in force after adjudication.
