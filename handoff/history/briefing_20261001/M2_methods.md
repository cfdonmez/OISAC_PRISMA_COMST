The pre-pilot form covered bibliographic identity, optical modality/architecture, communication and sensing tasks and metrics, measurement plane, tradeoffs, validation/scenario, enabling technologies, comparability, reproducibility, benchmark readiness, limitations, and source/verification notes. The operational form became the workbook’s study master, report lineage, long-form evidence, metric and tradeoff sheets, codebook, review/QA sheets, and claim-governance sheets. A **five-study/six-report** pilot preceded full-corpus extraction. The old standalone `data_extraction.csv` and `01_veri_cekme_formu.md` were preserved as scaffolds, not overwritten as the final extraction authority. Core record fields include study/report IDs and roles; source report and page/section/table/figure/equation locators; modality and integration; sensing and communication functions; metric name, definition, domain, value, unit, measurement plane, condition set, comparator, normalization and admissibility; tradeoff axes/objective/constraints; validation, artifacts, source conflicts, extraction and verification statuses. Blank means not yet reviewed in the baseline; post-review missingness uses `NR`, `NA`, or `UNC`. Unsupported inference and undocumented calculated/digitized values were prohibited.
