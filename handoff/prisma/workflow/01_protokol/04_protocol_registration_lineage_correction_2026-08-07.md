## Why this amendment is required

The review has an accepted public OSF registration:

- title: *Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC): A PRISMA-Guided Systematic Survey*;
- OSF registration: `7f6wb`;
- registration date: 12 February 2026;
- URL: <https://osf.io/7f6wb>;
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/7F6WB>.

The registration was created after the review had begun. At registration, the record stated that searching and screening were complete, the review was in synthesis and manuscript drafting, and the then-current corpus contained 221 included studies. It is therefore a **retrospective registration**, not a prospective preregistration.

Earlier current-project text saying that the review was not externally registered is superseded for reporting purposes. The dated initial protocol and the immutable OSF record remain unchanged as historical evidence.

### Item 24c

> The retrospective OSF record captured a legacy review state with a 30 November 2025 last-search date and 221 included studies. Its frozen materials were internally inconsistent about the date window: the registration description characterized the corpus as 2020–2025, whereas an attached protocol proposed searching from 2000 and retaining eligible pre-2020 functional O-ISAC studies. The review was subsequently re-baselined in June–August 2026, with the primary-evidence window fixed at 1 January 2020–22 June 2026 and pre-2020 material restricted to contextual/background use. The executed search used six sources and produced 227 eligible reports mapped to 206 included studies. Planned independent duplicate human review, third-reviewer arbitration, formal publication-bias assessment, and formal sensitivity analysis were not implemented across the final corpus. The planned cross-study rate–range–resolution scatter/frontier analysis was also not implemented because heterogeneous tasks, units, measurement planes, and conditions did not support a single admissible frontier; trade-offs were instead synthesized as 404 source-grounded, condition-aware records grouped into prespecified families. The executed workflow was investigator-supervised, AI-assisted, user-delegated, and claim-governed, and technical evidence was appraised with a deterministic review-specific eight-dimension TQAF. These departures and their rationales are reported transparently; the OSF 221-study figure is a superseded legacy snapshot with different denominators and is not presented as direct attrition to the final 206-study universe.
