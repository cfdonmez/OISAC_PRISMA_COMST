# Bilgi Notu 02 - PICO, PCC ve Soru Kurma

Bu review icin ana cerceve PICO degil PCC - Population / Concept / Context olarak tutulur. PICO, mudahale-etki sorulari icin yararli olsa da O-ISAC for 6G calismasi cross-modality taxonomy, metric reporting, validation maturity ve benchmark readiness odakli oldugu icin PCC daha uygundur.

## PCC Cercevesi

| PCC bileseni | O-ISAC review karsiligi |
|---|---|
| Population | Peer-reviewed studies on Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication / O-ISAC systems |
| Concept | Cross-modality O-ISAC architectures, taxonomy, sensing/communication metric reporting, metric-governed comparison, rate–sensing tradeoff synthesis, enabling technologies, application mapping, validation maturity, and benchmarking readiness |
| Context | 6G-oriented optical platforms, including fiber, FSO, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, and hybrid optical communication-sensing systems |

## Manuscript-ready Main Research Question

How has Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC) been investigated across fiber, free-space optical, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, and hybrid optical platforms, and what do existing peer-reviewed studies reveal about cross-modality taxonomy, sensing and communication metric reporting, rate–sensing tradeoffs, enabling technologies, application domains, validation maturity, benchmarking readiness, and remaining research gaps for 6G-oriented O-ISAC systems?

## Ikincil Soru Alanlari

1. O-ISAC modalities nasil siniflandirilabilir?
2. Architecture and integration mechanisms hangi ortak kaliplari gosterir?
3. Sensing ve communication metrics hangi kosullarda raporlanmistir?
4. Rate–sensing ve rate–resolution tradeoffs hangi modality ve task duzeylerinde tartisilabilir?
5. Validation maturity ve benchmark readiness acisindan kanit gucu nasil dagilir?
6. Enabling technologies ve application domains 6G O-ISAC roadmap icin hangi bosluklari isaret eder?

## PICO Ne Zaman Gerekli Olabilir?

Bu calismada PICO ana framework degildir. Ancak belirli alt analizlerde, ornegin bir waveform design yaklasiminin belirli bir sensing veya communication metric uzerindeki etkisi incelenecekse, PICO mantigi yardimci olabilir:

- P: specific O-ISAC platform or modality
- I: architecture, waveform, signal processing, or integration mechanism
- C: baseline design, separate sensing/communication design, or alternative architecture
- O: communication and sensing metrics

Bu alt kullanim, ana PCC yapisini degistirmez.

## Uygun Soru Ozellikleri

- O-ISAC terimini ve optical platform baglamini acik tutar.
- 6G-oriented context ile sinirlandirilir.
- Taxonomy, metric reporting, comparability, validation maturity ve benchmark readiness hedeflerini birlikte tasir.
- Nicel pooling varsaymaz; structured narrative synthesis ve scoping-style mapping ile cevaplanabilir.
- Uydurma sonuc veya arama sayisi icermez.
