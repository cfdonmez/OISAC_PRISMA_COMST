# 01 - Konu ve Soru Formu

Bu dosya O-ISAC PRISMA calismasinin baslangic kararlarini tutar. Workflow aciklamalari Turkce, manuscript-ready baslik ve research question metinleri English yazilir.

## 1. Basliklar

Gecici / ic calisma basligi:

- O-ISAC for 6G PRISMA narrative systematic review ve PCC-based scoping-style mapping calismasi.

Manuscript-ready proposed title (English):

> Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication for 6G: A PRISMA-Grounded Systematic Review and Metric-Governed Cross-Modality Survey

## 2. Konu Kisa Tanimi

Bu calisma, 6G odakli Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC) literaturunu fiber, FSO, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz ve hybrid optical systems kapsaminda sistematik olarak haritalar. Odak yalnizca yayinlari listelemek degil; optical modality, system architecture, sensing task, communication metric, sensing metric, validation method ve measurement plane farklarini ortak bir taxonomy ve metric-governed comparison mantigi ile sentezlemektir.

Operational definition (English):

> In this review, O-ISAC refers to optical or photonic systems in which sensing and communication functions are jointly considered, integrated, co-designed, co-optimized, or evaluated within the same architecture, optical link, waveform/resource framework, hardware platform, channel model, or application scenario.

Corpus distinction:

- Primary technical evidence corpus: peer-reviewed journal articles, early-access journal articles, and full-length conference/proceedings papers.
- Contextual corpus: review/survey papers and pre-2020 foundational studies used for background, terminology, taxonomy cross-checking, or technology lineage.
- Contextual records must not be counted as primary technical evidence.

## 3. Gerekce

O-ISAC literaturu farkli optik platformlar, farkli olcum duzlemleri ve farkli validation yaklasimlari uzerinden gelismektedir. Bu heterojenlik, klasik meta-analysis icin uygun bir nicel pooling zemini olusturmamaktadir; ancak PRISMA-grounded narrative systematic review, scoping-style PCC component ve evidence tabulation yaklasimi ile alanin taxonomy, metric reporting, validation maturity ve benchmark readiness acisindan daha izlenebilir bicimde degerlendirilmesini mumkun kilar.

Manuscript-ready rationale draft (English):

> Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC) is emerging as a candidate paradigm for 6G systems in which optical communication infrastructures also support sensing, localization, monitoring, or environmental perception. However, existing studies are distributed across fiber, free-space optical, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, and hybrid optical platforms, and they differ substantially in architecture, sensing task, communication metric, sensing metric, validation method, and measurement plane. A PRISMA-grounded narrative systematic review with a scoping-style PCC component is therefore needed to organize the field, compare metric reporting practices, synthesize rate-sensing tradeoffs, and identify validation and benchmarking gaps.

## 4. Review Tipi

Secilen review tipi:

- PRISMA-grounded narrative systematic review with a scoping-style PCC component.

Bu calisma PICO tabanli bir intervention-effect review degildir. Ana framework PCC - Population / Concept / Context olarak tutulur.

Meta-analysis yapilmayacaktir. Gerekce: O-ISAC literaturu optical modality, system architecture, sensing task, communication metric, sensing metric, validation method ve measurement plane acisindan heterojendir. Bu nedenle sentez structured narrative synthesis, scoping-style taxonomy mapping, evidence tabulation, metric-governed comparison, validation maturity mapping, benchmark readiness assessment ve research roadmap synthesis ile yurutulecektir.

- Rate-sensing tradeoff synthesis.
- Enabling technologies.

## 6. Comparator ve Karsilastirma Mantigi

Bu calismada klasik intervention comparator yoktur. Karsilastirma, bir mudahalenin standart bakima veya baska bir mudahaleye karsi etkisini olcmek yerine su eksenler uzerinden yapilacaktir:

- Optical modality: fiber, FSO, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, hybrid optical systems.
- System architecture ve integration level.
- Communication metrics: rate, capacity, throughput, BER, latency, OSNR, SNR, ESNR ve benzeri metrikler.
- Sensing metrics: range, resolution, accuracy, detection probability, localization error, CRB, FIM ve benzeri metrikler.
- Rate-sensing tradeoff reporting.
- Validation types: theoretical analysis, simulation, laboratory experiment, field test, prototype, dataset/benchmark-based evaluation.
- Reporting completeness ve reproducibility indicators.
- Validation maturity ve benchmark readiness.

## 9. Inclusion Criteria

Ilk dahil etme kriterleri:

- English peer-reviewed journal articles, early-access journal articles, and full-length conference/proceedings papers.
- Publication date within January 1, 2020 - June 30, 2026.
- Studies explicitly addressing Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication, optical ISAC, integrated sensing and communication in optical systems, or closely equivalent optical communication-sensing integration.
- Studies located in at least one target modality: fiber, FSO, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, or hybrid optical systems.
- Studies with direct, inferred, weak, or not applicable 6G relevance coding for optical communication-sensing systems, enabling technologies, architectures, metrics, validation, applications, or benchmarking.
- Studies reporting enough technical information to support taxonomy coding, metric extraction, validation maturity mapping, or benchmark readiness assessment.

Date eligibility rule:

- If database filters retrieve the full 2026 publication year, records published or made available after June 30, 2026 will be excluded during date eligibility screening.
- Records with unclear day/month information will be flagged as `date_uncertain`.

Dusuk methodological/reporting quality tek basina dislama nedeni olmayacaktir; kanit gucu TQAF-style technical quality assessment ile nitelendirilecektir.

## 10. Exclusion Criteria

Ilk dislama kriterleri:

- Non-English publications.
- Publications outside January 1, 2020 - June 30, 2026.
- Non-peer-reviewed items, editorials, opinion pieces, patents, theses, posters without full technical paper, slides, newsletters, marketing material, or unsupported web content.
- Studies about RF-only ISAC without an optical communication-sensing component.
- Studies about optical communication only, with no sensing/perception/localization/monitoring function.
- Studies about optical sensing only, with no communication-system integration or communication-relevant analysis.
- Studies where the optical element is only a peripheral measurement tool and not part of an integrated communication-sensing system.
- Duplicates or superseded versions when a more complete peer-reviewed version is available.
- Papers without sufficient accessible information for eligibility judgment after reasonable full-text checking.

Pre-2020 rule:

- The primary systematic corpus is limited to January 1, 2020 - June 30, 2026.
- Pre-2020 foundational studies may be cited for background, terminology, or technology lineage, but will be labeled separately and excluded from primary technical evidence synthesis unless explicitly justified.

## 11. Ana Research Question

Manuscript-ready main research question (English):

> How has Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC) been investigated across fiber, free-space optical, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, and hybrid optical platforms, and what do existing peer-reviewed studies reveal about cross-modality taxonomy, sensing and communication metric reporting, metric comparability, comparison admissibility, rate-sensing tradeoffs, enabling technologies, application domains, validation maturity, benchmarking readiness, and remaining research gaps for 6G-oriented O-ISAC systems?

## 12. RQ1-RQ7

RQ1 (English):

> What optical modalities, system architectures, integration levels, sensing tasks, and application domains define the current O-ISAC literature for 6G-oriented systems?

RQ2 (English):

> How consistently do O-ISAC studies report communication metrics and sensing metrics across fiber, FSO, VLC/LiFi, photonic-THz, and hybrid optical systems?

RQ3 (English):

> To what extent are reported O-ISAC metrics comparable across modalities, measurement planes, validation methods, and reporting conditions, and when is cross-study comparison admissible?

RQ4 (English):

> What rate-sensing tradeoffs are analyzed or experimentally demonstrated in O-ISAC studies, and how are these tradeoffs shaped by modality, architecture, waveform design, resource sharing, and measurement plane?

RQ5 (English):

> What enabling technologies, signal processing methods, channel models, hardware assumptions, and system design strategies are used to support integrated optical sensing and communication?

RQ6 (English):

> What is the validation maturity and benchmark readiness of the O-ISAC evidence base, including theoretical analysis, simulation, laboratory validation, field testing, prototyping, dataset availability, reproducibility support, and benchmark-based evaluation?

RQ7 (English):

> What benchmark readiness gaps, reporting limitations, reproducibility barriers, and research roadmap priorities remain for 6G-oriented O-ISAC systems?
